## Why this exists

AGENTS.md's hard rule: "Geometry that was not observed can never pass or fail a part. It may
only trigger a Rescan. A model's guess must never approve or condemn a component. This governs
the verdict logic, the provenance labelling, and how the heatmap is coloured. Do not weaken it
for convenience." Weakening it is explicitly on AGENTS.md's refusal list. The sprint plan's S4
bullet names this story's two concrete jobs: "label completed surface Inferred and exclude it
from the deviation maths; enforce the rule that unobserved geometry never passes or fails a
part." A-04 already deliberately deferred half of this here: "The sprint plan's S4 Inspection
bullet put 'label completed surface Inferred' in the inspection lane. Defining the label moves
here [A-04], where the surface is produced; excluding it from deviation stays with I-06/I-07." So
this story is what makes the hard rule structurally true across both axes — Unobserved surface
*and* Inferred surface — rather than trusting I-06/I-07 to remember it informally each time.

## Acceptance criteria

- A region with `provenance: unobserved` never has `contributes_to_verdict: true`, and can only
  ever be a factor in a `Rescan` outcome, never `Pass` or `Fail` — checked as a structural
  invariant on I-07's actual output, matching
  `contracts/examples/getVerdict.response-200-rescan-unobserved.json`'s shape
  (`provenance: unobserved`, `contributes_to_verdict: false`, `sealing_face_not_seen` in
  `reason_codes`).
- Inferred surface (A-04's shape-completion output) is excluded from the deviation maths
  entirely: it never gets a `_DEVIATION_MM`/`_DEVIATION_MEASURED` value from I-06, and never
  appears in a `Region` with `contributes_to_verdict: true` — per D-005/D-006 and the glossary's
  Provenance entry ("Surface a generative model produced rather than a camera measured is
  Inferred: it never contributes to a verdict").
- A test fixture with a deviation lying only in an Unobserved region produces `Rescan`, never
  `Fail` or `Pass` (the table's third row).
- Tests, written with the code: a synthetic mesh (I-01) with Unobserved patches, and separately
  one with Inferred-labelled vertices stapled in, each run through I-07's verdict function and
  asserted to never flip to Pass/Fail on the strength of that surface alone — the regression
  suite for the hard rule, in the same spirit as R-29's Inferred-aware hard-rule test at the
  reconstruction layer.

## Notes and decisions

- This story is the enforcement point, not the label definition (A-04 already defines Inferred)
  and not the per-vertex computation (I-06/I-07 already compute deviation and verdict) — it is
  the cross-cutting invariant check that ties the two together.
