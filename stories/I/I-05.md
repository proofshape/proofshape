## Why this exists

D-008: "on a near-symmetric part the feature that breaks the symmetry is often the
defect. A least-squares fit can silently choose the alignment that explains it away."
So "if the runner-up alignment residual is within 10% of the best, the session is marked
ambiguous" (D-061 later set 20%; see the notes), the verdict is Rescan, and the user is asked to place the part as the PO's
placement note says. The board's orientation is known, so placement resolves it.

## Acceptance criteria

- `inspection.registration.check_ambiguity(candidates, model, cad)` refines every I-03
  candidate with I-04, merges only poses whose transforms agree within a stated
  tolerance, and returns `orientation_ambiguous = True` when the runner-up `inlier_rmse`
  is within the threshold of the best, relative to the best. The threshold is a parameter
  whose default is D-061's 20%, which supersedes D-008's 10% (changed in this story's review,
  with both other members' agreement; see the notes).
- Poses that are equivalent under the CAD's symmetry are **kept separate**, never merged.
  For the printed part they are different poses, and the difference is where the defect
  would be. Merging them would make this check a no-op on exactly the parts it exists for.
- The result maps onto the contract's `VerdictResult.orientation_ambiguous` and reason code
  `registration_orientation_ambiguous` (`contracts/examples/`); the verdict itself is
  I-07's.
- **Tests, written with the code:** s-05 (30 × 30 × 60 mm, four-fold about z) is
  ambiguous, and still ambiguous with a patch removed; s-04 (a scalene triangle) is not.

### Implementation decisions (2026-11-06, @TabeenRaoof)

- **`check_ambiguity` returns an `AmbiguityCheck`:** `orientation_ambiguous`, the `best` pose
  (what I-06/I-07 measure against), the `runner_up`, `rmse_ratio`, the `threshold`, every
  `refined` pose ranked, the `failed` candidates with their reasons, and `reason_codes`, which is
  `("registration_orientation_ambiguous",)` or empty. The verdict itself stays I-07's.
- **Ranking: fitness first, then RMSE**, as I-03 ranks its candidates. Refined plausible poses
  all reach fitness about 1.000, so RMSE does the separating. The fitness-first order still
  matters at the margin: on one measured case the runner-up by fitness had the lower RMSE (ratio
  0.82), and ranking by RMSE alone would swap them and read 1.22, missing the ambiguity. No
  separate fitness guard: on every measured case it changed nothing, and it could only remove
  flags, which is the less safe direction.
- **Merging:** only poses whose transforms agree within I-03's tolerance (2°, and 1% of the
  CAD's extent with a 0.25 mm floor). Symmetric alternatives differ by 90° or 180° and are never
  merged. A test pins this against the realistic bug, merging poses that put the CAD surface in
  the same place.
- **Every candidate is refined with the same seed**, so all are scored on identical samples and
  their RMSEs compare like for like.
- **Failure is explicit:** a candidate that can't be refined (I-03's `ransac:0` is often an
  identity at fitness 0; 0–1 per run here) is listed in `failed` with its reason. If none can be
  refined, it raises. Missing provenance is refused up front, so it can't be mistaken for every
  candidate failing.
- **The threshold is 20%, D-061, proposed here and superseding D-008's 10%.** Measured on I-01
  output with I-01's 20% hidden patch, 12 seeds per class: s-05 is flagged 12/12 at either
  threshold; a plate whose one symmetry-breaking hole is **missing** (D-008's own case) is flagged
  9/12 at 10% and 12/12 at 20%; the same plate with the hole in place or shifted 3 mm, and s-04,
  are flagged 0/12 at either. The margins are narrow (1.15 against 1.22); D-061 records them and
  why the asymmetric costs favour 20%.
- **Refining every candidate also fixes a wrong I-03 pick:** on #251's shifted-hole seed, I-03's
  best was the plate's 180° mirror, 11 mm off. `check_ambiguity`'s best is 0.05 mm off.

### Notes carried from #233's review, closed here

- **Free-scale residual sanity bound:** `_free_scale_residual` now returns `None` beyond ±25%
  (`DEFAULT_SCALE_RESIDUAL_SANITY_BOUND`, a labelled assumption). With s-04 ×1.03 (true +0.03) and
  the bottom face plus half the part hidden, 5 of 24 seeds read +0.29 to +6.36, now `None`.
  *Two limits:* inside the bound, occlusion that heavy still misreads, often with the wrong sign,
  so this is not an occlusion gate; and an absurd reading now gives D-039's scale agreement no
  CAD vote, where before it would have read as a disagreement. A gate on Observed coverage
  belongs wherever scale agreement is wired to registration (I-07).
- **The s-05 basin test** now measures yaw relative to the true pose, not absolute board yaw.
- **`ransac:0`** is handled by the failure record above; I-03 still appends it.
- **The two mutations that survived I-03's tests** (dropping the rigid pre-pass; shrinking the
  dense CAD target to 700 points) now each fail two cases of the bottom-face test, parametrised
  with I-01's patch and seeds 3 and 4.
- I-03's inline 0.25 mm floor is now `DEFAULT_DISTINCT_TRANSLATION_FLOOR_MM`.
