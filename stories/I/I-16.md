## Why this exists

`inspect/inspection/registration.py` searches the CAD's turn about the board normal, plus x and
y. It assumes the part rests on the face its CAD rests on, and it can't turn the CAD over. When a
part lies on a different face, the search still returns its closest fit, and nothing flags it.

That has happened on a fixture. In the golden s-04 capture the block lay on its other triangular
face:
- the outline check on #427 found the reconstruction to be the mirror image of the registered
  CAD;
- I-15's placement reading of `s-04/IMG_4093.jpeg` (2026-11-24) shows the other hand from the
  CAD.

The pose comes back unflagged on all three of @TabeenRaoof's scratch variants, at coverage
ratios 1.048, 1.505 and 1.914 against D-061's 1.025 cutoff (#440). I-15 records golden s-04 as a
known limit, neither a pass nor a failure, because making it flag needs a check that doesn't
exist yet. This story is that check.

A part on the wrong face is exactly the "quiet wrong answer" the project rules forbid: the
deviation is then measured against a CAD the part doesn't match. One unverified phone session,
`sess_cdf9822969a4`, also appears to show s-04 on one of its long sides (#439). Nobody has
confirmed it, so it isn't evidence here.

## Acceptance criteria

- **A mismatched face is flagged, never served as a clean pose.** Before a pose is served, the
  model's own mid-height outline is compared with the posed CAD's for handedness: corner order
  around the outline, or the sign of a mirror-sensitive moment, with no fitting. On a mismatch
  the result is flagged or refused. **Which reason code it uses is settled with the team before
  any code is written.** The existing `registration_orientation_ambiguous` would keep the
  contract unchanged. A new code would change `contracts/openapi.yaml`.
- **A CAD with no handedness is never flagged by this check.** An outline that is its own mirror
  image under some turn (a box, a regular polygon) can't be on "the wrong face" as far as the
  outline shows. The check says so and passes it, rather than guessing.
- **Nothing that works today gets worse, on record.** Run on the ten calipered parts and the
  three saved s-04 phone sessions:
  - golden s-04 flags;
  - every capture whose reading shows its CAD's own face down doesn't flag (`g02_03` and the
    G-05 plates among them);
  - D-061's rule, cutoff and synthetic table are unchanged.

## Tests

Unit tests are written with the check, on I-01 synthetic samples:
- a CAD mirrored before it is posed flags;
- the same CAD posed normally doesn't flag;
- a box and a regular polygon never flag, whatever their turn;
- noise and an Unobserved patch on the slice don't flip the answer.

The real-capture runs are a written check, with commands and numbers in this file. Captures stay
outside git.

## Notes and decisions

- **Written up on 2026-11-24** (@dalwalyk), from I-15 (#440), where golden s-04 is recorded as a
  known limit pending this story.
- **What already exists.** `verify_outline.py` in @TabeenRaoof's scratch bundle
  (`runs/i15-scratch-meshes-2026-11-23/compare/how-it-was-made/`) reads handedness from the
  mid-height outline, by corner order for triangles and by the sign of Hu's seventh moment. I-15
  added `inspection.pose_judgement.outline_at`, which cuts that outline from a mesh. Neither is
  wired into the served path.
- **Out of scope:** searching the other faces, that is, turning the CAD over and fitting it. That
  would find the right pose instead of flagging the wrong one, but it is a larger change to the
  search and to D-061's candidate set. This story only refuses to serve a pose it can show is on
  the wrong face.
- **Reason code: reuse `registration_orientation_ambiguous`** (@mbj1994, 2026-11-25, review on
  #441). The existing code's contract semantics and resulting non-clean verdict already cover a
  demonstrably mismatched face, so no contract change to `contracts/openapi.yaml` is needed. If
  implementation shows the semantics don't fit, agree a distinct reason code before coding. Keep
  insufficient or unreliable outline evidence separate from a proven handedness mismatch.
- **After the Dec 3–4 code freeze,** as I-15 says. It adds a new capability rather than correcting
  shipped behaviour.
- **Estimate basis:** I-13 was estimated at 3 h for a change of similar size in the same file.
  This adds a real-capture record on ten parts and three phone sessions, so 4 h. It is an
  estimate, not a measurement.
- **Built (first criterion), 2026-11-25** (@dalwalyk). `pose_judgement.check_handedness` cuts a
  mid-height outline from both the posed CAD and the model using `outline_at`, computes Hu's
  seventh moment on each (sign-sensitive to reflection, invariant to translation/rotation/scale),
  and flags when the signs differ. The CAD's |h7| must exceed 1e-3 for the check to engage;
  below that, reconstruction noise and planted defects can flip the model's h7 sign on a
  correctly-posed part (G-05 defect-ladder regression, 2026-11-25: the control plate's notch
  gives |h7_cad| ~ 6e-4, well below the floor, so it is treated as having no handedness).
  The model's own floor is 1e-7 — much lower, because reconstruction always weakens the moment,
  and even a small but nonzero model h7 with opposite sign from a strongly chiral CAD is evidence
  of a face flip.  `check_ambiguity` in `registration.py` calls it after choosing the best
  pose; a mismatch sets `orientation_ambiguous = True`, reusing the existing
  `registration_orientation_ambiguous` reason code with no contract change.
  - **Tests** (`tests/test_handedness.py`, 15): Hu moment properties (nonzero for a triangle,
    sign flips on mirror, rotation- and translation-invariant, near-zero for square and hexagon);
    s-04 normal pose matches, s-04 mirrored flags, yaw doesn't flag, box and pentagon and s-05
    have no handedness, weakly chiral CAD treated as no handedness (G-05 regression), noise
    doesn't flip the answer, missing outline is inconclusive.
- **Real-capture record (second criterion), 2026-11-25** (@dalwalyk). Each run was loaded from
  `fixtures/data/recon_runs/` (calipered parts) or `proofshape-runs/r44-sessions-2026-11-23/`
  (phone sessions), registered with `global_register`, then checked with `check_handedness`.
  Commands: `PYTHONPATH=inspect .venv/bin/python run_handedness_record.py` (scratchpad script).
