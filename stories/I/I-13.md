## Why this exists

I-05's `check_ambiguity` (`inspect/inspection/registration.py`) **ranks** the refined poses
fitness first, then inlier RMSE. It then **decides** ambiguity on inlier RMSE alone: the
runner-up's RMSE within 20% of the best's (D-061). Inlier RMSE only counts matched points, so a
wrong pose that leaves part of the outline uncovered isn't penalised for it. Real captures show
that matched-point RMSE can mark clearly worse poses ambiguous, forcing Rescan:

| Session | Best | Runner-up | RMSE ratio | Fitness, best / runner-up |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `g05_00`, the G-05 control (#407) | yaw −0.9°, RMSE 1.375 mm | 178° away, 52 mm; RMSE 1.364 mm | 0.99 | 0.717 / 0.587 |
| `g05_02`, the 4 mm hole shift (#407) | yaw −179.4°, RMSE 1.421 mm | 41° away, 21 mm; RMSE 1.665 mm | 1.17 | 0.629 / 0.583 |
| real `s-04` (measured 2026-11-10 in #290's review) | fitness 0.985, RMSE 1.67 mm | 1.6° and 4.2 mm away; RMSE 1.32 mm | 0.79–0.83 | 0.985 / 0.962 |

- **`g05_00` and `g05_02` aren't symmetric.** The plate's corner notch fixes its orientation
  (G-05). Both poses are clearly worse by fitness.
- **`s-04`** is the near-duplicate case. The two poses are 1.6° apart but don't merge, because
  I-05 merges only within 2° *and* 1% of the CAD's extent (about 0.65 mm on `s-04`).
- **X-03 can't pass without this.** Its planted-shift scenario can't reach Fail while the control
  and the defect part both Rescan on ambiguity alone (#407).

## Acceptance criteria

- **The rule uses a coverage-aware residual,** in the form chosen from the fork below and
  recorded as an amendment to D-061. D-061 needed both other members' approval, so this does too.
  The margin is measured, not guessed (D-003), and the measurement is in the decision.
- **D-061's synthetic table is re-run under the new rule:** 12 seeds per class, on I-01's
  output. The cases that should flag still flag 12/12: `s-05` (truly four-fold symmetric) and
  D-008's plate with its hole **missing**. The cases that shouldn't still flag 0/12: the
  shifted hole, the hole in place, and scalene `s-04`. The margins on each side are reported, as
  D-061's were.
- **On real data,** using the saved sessions from #407 and the golden `s-04`/`s-05` at current
  `main`:
  - `g05_00` is not flagged.
  - `g05_02` is not checkable on the upside-down capture and needs a right-side-up recapture.
  - `s-05` still is.
  - `s-04` is not flagged. If no measured rule can clear `s-04` without breaking the synthetic
    table, that is recorded as the finding, with its numbers. A different threshold isn't picked
    quietly to make it pass.

  Each result is recorded with its coverage ratio and inlier-RMSE/fitness diagnostics.
- **Tests,** in `tests/test_registration.py`, beside I-05's own:
  - a runner-up that leaves CAD outline support unmatched is distinguished by the coverage score;
  - every existing `check_ambiguity` test still passes unchanged;
  - the new margin is pinned at its boundary.

## Notes and decisions

- **Choice (2026-11-19, @dalwalyk): option 2, the coverage-aware residual.** Candidate fitness
  ranking stays unchanged; ambiguity is measured on the union of CAD samples visible within the
  refinement radius under any candidate pose, against only fully Observed model geometry. Distances
  are capped at the refinement radius, so unmatched visible CAD support is penalized without
  letting hidden or Inferred model geometry affect the comparison.
- **The fork considered:**
  1. **Both must be close:** ambiguous only when the runner-up's RMSE is within 20% *and* its
     fitness is within a measured margin of the best's. This is the smallest change. Its risk is
     D-008's case: if a missing symmetry-breaker also lowers the runner-up's fitness, the case
     could stop flagging. Criterion 2 is there to catch that.
  2. **A coverage-aware residual:** score each pose on all of the CAD's visible points, with
     distances capped at the refinement radius, instead of inliers only. This stays one number
     with one threshold, but it changes what D-061's 20% was measured against.
  3. **For `s-04` only:** treat a runner-up within the 2° rotation tolerance as translation
     uncertainty, not orientation ambiguity. This fixes the near-duplicate but not the `g05`
     cases, so it is at most a complement to 1 or 2.
- **The cost asymmetry still holds (D-061):** a missed ambiguity can choose the pose that
  explains a defect away, and a false flag costs one Rescan. A rule that clears `g05` by
  missing D-008's case is worse than the bug.
- **Freeze:** this fixes shipped behaviour that X-03 depends on. It isn't a new feature, the
  same footing as R-41 and R-42. It needs merging before the Dec 3–4 code freeze.
- **Fold check (P-35, 2026-11-19):**
  - I-05 is Done. Its notes explain why it had no fitness guard: on the synthetic cases, refined
    poses "all reach fitness about 1.000, so RMSE does the separating", and a guard "changed
    nothing" and "could only remove flags, which is the less safe direction". On the real
    captures above, fitness is 0.58–0.99 and does separate the poses. That is the new evidence,
    and criterion 2 keeps I-05's safety point.
  - X-03's I-05 criterion only checks the risk; it doesn't fix it.
  - No origin branch changes `inspect/inspection/registration.py` relative to `main`, and no
    open PR touches it. Checked 2026-11-19 with `git diff origin/main...<branch>` for every
    branch, and `gh pr list` file lists.

  So it's a new story, not a criterion added to a Done one.
- **Estimate basis:** I-05 itself was 3 h, including D-061's measurement. This is the same
  measurement re-run, plus one rule change.

### Option 2 coverage measurement (2026-11-19)

For each refined candidate, the prototype transforms 20,000 CAD surface samples and measures
nearest distances to the fully Observed model surface, capped at I-04's correspondence radius.
The comparison support is the union of CAD samples within that radius under any candidate, so
hidden CAD surfaces do not contribute when no pose observes them. On the 12-seed D-061 synthetic
table at σ = 0.3 mm and 20% hidden patch, the runner-up/best coverage ratios were:

| Class | Coverage ratio range | Result at 1.025 |
|---|---:|---|
| s-05, four-fold symmetric | 0.989575–1.009892 | 12/12 ambiguous |
| Plate with missing hole | 0.977358–1.018260 | 12/12 ambiguous |
| Hole shifted 3 mm | 1.026777–1.067701 | 0/12 ambiguous |
| Hole in place | 1.045631–1.118173 | 0/12 ambiguous |
| s-04, scalene | 1.521174–2.019793 | 0/12 ambiguous |

The first 12 seeds suggested **1.0225**. The extended run below supports the team's choice of
**1.025**, with more false Rescans in exchange for catching the missed missing-hole case. The existing G-05
missing-notch case has a coverage ratio of **0.999362**, so it remains ambiguous despite its
fitness gap of 0.003125. The two #407 session directories are still needed.
