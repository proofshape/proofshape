## Why this exists

C-14 (heatmap viewer, Done) and C-15 (report page, Done) are both built against a mock heatmap
and have "nothing real to render" until this story lands — their own notes say so. D-062 already
settled *how* the number must travel, so this story is "written against it," not a fresh design:
the served GLB gains two application-specific vertex attributes rather than baking colours
server-side, so the viewer keeps choosing the range, the legend and the provenance toggle. The
computation itself is proposal §7.2: "Signed point-to-mesh distance from each Observed vertex to
the CAD." I-04's pose is the one to measure against — its own notes already fix this: "Only fully
Observed geometry drives the fit... This pose is the one I-06/I-07 measure deviations against."

## Acceptance criteria

- For every vertex **labelled Observed** (COLOR_0) in the served mesh, computes the signed
  point-to-mesh distance to the CAD in millimetres, positive where the part has extra material,
  negative where material is missing (glossary; proposal §7.2), using I-05's selected `best` pose.
  A vertex I-06 doesn't compare (outside the registered part, or not Observed) is left
  unmeasured, matching D-062's "I-06 measures Observed vertices only." **Outside the registered
  part** means: below the board-vs-part cut the model's own fusion run already resolved
  (`BoardSurface.height_threshold_mm`), or outside the posed CAD's own xy footprint plus a
  declared margin. **What this actually excludes on real data, re-reviewed on #309's second
  round rather than assumed from the logic alone:** on real golden s-04/s-05 (the two with CAD),
  neither gate excluded anything at all — 1,602/1,602 Observed vertices measured on s-04,
  2,247/2,247 on s-05. The height gate excludes essentially nothing on a real served mesh
  because fusion's own cut already removed that surface before I-06 ever sees it, so its real
  job is defending against a degenerate input, not filtering real board skin. The footprint
  margin is what does real work, but only beyond the margin: 31% of s-04's measured vertices and
  15% of s-05's sit outside the posed CAD's own hull but within the margin, mostly base flare —
  the margin let them through rather than excluding them, which is the right call for that
  geometry, but it means board skin sitting within the same margin of the hull (#292's s-02
  case, not one of the two objects measured here) would also still be measured today. That's a
  declared, recorded gap, not something either gate actually closes — #292's or I-07's call, not
  re-decided here.
- Writes two new float32 vertex attributes via `recon.mesh_cleanup.write_glb`'s existing optional
  extra-attributes parameter (D-062, already built for C-14's mock fixture): `_DEVIATION_MM` (one
  value per vertex, mesh's own vertex order, vertices not moved) and `_DEVIATION_MEASURED` (1.0
  exactly where a deviation was computed, 0.0 elsewhere; `_DEVIATION_MM` is 0.0 wherever
  `_DEVIATION_MEASURED` is 0.0). Neither may hold NaN (glTF forbids it in float accessors).
  COLOR_0 is untouched.
- Writes the new GLB beside the old one and flips the session record to it in one atomic write,
  the same pattern `SessionStore` already uses (`inspection.deviation.publish_deviation_glb`,
  review on #309), so `glb_url` never changes and no viewer reads a half-written file. A GLB
  without these attributes stays valid (every session with no CAD, and every session before its
  verdict, per D-062's "When it appears"). Not called from the real pipeline yet: no session has
  a per-order CAD to measure against until R-34 exists, so `recon/service.py` isn't touched here.
- Tests, written with the code: a round trip through `read_glb`/a real `GLTFLoader.parse` recovers
  both attributes with no NaN; a vertex that is Unobserved, Inferred, below the board cut, or
  outside the registered footprint never gets `_DEVIATION_MEASURED = 1`; the sign convention is
  checked against a synthetic part (I-01's generator) with a known planted offset; the footprint
  margin is checked against a defect at G-05's own largest size (8 mm), so it isn't accidentally
  excluded; the CAD must be watertight (Open3D's inside/outside decision is only meaningful for a
  closed mesh); `publish_deviation_glb`'s atomic flip is checked against a real `SessionStore`.

## Notes and decisions

- D-062 is the governing decision for this story's whole shape; do not re-derive the attribute
  layout, only implement it.
- `_DEVIATION_MM`/`_DEVIATION_MEASURED` naming, and the leading underscore, are glTF's own
  convention for application-specific attributes — not this story's choice.
- This story is Ready as a library function (the computation, the GLB writer, and the session
  flip against a real `SessionStore`), but no session has a CAD to measure against until R-34
  exists, so the real pipeline hook into `recon/service.py` can't land before then — caught on
  review (@TabeenRaoof, #302).
- **This is a D-037 exception, not a compliant claim, and says so plainly (as P-31 does) rather
  than leaving it unstated.** The claim and the implementation landed bundled in the same pull
  request (#309), with no separate claim-only PR merged first — unlike the usual pattern this
  project otherwise follows (see e.g. R-33's `chore/claim-r-33` landing separately before any
  implementation commit). Recorded here as what actually happened. It caused a real collision:
  @mbj1994 opened a second claim for I-06 (#313) while this one sat unmerged and so looked
  unclaimed on `main` — exactly the D-037 risk the rule is there to prevent. #313 was closed in
  favour of this PR, which already carried the implementation.
- **Review on #309 (@TabeenRaoof), ran on the real golden meshes** (s-04 and s-05, the two with
  CAD; pose from `global_register` → `check_ambiguity` at defaults): the written GLB is correct
  (attributes present, 4-byte aligned, no NaN, vertex count matches). s-05's median deviation of
  about −0.9 mm is its already-known free-scale residual (−0.085, R-22/R-32) showing through, not
  an I-06 defect. **Best-versus-runner-up sensitivity, worth flagging for whoever next touches
  I-05's merge tolerance:** on s-04, I-05's `best` and the near-duplicate pose it doesn't merge
  (1.6° and 4.2 mm apart, recorded in R-33.md) move the share of vertices beyond ±3 mm from 16.7%
  to 12.0% depending on which one is picked. No tolerance claim is made here (D-003) — these are
  the first real numbers, not a measured accuracy figure.
- **Re-review on #309 (@TabeenRaoof), second round: the region-exclusion criterion's wording and
  the margin's stated rationale didn't match what the code does on real data.** Corrected in the
  acceptance criteria above, `inspection/deviation.py`'s docstrings and `inspect/README.md`,
  rather than left as a logic-only claim. The real baseline, for whoever next touches this (I-07
  or #292): on s-04/s-05, neither gate excluded anything (100% of Observed vertices measured on
  both); 31% of s-04's measured vertices and 15% of s-05's sit outside the posed CAD's own hull
  but within `DEFAULT_FOOTPRINT_MARGIN_MM`, with deviation medians of about +1.7 mm and +1.1 mm —
  mostly base flare, about half of it under 8 mm high. `DEFAULT_FOOTPRINT_MARGIN_MM` (10 mm) is
  a declared parameter, not derived from G-05's defects (which grow upward on the top face, not
  outward in xy, so can't justify an xy margin) or from any other measured source — real data
  shows it isn't obviously too tight for s-04's own base flare, but it isn't validated as the
  right number for anything in particular either.
