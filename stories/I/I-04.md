## Why this exists

The second half of the settled method (proposal §7.1, D-008): refine the global pose
with point-to-plane ICP under a Tukey loss, "so a missing patch or a shifted hole does
not drag the fit toward itself." A least-squares fit would explain the defect away by
shifting the whole part, which is exactly the thing inspection must not do.

## Acceptance criteria

- `inspection.registration.refine(candidate, model, cad)` runs Open3D `registration_icp`
  with `TransformationEstimationPointToPlane(TukeyLoss(k))` against the sampled CAD
  surface and its face normals, and returns the refined pose, `inlier_rmse` and
  `fitness`, plus the free-scale residual recomputed on the refined correspondences.
- **Tests, written with the code:** starting from I-03's best candidate on I-01 output,
  the pose error after refinement is below the error before it. With I-01's missing patch,
  and with a shifted hole the test builds into the model, the pose error stays below the
  defect's size: the loss does not let the defect drag the fit.

## Tests

Unit tests in `tests/test_registration.py`, written alongside the code (D-029).

## Notes and decisions

- The Tukey `k`, the convergence criteria and the correspondence distance are parameters
  with stated defaults.
- Face normals come from the sampled CAD surface. `recon.next_view.vertex_normals` on a
  6-vertex prism gives corner normals, which point-to-plane can't use.

### Implementation decisions (2026-11-05, @TabeenRaoof)

- **Only fully Observed geometry drives the fit** (agreed before coding). This pose is the one
  I-06/I-07 measure deviations against, so Unobserved or Inferred (A-04 completion) geometry
  must not move it (D-005). A model with no provenance labels, or no fully Observed face,
  raises an explicit `ValueError`; it isn't refined on whatever geometry happens to be there.
  This matches I-03's rule for the scale diagnostic.
- **Reading of "the free-scale residual recomputed on the refined correspondences"** (agreed
  before coding). `refine` re-runs I-03's two-stage similarity ICP (`_free_scale_residual`) from
  the refined pose, on the same Observed-model and dense-CAD samples. It deliberately does not
  solve one similarity on the refine ICP's final pairs: #233's review measured that frozen
  nearest-neighbour pairs underestimate scale (+3% read as about +1%). The residual is computed
  even for a candidate that arrived without one (I-03 only attaches it to the best), which is
  what I-05 needs when it refines every candidate.
- **ICP direction: Observed model → CAD.** This puts the CAD's triangle normals on the target,
  where point-to-plane needs them. It's also I-03's lesson: CAD samples over a hidden patch
  can't pull toward the hole.
- **Full rigid 6-DoF**, as `registration_icp` returns. It is not constrained to I-03's
  yaw-plus-xy board pose, so small real board-plane errors (R-23's measured offsets and tilts)
  can be absorbed.
- **Defaults, all extent-relative from the CAD and none a tolerance:** `k` = 1 voxel (3% of CAD
  extent), correspondence distance 2.5 voxels (I-03's), 100 iterations. On I-01 output (σ 0.3 mm,
  20% patch), `k` anywhere from 0.5 to 2 voxels refined s-04 and s-05 to the same 0.04–0.09 mm
  surface error, so 1 voxel is a middle choice, not a tuned optimum. The CAD's extent rather than
  the model's keeps floaters in a whole-scene reconstruction from inflating `k`.
- **Evidence the loss does its job:** with a 4 mm bump or dent over 10% of the surface, all of it
  Observed, Tukey's surface error stays at 0.03–0.08 mm. The same code with least squares is
  dragged to 1.1–1.6 mm, 16–49× worse. The test asserts both, so a later switch to a non-robust
  loss fails.
- **Failure is loud.** Fewer than three correspondences (a candidate nowhere near the model)
  raises instead of returning Open3D's `inlier_rmse = 0`, which I-05's comparison would read as
  a perfect fit.
- **Left for I-05, from #233's review:**
  - an occlusion sanity bound on the free-scale residual (one heavily occluded I-03 seed read
    +30.6);
  - the s-05 basin test counting absolute rather than relative yaw;
  - `ransac:0` being appended even when it's an identity at fitness 0.

  `refine` doesn't change any of these. I-05 is where every candidate gets refined and compared.
