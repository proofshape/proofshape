## Why this exists

On 2026-11-24, P-41's committed script was re-run on the ten calipered parts with `main` at
`70b2773` (I-15 merged, R-44 off by default). It was compared with P-41's own committed-script
run at `89d0de4`. Every part's reconstruction was byte-identical, so every change below comes
from registration and measurement, not from the model. The run reproduces I-15's criterion-4
record for the `base` variant exactly.

Two changes have no explanation.

**`g05_02`'s walls read worse with the same pose.** It's the 4 mm hole-shift part, against its
own CAD.

| | Before I-15 | After I-15 |
|---|---|---|
| Wall readings, 95% within | 3.02 mm | 3.44 mm |
| Top face, 95% within | 1.00 mm | 0.98 mm |
| Worst reading | 5.01 mm | 5.01 mm |
| Coverage ratio (D-061) | 1.241 | 1.130 |
| Pose gap to the other run | | 0.65 mm: the same pose, by `poses_are_same` |

No other part's wall figure got worse by more than 0.05 mm. Two improved: `g05_00`
(3.46 → 3.00 mm) and s-05 (3.93 → 3.77 mm).

**s-05's pose is off its walls, and I-15 moved it further.** I-15's record has `main`'s s-05
pose "wrong" before and after:
- 11.5° from the photograph's reading on both;
- 9.3° from its walls before, and 13.6° after;
- 5.50 mm between the two poses.

The same gap was measured on this run. I-15 says outright that it doesn't fix s-05. The `R`
variant lands s-05 right (1.1° from its walls), so the part can be registered. `main` just
doesn't.

s-05 is a golden part, and a pose 9–14° off the walls is measured against the wrong place on
every wall. A wall reading that moves 0.4 mm with the same pose has a cause nobody has named.
Both matter before the expo and the final report.

### g05_02: the support cut is the cause

**Run-to-run variation is zero.** Ten runs of `i17_measure_variation.py` on the `base` model
with `OMP_NUM_THREADS=1` gave identical results every time: wall |95%| = 3.439 mm, top |95%| =
0.977 mm, yaw = -0.34°. The 0.42 mm change is not within Open3D's nondeterminism — it is
systematic.

**Disabling the support cut restores the old figure.** With `POSE_SUPPORT_MIN_HEIGHT_CAP_MM`
set to 0 (no cut), the wall reading is **3.019 mm** (matches the 3.02 before I-15). With the
current 5 mm cut, it is **3.439 mm**. The top face improves slightly (1.001 → 0.977 mm).

Command (from repo root, on the `base` model):
```
PYTHONPATH=.:inspect OMP_NUM_THREADS=1 .venv/bin/python scripts/i17_measure_variation.py \
  --model proofshape-data/handoff/i15-scratch-meshes-2026-11-23/g05_02/variants/base/uncertainty/mesh_uncertainty.glb \
  --cad fixtures/reference_cad/g05_02_hole_shift_4mm.stl \
  --board-json proofshape-data/handoff/i15-scratch-meshes-2026-11-23/g05_02/variants/base/fusion/tsdf_fusion.json \
  --runs 10 --out <output>.json
```
For the cut = 0 run, patch `registration.py` line 205: `POSE_SUPPORT_MIN_HEIGHT_CAP_MM = 0.0`,
then re-run the same command. No runs at `89d0de4` were needed: cut = 0 reproduces the
before-I-15 figure exactly.

| | Cut = 0 (before I-15) | Cut = 5 mm (I-15) |
|---|---|---|
| Wall |95%| | 3.019 mm | 3.439 mm |
| Top |95%| | 1.001 mm | 0.977 mm |
| Yaw | -0.25° | -0.34° |
| Fitness | 0.733 | 0.978 |
| Pose gap | — | 0.642 mm |

**Mechanism:** g05_02 is 20 mm tall, so the 5 mm support cut removes the bottom quarter —
48.6% of the Observed faces (1 659 of 3 416). ICP converges to a slightly different pose on the
remaining upper surface (0.64 mm gap, within `poses_are_same`'s 2 mm threshold). The wall
reading shifts because a 0.64 mm pose change moves the measurement surface relative to the CAD
walls. The top face is unaffected because it is flat and large.

**Not a bug.** The support cut exists to prevent board skin from corrupting the pose (I-15's
motivating case: g05_00 R turned 179° by stray surface). On g05_02 it trades 0.42 mm of wall
accuracy for board-skin resistance. No other part's wall figure got worse by more than 0.05 mm.

**No fix proposed.** Lowering the cut would re-expose the board-skin vulnerability that I-15
fixed. g05_02 first Passes at 5.5 mm on both commits, and the verdict is the same at every
swept tolerance from 0.5 to 12 mm.
Recorded as a known limit; see #461. P-38 should state this limit.

### s-05: refine's Tukey cost prefers the wrong pose on the base model

**The right pose is lost at refinement on both commits.** `trace_pose_search.py` on s-05
`base`, at the current commit and at `89d0de4` (before I-15), shows the same pattern: a right
candidate enters selection, but all 8 non-RANSAC candidates refine to the same wrong pose.

Selected pose yaw error: 11.5° before I-15, 11.5° after.
Selected pose wall error: 9.3° before I-15, 13.6° after.

I-15's support cut removed only 7.9% of Observed faces on s-05 (326 of 4 120) — the part is
60 mm tall, so 5 mm is a small fraction. The cut shifts the wall error from 9.3° to 13.6°
but does not change the outcome: the pose was already wrong.

Command:
```
PYTHONPATH=.:inspect OMP_NUM_THREADS=1 .venv/bin/python scripts/trace_pose_search.py \
  --model proofshape-data/handoff/i15-scratch-meshes-2026-11-23/s-05/variants/base/uncertainty/mesh_uncertainty.glb \
  --cad fixtures/reference_cad/s05_rect_prism_30x30x60mm.stl \
  --faces far --turned 0 --clockwise --squares-from-left 3.5 --squares-from-caption 3.0 \
  --face not-judged --side-normal 0 1 --symmetry 90 --straight-walls \
  --out <output>.json
```

**The R variant lands right from every seed.** On s-05 `R` (R-44's correction on), all 8
refined candidates converge to the right pose: 0.9° yaw error, 1.1° wall error, fitness 1.0.

**Refine's Tukey cost itself selects the wrong pose.** The Tukey cost was evaluated on s-05
`base`'s Observed support surface (3 794 faces, 1 600 sampled points) at both poses, without
running ICP — just measuring the objective:

| `base` support surface vs CAD | at `base`'s (wrong) pose | at `R`'s (right) pose |
|---|---|---|
| Tukey total cost | 631.3 | 715.6 |
| Tukey inliers (≤ k = 1.8 mm) | 872 / 1 597 | 945 / 1 598 |
| Within 0.5 mm | 12.4% | 7.6% |
| Within 1 mm | 36.5% | 20.5% |
| Within 2 mm | 58.9% | 74.1% |
| Median / 95th | 1.56 / 3.74 mm | 1.66 / 3.31 mm |

The wrong pose has fewer inliers (872 vs 945) but more of them very close (36.5% vs 20.5%
within 1 mm), and Tukey's cost rewards those more than the right pose's extra inliers between
1 and 1.8 mm, giving a lower total cost (631 vs 716). The right pose fits better by 95th
percentile (3.31 vs 3.74 mm) and share within 2 mm (74.1% vs 58.9%), but with Tukey
k = 1.8 mm, residuals beyond k get zero weight, so the cost prefers the wrong pose.

**This is a pre-existing limit, not a regression from I-15.** I-15's record already states:
"main's s-05 pose is not fixed by this story." R-44's correction fixes it because R-44's
re-anchoring improves s-05's wall surfaces enough that the right pose also wins the Tukey cost.

**A registration fix was not tried.** A wider Tukey k or a distance-based ranking might fix
s-05 without R-44, but neither has been tested against D-061's 100-seed classes and the
ten-part record. The Dec 4 code freeze is the reason this is recorded as a known limit rather
than investigated further. Recorded as a known limit; see #461. P-38 should state this limit.
