## Why this exists

I-06 measures every Observed vertex inside the posed CAD's xy footprint plus a 10 mm margin
(`DEFAULT_FOOTPRINT_MARGIN_MM` in `inspect/inspection/deviation.py`). That margin is declared,
not measured. Its own comment says it "isn't validated to be the right number for anything in
particular". The reconstructed surface flares outward at the part's base, and that flare sits
inside the margin, so it is measured as part. It then fails parts that are good:

| Part | Measured vertices outside the CAD's outline, inside the margin | Their median deviation | Effect on the verdict |
|---|---|---|---|
| `s-04` (I-06's notes) | 31% | +1.7 mm | Fails at every tolerance from 3.69 to 15 mm, from 40–141 vertices in this ring (I-07's notes) |
| `s-05` (I-06's notes) | 15% | +1.1 mm | — |
| `g05_00`, the G-05 control (#407) | 39% (524 of 1,331) | +2.1 mm (inside the outline: −0.2 mm) | Fails at 12.85 mm at every spec once ambiguity is set aside. 124 of its 167 vertices above 4.2 mm are in this ring, up to 9.1 mm outside the outline |
| `g05_02`, the 4 mm hole shift (#407) | 46% (682 of 1,486) | +4.1 mm | 340 of 446 vertices above 4.2 mm are in this ring, so a Fail can't be attributed to the hole |

The `g05` figures use the CAD's xy bounding box as the outline. The plate's corner notch lies
inside that box, so these counts understate the ring slightly.

I-07's notes already name this, "#292, or a rule excluding the band near the board", as "a real
prerequisite for X-03". #292 itself is board skin on `s-02`, in the clearance band at the board.
This is a separate effect. It sits outside the outline and higher up: on `g05_00`, the vertices
above 4.2 mm sit at a median 5.4 mm above the board, and every measured vertex is above that
session's 2.75 mm board cut.

## Acceptance criteria

- **The cause is recorded with numbers,** on `g05_00`, `g05_02`, `s-04` and `s-05` at current
  `main`. For the measured vertices outside the outline, record:
  - their share, deviation and height above the board;
  - their provenance and number of contributing frames;
  - a comparison with G-05's calipered print (`fixtures/caliper_g-05.json`), to confirm the print
    itself has no flare of that size.

  It should be clear whether this is reconstruction smear, board surface, or the real part.
- **A fix is chosen from the fork below and recorded as a decision** (a new D-number), because
  it changes what I-06 counts as "within the registered part". On the same saved sessions, with
  ambiguity set aside and X-03's spec, the controls (`g05_00`, `s-04`) no longer Fail because of
  vertices outside the CAD's outline. Whatever verdict remains, and what drives it, is recorded.
- **The planted defect stays measured, and nothing is hidden silently:**
  - `g05_02`'s top-face deviations are unchanged by the fix. #407 has them: p95 6.69 mm, against
    2.00 mm on the control.
  - A vertex the fix excludes is served with `_DEVIATION_MEASURED = 0` (D-062), so the heatmap
    greys it like any unmeasured surface.
  - The excluded band is named in `inspect/README.md` and the manual as not checked, the same
    standing as D-080's unmeasured geometry. It is not counted as Pass evidence (D-005).
- **Tests,** in `tests/test_deviation.py` (and `tests/test_verdict.py` if the verdict changes),
  on I-01's synthetic part:
  - a planted outward flare outside the outline is not measured;
  - a defect inside the outline is still measured;
  - G-05's largest defects (8 mm) are still measured;
  - the boundary of the new rule is pinned.

## Notes and decisions

- **The fork, to settle at claim or on review, not here:**
  1. **A measured margin:** replace the declared 10 mm with a margin measured from real parts, as
     D-003 asks, so the base flare falls outside it. This is the smallest change. Its cost: real
     excess material beyond the outline (flash, an oversized part) also goes unmeasured, leaving
     it to the size and scale checks (R-16, R-42).
  2. **A band near the board:** stop measuring vertices that are both outside the outline and
     below a measured height. This is narrower than 1, but it is a second declared shape.
  3. **Fix it in reconstruction:** stop the flare forming in fusion or segmentation. This is the
     real fix, but it is the largest. It would also change R-33 and R-35's measured surfaces, so
     it probably doesn't fit before the Dec 3–4 code freeze.
- **The hard rule:** excluding vertices can only remove evidence, never add it. That is
  safe in the direction D-005 cares about, but only if the excluded band is reported as not
  checked, never as passed. Criterion 3 holds the fix to that.
- **#292 stays open.** It is `s-02`'s board skin in the clearance band, a different place, and
  P-34 recorded it as a known limitation. This story may close part of it in passing; if so,
  record that on #292, with no closing keyword in a commit (#231 has reopened twice from one).
- **Freeze:** this fixes shipped behaviour that X-03 depends on. It isn't a new feature. It needs
  merging before the Dec 3–4 code freeze.
- **Fold check (P-35, 2026-11-19):**
  - I-06 and I-07 are Done, and both name this gap as someone else's call: "#292's or I-07's call,
    not re-decided here", and I-07's "real prerequisite for X-03".
  - #292 has no story (P-34).
  - No origin branch changes `inspect/inspection/deviation.py` or `verdict.py` relative to
    `main`, and no open PR touches them. Checked with `git diff origin/main...<branch>` for every
    branch, and `gh pr list` file lists.

  So it's a new story.
- **Estimate basis:** criterion 1 reuses #407's scripts (registration re-run, deviation split by
  outline and height). The fix is one gate in `compute_deviation` plus its tests. Fork option 3
  would not fit in 4 h.
