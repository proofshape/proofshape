## Acceptance criteria

- Computes the similarity transform aligning reconstruction cameras to board poses.
- Output geometry is in millimetres in the board frame.
- Alignment residual reported per frame.
- An object of known size measures correctly within a stated tolerance.

## Why this matters

The bottleneck of the phase. Everything after this needs it.

2026-09-27: Implemented the similarity alignment in `recon/metric_alignment.py`. R-01 and R-02
camera-from-frame extrinsics are converted to camera centres and paired by frame name. The
Umeyama solve maps the arbitrary reconstruction frame to the ChArUco board frame in millimetres,
and `metric_alignment.json` records the transform plus per-frame residuals. No object-accuracy
tolerance is claimed without an independent known-size measurement.

2026-09-27: Lightning run on s-04 used all 30 R-01 frames and 29 calibrated-K R-02 board poses.
The fitted camera-centre alignment RMS was 10.015 mm. On the same 29 frames, EXIF-K RMS was
9.864 mm; the two largest calibrated-K residuals were IMG_4092.jpeg (37.591 mm) and
IMG_4093.jpeg (27.383 mm). A diagnostic fit excluding those two frames had 4.542 mm RMS, but
both frames remain included in the reported alignment; no exclusion is justified by this
diagnostic alone. Median aligned camera-orientation difference was 0.50 degrees. These are
alignment diagnostics, not object-accuracy or tolerance measurements.

The independent known-size check is pending. @TabeenRaoof was asked on PR #35 to take repeated
caliper measurements of an identifiable s-04 feature; record the feature, instrument and
readings before comparing it to the aligned reconstruction. No tolerance has been established.
