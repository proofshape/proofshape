## Acceptance criteria

- At least three rigid objects, **one with a glossy or reflective surface**.
- Each shot on a printed ChArUco board, 25–30 frames, from a phone.
- At least one object shot under two different lighting conditions.
- Stored outside git behind a download script; the script and a checksum are committed, the images are not.
- A reference CAD model committed for at least one object.

## Why this matters

This is the single most important story in Phase 1. Because nobody is building the capture app yet, this set is the *only* source of real photographs for the entire backend push. Shooting it badly poisons two months of work.
