## Acceptance criteria

- At least three rigid objects, **one with a glossy or reflective surface**.
- Each shot on a printed ChArUco board, 25–30 frames, from a phone.
- At least one object shot under two different lighting conditions.
- Stored outside git behind a download script; the script and a checksum are committed, the images are not.
- A reference CAD model committed for at least one object.

## Why this matters

This is the single most important story in Phase 1. Because nobody is building the capture app yet, this set is the *only* source of real photographs for the entire backend push. Shooting it badly poisons two months of work.

## Notes and decisions

2026-09-20: Golden capture evidence verified in the team's shared Google Drive. There are five
capture sets representing four physical shapes; `s-01` and `s-02` are the same reflective
metal padlock captured under visibly different lighting/background conditions. Frame counts are:
`s-01` 26, `s-02` 30, `s-03` 27, `s-04` 30, and `s-05` 29. Representative frames show
the printed ChArUco board under every object.

The remaining three shapes are a rounded rigid part (`s-03`), yellow triangular prism
(`s-04`), and red rectangular prism (`s-05`). The shared Drive also contains STL reference
models for `s-04` and `s-05`; the `s-04` triangular-prism STL is committed to this branch as
the required in-repo reference CAD.

The photograph bytes remain outside git. `fixtures/golden_capture_manifest.json` records the
Drive folder IDs and expected inventory; `fixtures/golden_capture_manifest.sha256` locks that
inventory; and `fixtures/download_golden_capture.sh` downloads the five sets, validates the
expected JPEG counts, and generates local per-image SHA-256 hashes after download.

This exceeds the minimum capture requirement: four shapes instead of three, five capture sets,
one reflective object, and one object repeated under two lighting conditions.
