## Why this exists

R-36's home pre-check measured real iPhone scans of s-04 with `recon/cad_size_check.py`. On
`sess_2e59418b1077` and `sess_8bf24fbc0f29`, the top correctly sits at about 30 mm, but
`measure_prism` fails with "No candidate points fall in the expected top-face height range"
(`cad_size_check.py:793`/`800`). It fails even with `--min-confidence 1.0001` (D-045). The
largest connected blob among the candidates (`_largest_connected_blob`, `cad_size_check.py:438`)
is a low pile of stray points, not the part (#356).

The workaround confirms that diagnosis. Fit the board plane on the full cloud, and pass
`measure_prism` only the points more than 3 mm above it. `8bf2` then measures height 0.958,
residual 0.68 mm, and edges 1.071 / 0.985 / 1.008. A run that already measured isn't changed by
the filter.

This tool is how the team measures accuracy: G-07, G-10, X-03, and R-36's accuracy checks. D-073
checked G-04's six runs and found them unaffected. Those were 5712×4284 stills, 25–30 per part.
Phone captures are 480×640 at about 10–16 frames, and they hit this failure.

## Acceptance criteria

- **The two failing real clouds measure without a hand filter.** On `sess_2e59418b1077` and
  `sess_8bf24fbc0f29` (outside git), the tool measures the part. Height and fit residual come out
  close to the workaround's figures above. Edges are whatever R-37's new edge measure gives.
- **No regression.** Every capture that measures today gives the same result: golden s-01 to s-05
  and G-04's six specimens. The comparison is recorded.
- **A committed synthetic reproduction.** A part's top band, plus a larger stray blob lower in
  the candidate height range. The current selection picks the stray blob; the fixed one picks the
  part.

## Tests

The synthetic reproduction in the third criterion, in `tests/test_cad_size_check.py`, is written
before the fix and fails on the current selection. The real clouds can't be committed, because
they come from photographs. They're the recorded check in the first two criteria.

## Notes and decisions

- **Why it depends on R-37:** R-37 (@mbj1994, PR #364) rewrites `measure_prism`'s edge measurement
  and pose search in the same file. Starting this before #364 merges would conflict with it. If
  R-37's owner would rather absorb this, the team can fold R-39 into R-37 instead, and this file is
  marked as folded.
- **Fix directions from #356, for the owner to choose:** choose the blob by height (for example,
  the one containing the densest top band), or raise the candidate region's lower cut. Whichever
  it is, it goes in the code comments with the failing case.
- **R-39 implementation/validation (PR #378, head `bc69582`):** the selector preserves the
  historical largest component whenever it already contains a supported top-face peak, and only
  falls back to another height-supported connected component when the historical choice would
  fail. The committed synthetic regressions cover both the #356 recovery case and the
  no-regression case where a smaller higher stray cluster must not displace an already-good part.
- **Two original #356 phone clouds now measure with no hand filter (M2 Max):**
  - `sess_2e59418b1077` (16 frames): height ratio **0.9907** (**29.72 mm**), residual
    **0.65 mm**, rotation **-10.9 deg**, edge ratios **1.012 / 1.075 / 1.075**.
  - `sess_8bf24fbc0f29` (10 frames): height ratio **0.9581** (**28.74 mm**), residual
    **1.05 mm**, rotation **-14.4 deg**, edge ratios **1.001 / 1.088 / 1.094**.
  The recovered heights match the >3 mm workaround to four decimals; edge ratios differ by at
  most 0.015. Residual/rotation move both directions on these sparse phone clouds and are treated
  as R-37 pose stability, not a blob-selection regression.
- **No regression on real data:** @dalwalyk compared this branch with current `main` across
  G-04's six specimens plus golden s-01..s-05; all 11 outputs were byte-identical apart from path
  strings. @TabeenRaoof compared all local R-36 phone sessions that already measured; their
  reports were likewise identical.
- **Bonus recovery:** `sess_d258ee924694`, a hexagon cloud that hit the same #356 failure, now
  measures against the correct g02_05 CAD (height ratio **0.965**, residual **2.12 mm**) while
  `sess_8e1545f6cb19`, which already measured, stays identical.
