## Why this exists

R-17 compared the two golden objects that have CAD with their reconstructions:

| Object | Height vs CAD | Cross-section best-fit scale |
|---|---|---|
| s-04 triangular prism | 28.2 vs 30 mm (−5.8%) | 0.88 of nominal size |
| s-05 rectangular prism | 57.2 vs 60 mm (−4.6%) | 0.83 of nominal size |

The parts shrink far more horizontally than vertically. R-04 is a similarity transform with one
scale factor, so it can't be the whole cause. R-16 will measure accuracy once calipers exist (G-03),
but nothing owns finding the cause of a bias this large.

## Acceptance criteria

- A repeatable measurement of reconstructed against CAD dimensions for s-04 and s-05 is committed
  with tests, so the numbers can be rerun after any fix. R-17 used a one-off script; this replaces
  it.
- The stage or stages responsible are identified, with evidence. Leads from R-17 are in the notes.
- Findings are recorded. A fix that is more than small becomes its own story. No accuracy or
  tolerance claim is made here (D-003); that belongs to R-16.

## Tests

Synthetic point clouds of known size (a box, a triangular prism) with added noise, rotation and
offset. The measurement must recover the dimensions, and must fail when the synthetic part is
scaled. The investigation itself is evidence recorded in the notes.

## Notes and decisions

2026-10-09 (when written up, from R-17). Leads:

1. **VGGT's focal length is 3–5% longer than R-03's board calibration** on s-04 and s-05 (1.03–1.05×
   in x and y). A focal length that is too long narrows lateral extents. That is the right
   direction, but too small to explain 12–17% on its own.
2. **The board is an in-scene ruler.** The reconstructed ChArUco squares should measure 20 mm (D-034).
   Measuring them separates R-04's overall scale from errors in the object's geometry.
3. **R-02's board poses use EXIF intrinsics**, about 1.5% off R-03's calibration. Those poses are
   R-04's metric target, so they feed straight into the scale.
4. **The board plane sits 1.6–5.4 mm below z = 0 after R-04 on all five objects** (R-21). This may
   share a cause with the shrinkage.

Caveats on R-17's numbers:
- They are measured on the raw point cloud before fusion.
- They are compared with the CAD's nominal sizes, not caliper readings of the printed parts. FDM
  drifts by a few tenths of a millimetre (proposal §12). That is small next to these 1.8–7 mm gaps,
  but one caliper reading per part would remove the doubt.

2026-10-11: Investigated and closed. `recon/cad_size_check.py` (tests in
`tests/test_cad_size_check.py`, 29 synthetic cases — box and triangular-prism recovery, rotation/
offset/board-tilt, a deliberately-scaled part measuring smaller as required by the acceptance
criteria, stray-point rejection, and the board-ruler's own scale/focal-ratio mechanism) replaces
R-17's one-off script. `fixtures/reference_cad/s05_rect_prism_30x30x60mm.stl` is now committed too
(it was already listed in `golden_capture_manifest.json`'s `reference_cad` block, just not yet
added to git), so both CAD samples can be checked, not only s-04.

**Method.** Two independent measurements, both on the board-frame point cloud R-04 already writes,
no confidence filtering (R-20 isn't landed yet, and most pixels — including the board's — sit at
VGGT's confidence floor per R-17):
- `measure_board_scale`: every ChArUco corner R-02 already detected is lifted through R-01's own
  depth and camera, passed through R-04's transform, and fit against the board's true, known
  geometry with R-04's own Umeyama solve (`metric_alignment.solve_similarity`, reused directly).
  The board is an exact, independent ruler — if it reconstructs at the wrong size, the cause is
  upstream of the part (R-01/R-04), not segmentation or fusion. Also reports the ratio between the
  focal length R-02's board poses were actually solved with and VGGT's own predicted focal
  (rescaled to the same pixel frame), since R-04 fits camera centres and a camera's distance from
  the scene scales with the focal length assumed for it.
- `measure_prism`: an extruded-prism CAD part (STL read by `read_ascii_stl`/`prism_from_cad`, the
  only two shapes the golden set has) located in a mid-height band of the cloud. Height comes from
  the top-face point cluster; each footprint edge's orientation is found by matching the blob's own
  longest convex-hull edges against the CAD's edges (tried and rejected first:
  `cv2.minAreaRect` comparison, which can align a near-right triangle to its hypotenuse instead of
  its legs, unpredictably depending on point noise); each edge's length then comes from the
  *spread* of the points assigned to it, not from an assumed match to the CAD's length, so a
  genuinely smaller part measures smaller rather than being silently reported at full size.
  `build_report` divides every prism result by the board scale too
  (`*_ratio_over_board_scale`), isolating whatever error is specific to the part from whatever the
  board ruler already explains.

Three bugs surfaced and fixed while validating against real s-04/s-05 data (each is why
`_fit_rigid_2d`'s docstring reads the way it does): a margin excluding points near an assumed
corner, sized as a fraction of the CAD's edge length, silently capped how short a shrunk edge could
ever measure, independent of the real data; a "two edges equidistant" corner-ambiguity test
over-excluded a scalene triangle's acute-angled edges, where a third edge's extended line passes
close to points nowhere near the real corner; and an unweighted per-point least-squares translation
refinement let a long, densely-sampled edge outvote a short or more-shrunk one, pulling the whole
fit toward the big edge at the small edge's expense. All three were only visible on real data, not
the original synthetic tests — the synthetic test set above was extended afterward (rotation,
board tilt, an intentionally near-square/ambiguous box orientation, and an unequal real-data-like
per-edge shrink) so a regression in any of them is now caught without needing the golden set.

**Finding 1 — the whole scene reconstructs 4–9% small, from a focal-length mismatch between R-01
and R-02's pose sources, carried through R-04's camera-centre alignment.**

| | board scale | focal ratio (R-02 K ÷ VGGT K) |
|---|---|---|
| s-01 | 0.928 | 0.935 |
| s-02 | 0.907 | 0.918 |
| s-03 | 0.938 | 0.938 |
| s-04 | 0.958 | 0.956 |
| s-05 | 0.943 | 0.944 |

The board-ruler scale and the focal ratio track each other closely on all five objects (rms
residual 1.5–2.7 mm on 195–629 corner observations per object). Mechanism: a camera's distance from
the scene, in whatever units its pose source works in, scales with the focal length assumed for
that pose. VGGT predicts a focal 4–7% longer than R-02's board-calibrated one (R-02 currently uses
EXIF-derived K, ~1–3% short of R-03's own calibration — rerunning R-03 on s-04/s-05 gives a focal
ratio of ~0.96–0.97 against VGGT, same direction, a smaller gap). R-04's Umeyama fit aligns
reconstruction camera centres to board camera centres, so this mismatch becomes a global scale
error carried into every downstream millimetre. `height_ratio_over_board_scale` on s-04/s-05 is
0.994/1.006 — height is explained almost entirely by this one mechanism.

**Finding 2 — cross-sections shrink further than the board scale alone explains, and unevenly
per edge.**

| Object | edge (CAD mm) | ratio | ratio ÷ board scale |
|---|---|---|---|
| s-04 | 26.0 (short leg) | 0.838 | 0.874 |
| s-04 | 65.0 (hypotenuse) | 0.941 | 0.982 |
| s-04 | 60.0 (long leg) | 0.942 | 0.984 |
| s-05 | 30.0 × 4 | 0.760–0.799 | 0.806–0.847 |

Once the board's own scale error is divided out, s-05's four walls are still only 81–85% size, and
s-04's short leg is a noticeable outlier even among its own three edges (87% vs 98%/98%). This is
not explained by Finding 1. It is consistent with what R-17's segmentation check already flagged as
"depth-edge skirts" on the padlocks (s-01/s-02): VGGT's own per-pixel depth is pulled inward on a
wall seen at a shallow viewing angle, variably by wall and by object, not by a single scale factor
R-04 could absorb.

**What this means for ownership.** Finding 1 is R-04's to fix — it is a similarity transform fit on
camera centres, and camera-centre alignment is exactly where a focal mismatch between two pose
sources turns into scene scale. Split out to **R-23** (anchor R-04's scale on the board actually
visible in the scene, instead of on camera centres), since a fix here is more than "small" per this
story's acceptance criteria, and R-23 would also remove most of R-21's board-offset problem as a
side effect (noted on R-21). Finding 2 is R-01's per-pixel depth, not R-04's — no story yet;
suggested next step is rerunning s-04/s-05 through MASt3R or COLMAP (R-11/R-12, needs Lightning)
and comparing with `recon/cad_size_check.py`, once R-23 has removed Finding 1's contribution so the
comparison isolates Finding 2 cleanly.

No accuracy or tolerance claim is made anywhere above or in the code (D-003) — every number here is
`recon/cad_size_check.py`'s raw output, not a judgement against a tolerance. That is R-16's job,
once a real one exists.
