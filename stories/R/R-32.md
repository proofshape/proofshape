## Why this exists

R-22 found two reasons parts reconstruct off their CAD size, and R-23 fixed the first, the
focal-length scale error. The second has no story. Cross-sections shrink unevenly from edge to edge,
and R-22 traced that to R-01's per-pixel depth.

It's still there:
- **After R-23,** s-04's edges measured 0.840 (26 mm), 0.936 (65 mm) and 1.002 (60 mm) of CAD.
  s-05's 30 mm edges measured 0.854–0.919.
- **On 2026-11-07 (P-29),** s-04 from all 30 photos gave 0.886 on the 65 mm edge. Its mid-height
  walls came out 3–9% narrow.

It's the only known error in the part's own shape that no story owns. R-22 named the next step:
rerun s-04 and s-05 through MASt3R or COLMAP and compare them with `recon/cad_size_check.py`.

## Acceptance criteria

- **Same frames, two or more backends, one measure.** Reconstruct s-04 and s-05, the two golden
  objects with CAD, from the same frames. Use VGGT and at least one other backend: MASt3R, plus
  COLMAP if it completes on these frames. Run each on `main`'s current pipeline with R-23's board
  anchor, and measure it with `recon/cad_size_check.py --cad`. Record side by side, for each run:
  - the board scale and height ratio;
  - every edge's ratio, and its ratio over board scale;
  - the fit residual.
- **Photo-subset noise is measured, not assumed.** Run every backend on all 30 frames and on the
  two 15-frame halves (every other frame, starting from the first and from the second). On
  2026-11-07, the choice of subset alone moved s-04's 65 mm edge from 0.886 to 0.963. A difference
  between backends counts only if it's larger than that spread.
- **A conclusion, with the numbers behind it.** Say which of three it is:
  - VGGT-specific: another backend's edges sit nearer 1 by more than the subset spread;
  - shared by every backend, which points at later stages or the capture itself;
  - unresolved.

  Then name the next step. A fix that's more than small becomes its own story, R-22's rule.
- **No accuracy or tolerance claim (D-003).** Every ratio is against nominal CAD, not calipers;
  caliper truth is G-03's and G-04's. FDM drift applies, so the notes say so beside the figures.

## Tests

This is a measurement spike. The commands and figures go in the notes, as its written check.

Prefer the existing tools, `scripts/smoke_r14.py --backend` and `recon/cad_size_check.py`, so no

## Notes and decisions

- **Where it runs.** MASt3R and COLMAP need CUDA. R-11 ran on the shared Lightning T4, and R-12
  uses PyCOLMAP's CUDA 12 build there. Running VGGT on the same host keeps every backend on one
  machine. If VGGT runs on the Mac instead, #261's `OMP_NUM_THREADS=1` workaround applies until
  R-31 lands. Stop the Lightning deployment afterwards: nothing stops it automatically (R-15,
  #107).
- **Confidence filters differ by backend.** VGGT's threshold is measured (D-045). MASt3R's is on a
  different, unmeasured scale. COLMAP's is a binary validity mask (R-12), so
  `reconstruction_runner.run_on_capture` defers to each backend's own default. Use those defaults
  and record them.
- **COLMAP may not finish.** R-12's real s-04 run used 8 frames. If a 30-frame or 15-frame run
  fails, record the failure as a result rather than dropping the backend silently.
- **Figures to compare against.**
  - R-22's Finding 2 table and R-23's golden rerun, in their story files.
  - The 2026-11-07 30-vs-15-photo test, in P-29 and the progress log. Those runs used
    `OMP_NUM_THREADS=1`.
- **G-04 measures each edge's bias against caliper truth later** (its first criterion cites R-22's
  Finding 2). This spike is about the cause. Its result tells G-04 which backend's numbers to
  expect.
- **Fold check** (2026-11-07).
  - Searched `stories/` and `docs/decisions.md` for Finding 2, per-pixel depth, shallow-angle,
    cross-section and wall-depth. R-17, R-20, R-22 and R-23 record the finding, and G-04 measures
    it once calipers exist. None investigates the cause.
  - R-16 validates known-size tolerance, and is blocked on G-03 and G-04.
  - R-30 changes fusion. This spike measures R-01's raw point cloud, which is what
    `cad_size_check` reads.

  So it's a new story. It can't fold into G-04, which is blocked behind lab access, while this
  can run now against nominal CAD.
