## Why this exists

D-060 (from the review on #241/R-26) traces the "raised board layer" R-17 found 4-6 mm above the
real board on two of the five golden objects to its actual cause: it is not a second physical
layer. It is the real board, as seen by a handful of frames whose VGGT pose is misaligned by a
few millimetres. R-23's alignment is one global similarity transform fit once across every
frame, so it cannot correct this per-frame drift.

Three rounds of R-26 tried to fix this downstream, in `measure_board_surface`'s layer-top search,
each catching the previous round's synthetic failure while introducing a new real-golden-data
one (round 3 cuts too low on s-02 through s-05 and fails s-01 outright). The reason every attempt
failed the same way: on the *merged* point cloud, this misalignment artifact and a genuine flat
part's own top are the same shape -- a dense planar patch a few millimetres above the board --
and no classifier built from height/location data on the merged cloud alone can tell them apart.
R-26 closes without solving this (D-060); it stays on `main`'s original search plus the explicit
`height_threshold_mm` override.

This story fixes it where the information actually still exists: per-frame, before the clouds are
merged. `fuse_run` already has each frame's own points and extrinsic. Re-anchoring each frame to
its own board plane, rather than trusting its pose as given, removes the misalignment at its
source instead of trying to classify around it afterward.

It also shrinks #231's board-skin artifact: one frame in that fresh s-04 run sits 3.0 mm above
the board with the cut at 2.5 mm, straddling the cut and surviving into fusion as skin on the
part. The same per-frame correction addresses that case too.

## Acceptance criteria

- Each frame's own board-plane offset is measured (within `fuse_run`'s existing board-candidate
  points for that frame) and corrected before that frame's points enter the merged cloud, so a
  misaligned frame's board no longer reads as an elevated layer.
- A frame with too little visible board to fit a plane raises/refuses explicitly rather than
  silently skipping correction or guessing -- the residual risk @TabeenRaoof's prototype flagged
  (every golden frame had enough board; a close-up phone frame might not).
- With re-anchoring applied, the five golden objects' height cut and kept geometry are measured
  and recorded, and compared explicitly against `main`'s existing (R-26-closed) cuts and against
  each other -- this changes every object's cut (D-060's prototype moved them from 3-6.5 mm down
  to about 2 mm), so this is not expected to match `main`'s numbers, only to be measured and
  recorded rather than assumed.
- `measure_board_surface`'s "first density gap from zero" search (the one round 1 of R-26
  rejected before the ghost layer was understood) is re-evaluated against the re-anchored cloud:
  D-060's prototype found it becomes correct once the ghost layer is gone. Record whether that
  holds, rather than reintroducing R-26's per-cell/connectivity machinery.

## Tests

- a synthetic frame whose own board plane is offset from the others is corrected into agreement,
  and a thin flat part placed on that frame's own board is no longer read as a second layer;
- a frame with too few board points to fit a plane raises rather than silently passing through
  uncorrected;
- the five golden objects, re-anchored, measured end to end (board σ, height cut, kept part
  geometry) and the figures recorded in this story's notes, not just asserted to "look better".

## Notes and decisions

- Follows from D-060 (R-26/#241's close-out). R-26 itself stays small and does not depend on
  this story landing.
- @TabeenRaoof's prototype (posted on #241) was a crude per-point vertical shift, not a proper
  extrinsic correction, and found per-frame tilt residuals up to 2.3 degrees -- the real fix
  should correct the frame's extrinsic (3 degrees of freedom), not just shift z. Estimate is
  @TabeenRaoof's own figure (4-5 h) plus the golden rerun, explicitly given as an estimate, not
  measured.
- This changes every golden object's fused output. R-24 and R-25's recorded figures were measured
  against the pre-re-anchoring geometry and must be re-measured against the corrected one, not
  assumed to still hold.
- Also addresses #231's board-skin artifact (a frame straddling the segmentation cut survives
  into fusion as skin on the part) -- the same per-frame drift, a different symptom.

- **Implementation (criteria 1-2, this PR):** `fit_board_plane_robust` is pulled out of
  `measure_board_surface` (R-21) so the identical robust fit runs per-frame, not a second,
  possibly-drifted copy of it. `reanchor_frames_to_board` (`recon/tsdf_fusion.py`) fits each
  frame's own board plane from its own candidate points, rotates its normal onto the global
  (pooled, already-robust) plane's normal, then shifts purely in z at that frame's own candidate
  centroid so the rotated plane lands exactly on the global one there -- the full rotation +
  translation the story asks for, not a per-point shift. Applied to both `board_frame_points`
  (so the global fit and `segment_part`'s masking see corrected geometry) and `board_extrinsic`
  (so `fuse_tsdf`'s voxel re-projection uses the corrected pose) -- derivation and the algebra
  are in the function's own docstring. A frame with fewer than `DEFAULT_MIN_FRAME_BOARD_POINTS`
  (100, a bare assumption, D-033, not yet checked against the golden set's real per-frame counts)
  of its own candidates raises explicitly (criterion 2).
- **What this PR doesn't cover (criteria 3-4):** the five-golden-object rerun and the
  "first density gap" re-evaluation both need the real golden fixture set, which this
  implementation pass doesn't have direct access to -- same limitation R-26 had. Asking
  @TabeenRaoof to run both on the final head, same as R-26's close-out. State stays `Claimed`,
  not `Done`, until that comes back.

- **Criteria 3-4, measured by @TabeenRaoof on `31c73e5` (review on #270):** every frame lands
  within 0.23 mm of the shared plane (was up to 4.4 mm), board sigma tightens 2-4x on all five
  objects, and every object's height cut drops to the 2.0 mm `min_clearance` floor as D-060's
  prototype predicted:

  Criterion 4 (first-density-gap rule, re-evaluated on the re-anchored pooled cloud): the first
  gap from the peak lands exactly where the current threshold-based cut already sits on all five
  objects (1.5 / 2.0 / 2.0 / 1.0 / 1.5 mm) -- D-060's prediction holds, and either rule picks the
  same cut once the ghost layer is gone.

  `DEFAULT_MIN_FRAME_BOARD_POINTS = 100` against real per-frame counts: smallest golden frame has
  8,384 candidates, median is about 25,600 -- the floor never comes close on golden data, so it
  remains unverified below that range, same caveat as when it was first set.

  Board-band coverage moved in both directions, not uniformly down: s-01/s-04 lose most of their
  board-skin false-Observed faces (24->0, 1,810->8 -- #231's artifact, as expected), but s-02's
  board-band Observed faces rise (315->1,773) because its cut moved from 5.0mm to the fixed 2.0mm
  floor while its post-correction board sigma (0.58mm) is still the highest of the five -- the cut
  sits at only about 3.4 sigma there. Noted, not acted on here; a sigma-relative cut is a
  candidate for R-33 or a later story, not this one.

- **Two blocking review findings on #270 (@TabeenRaoof), both resolved:**
  1. *Camera pose propagation* -- `provenance_run` independently rebuilt every frame's pose from
     `poses.npz` and the one global alignment, blind to this PR's per-frame correction, so the
     corrected mesh was being checked against the drifted, uncorrected cameras it was corrected
     away from. Fixed: `fuse_run` now persists its corrected per-frame extrinsic to
     `corrected_extrinsic.npz` in its own output directory; `provenance_run` takes an optional
     `fusion_dir` and prefers that file when present (recording which source it used as
     `extrinsic_source` in `provenance.json`), falling back to the old rebuild otherwise.
     `recon/service.py`'s real pipeline now passes its own `fusion_dir` through, and the
     standalone CLI gets a matching `--fusion-dir` flag. Covered by a new test that drifts a
     frame's raw pose and confirms `provenance_run` uses the corrected one instead when given.
     @TabeenRaoof reran the golden chain with `--fusion-dir` and confirmed `extrinsic_source:
     "reanchored"` on every object that completes -- the table below is that corrected-camera
     run, replacing the uncorrected-camera numbers recorded above.
  2. *s-03's re-anchored mesh fails provenance* -- the raw marching-cubes output had 6 edges
     shared by 3 faces (0 on `main`), tripping the same non-manifold refusal #247 already
     describes for the Poisson path, now reached via the default path too. Not guess-fixed:
     @TabeenRaoof measured it on the real s-03 mesh first (the 6 bad edges touch only 17 of
     43,043 faces, clustered at one part/board seam where the new 2.0mm cut meets the part; ruled
     out Open3D's `remove_non_manifold_edges()`, which deleted 14,751 faces on the same mesh) and
     handed back a small, evidence-backed fix rather than leaving the scoping call blind. Fixed:
     `mesh_cleanup.drop_non_manifold_faces` drops every face incident to an edge shared by more
     than two faces, before the component filter, so `fill_small_holes` closes the resulting gap
     normally; the dropped count is recorded in the cleanup report as
     `non_manifold_faces_dropped`. The other four golden objects have zero such edges, so this
     changes nothing for them. Covered by synthetic tests (an edge shared by three faces has
     every touching face dropped; a manifold mesh is untouched).

  **Corrected-camera golden figures (@TabeenRaoof, same head as the fix above), final
  area-weighted `observed_fraction`:**

  | | s-01 | s-02 | s-03 | s-04 | s-05 |
  |---|---|---|---|---|---|
  | `main` | 0.070 | 0.094 | 0.321 | 0.169 | 0.144 |
  | This PR, corrected cameras + non-manifold fix | 0.097 | 0.167 | 0.360 | 0.090 | 0.139 |
  | Sigma downgrades (R-10), corrected cameras | 62 | 102 | -- | 109 | 86 |

  s-03's own figures with the fix applied: cleanup keeps 35,979 faces, provenance observes
  8,213/21,312 vertices (37.4% of surface), uncertainty downgrades 333. The round-1 table above
  (uncorrected cameras, s-03 still failing) is left as-is for the record of what changed between
  rounds, not corrected in place.

  `#247` (Poisson output has the same non-manifold-refusal symptom at a much larger scale) is
  still open; `drop_non_manifold_faces` is a plain topological filter, not specific to this
  pipeline's marching-cubes path, so it should help there too -- noted on that issue, not claimed
  fixed by this PR, since Poisson's non-manifold counts (in the thousands) haven't been measured
  against it.

- **Non-blocking, still outstanding:** `offset_shift_mm`'s diagnostic conflates the real
  per-frame offset with an artifact of rotating about the board origin rather than each frame's
  own centroid (e.g. s-02 frame 26 recorded -8.46mm at a 2.45 deg tilt, though no frame's board
  actually sat more than about 4.4mm off). @TabeenRaoof confirmed the geometry itself is
  unaffected (rotating about the origin instead of the centroid only adds about 0.1mm of
  in-plane shift at 100mm and 2.3 deg) -- this is a reporting fix for a later story, not this
  one.
