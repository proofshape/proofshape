## Why this exists

D-072 puts the demo's reconstruction service on the M2 Max, reached from the phone over the
local network. Lightning is down twice over: no credits, and a platform security incident. The
integration runs (X-03, X-04), the Dec 2 backup recording (X-05) and the live defense all depend
on that path working on the day, in the room. The sprint plan's rule is "never demo live on a
laptop you have not tested in the room".

## Acceptance criteria

- **A demo-day runbook** in `recon/README.md`, followed from a clean checkout:
  - the Mac GPU setup the capture README still lacks: torch and torchvision first, then
    `requirements-gpu.txt`, `HF_HUB_DISABLE_XET=1`, and the weights cache;
  - starting the service: `PROOFSHAPE_RECON_DEVICE=mps uvicorn recon.service:app --host 127.0.0.1`,
    with `PROOFSHAPE_BUYER_KEY` set, and `GEMINI_API_KEY` for the live VLM tip;
  - starting the phone path: `PROOFSHAPE_RECON_PROXY=http://127.0.0.1:8000 npm run dev:device`;
  - **creating the order (added 2026-11-16, after R-34 and C-16 merged):** the service now refuses
    any code that isn't in its order store. Create one on `buyer.html` through the same dev
    server, with the buyer key and **the CAD of the part being scanned**, and open the capture
    link it gives (`?order=PO-…`) on the phone. A `curl` to `POST /v1/orders` is the fallback.
    `PO-48213-A` works only against the stub;
  - accepting the certificate on the phone;
  - shutting down.
- **One rehearsal in the defense room, or on its network,** with the iPhone: a 25–30-frame
  session, end to end to the model and report, timed per stage. It records which network works:
  the room's Wi-Fi, or the iPhone's personal hotspot with the Mac joined to it.
- **The S6 failure rehearsal, adapted to the Mac.** Stop the service in the middle of a session
  and bring it back. Record what the phone shows and whether the session recovers or has to
  restart.
- **The memory ceiling is in the runbook.** The demo runs on the 32 GB M2 Max. A fallback 18 GB Mac
  caps sessions at about 14 frames (measured on #146).

## Implementation notes

### 2026-11-15/16: home pre-check, before the room rehearsal (@TabeenRaoof)

The M2 Max (32 GB, macOS 26.6.2) on home Wi-Fi, with an iPhone in Safari through `npm run dev:device`,
on `main` at `5407b7d` (R-34 merged, C-16 not yet). Everything here is from the session records
in `proofshape-data/sessions/`, kept outside git.

- **Mac setup was already complete.** torch 2.14.1 with MPS (about 25 GiB recommended by Metal),
  VGGT, open3d 0.18 and google-genai were installed, and the VGGT weights were cached (4.7 GB).
- **Smoke test through the HTTP flow.** `scripts/smoke_r14.py` with the golden s-04 frames and
  `--base-url http://127.0.0.1:8000` passed. The script creates its own order, and needs
  `PROOFSHAPE_ENDPOINT_TOKEN` set to any value even locally. 30 frames reconstructed in
  47–50 s, against R-13's 80.21 s on the T4.
- **Real iPhone sessions of s-04**, each with an order carrying s-04's CAD:

  | Session | Frames | Finish to model | Reconstruction (load / inference) |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | `sess_8fdecd4b3a02` | 23 | 42 s | 38.8 s (14.4 / 19.8) |
  | `sess_1a215dae88d7` | 15 | 31 s | 29.6 s (13.9 / 11.0) |
  | `sess_fb1b028da4cd` | 15 | 51 s | 48.7 s (26.4 / 16.3) |

  All were metric on the ChArUco board, 69–79% Observed, with no warnings.
- **Accuracy**, against nominal CAD and not calipers (D-003). It's measured with
  `cad_size_check`, by hand, since I-07 doesn't exist yet. The clean 23-frame run read height
  0.947 and edges 0.961 / 0.953 / 1.041, with a 2.32 mm fit residual. The golden s-04 on the same
  Mac read 0.990 and 1.047 / 0.886 / 0.989, with 1.52 mm. So the phone path is comparable.
- **The lens didn't change.** The app's frames carry no EXIF, so each frame's focal length was
  estimated from the board. Every angled frame reads 474–586 px at 480×640, which is the main
  wide lens. The few outliers are straight-down views, where one view can't separate focal
  length from distance, and they look identical. The golden frames' EXIF is all the iPhone 16
  wide camera (5.96 mm f/1.6).
- **The live VLM tip works on the phone.** With a real `GEMINI_API_KEY`, in `sess_fb1b028da4cd`:
  - a photo of the Mac screen at frame 5 came back `screen_photo: true`, with the hint "Please
    take a direct photo of the physical part and reference sheet instead of photographing a
    screen";
  - the result was on disk 1.5 s later, so the responses for frames 6–10 carried it;
  - a later live tip was screenshotted on the iPhone.

  This closes the gap X-02 recorded.
- **The failure rehearsal passed.** In `sess_2e59418b1077` the service was stopped after frame 5
  for 91 s, and in `sess_8bf24fbc0f29` for 138 s. Both times the next capture continued in the
  same session, and both finished and reconstructed (31 s and 25 s). Sessions and orders live on
  disk, so nothing was lost.
- **Memory.** The first phone session, with swap at 60.9 of 61.4 GB, took 113.5 s, including a
  60 s model load. After a restart, swap climbs back to about 13 GB over a few runs, because a
  30-frame run peaks around 17 GB. The service drops to 0.2 GB between sessions, so it isn't a
  leak. **On the day: restart the Mac, close other apps, and do one warm-up run.** VGGT reloads
  for every session (14–26 s); keeping it loaded would be a small story of its own.
- **Gaps found, each for its own issue, not fixed here:**
  1. With the service down, the phone shows raw "uploadFrame: response body was not valid JSON
     (status 500)".
  2. Switching away from Safari reloaded the page and lost the session. `sess_9cd4d8e064eb`
     stays "capturing" on the server.
  3. The VLM hints are confusing: for frame 0 it asks for "reference frame 0", and when only the
     camera moved it says "orientation has changed".
  4. A screen photo isn't kept out of the model. The VLM only advises, and a screen on an
     unsampled frame (frame 6 in `sess_1a215dae88d7`) isn't checked at all. That run's 26 mm edge
     read 1.345, against 0.961 clean. This is A-02's case.
  5. The blur gate accepted a badly blurred frame (frame 20 of `sess_8fdecd4b3a02`).
  6. `cad_size_check` fails on the resumed sessions, even at `--min-confidence 1.0001`, because a
     low pile of stray points wins `_largest_connected_blob`. G-04 measures with this tool.
  7. The report's "Orientation" stays at "Loading…" whenever the verdict is unavailable. That's
     #335's follow-up.

### 2026-11-17: the runbook (criteria 1 and 4)

The runbook is in `recon/README.md`, under "R-36 · demo day on the M2 Max". It covers:
- setup from a clean checkout;
- the three terminals: the service, a warm-up run with golden s-04, and the phone path;
- the order through `buyer.html`, with `curl` as the fallback;
- the certificate, what to do when something goes wrong, the memory ceiling, and shutting down.

Every command is one that ran at home on 2026-11-15/16. The two checks that hadn't run before
were run on 2026-11-17: the Metal check prints `True`, and VGGT imports. Commands carry no
inline `#` comments, because zsh doesn't treat them as comments at an interactive prompt, which
broke the first home attempt.

Two stale lines that the runbook would contradict were corrected in the same PR:
- `recon/README.md`'s R-14 section said the service accepts any non-empty order code;
- `capture/README.md`'s C-13 section said the same, and pointed at `?order=PO-TEST`.

Since R-34, both are refused unless the code is in the order store.
