## Why this exists

D-072 puts the demo's reconstruction service on the M2 Max, reached from the phone over the
local network. Lightning is down twice over: no credits, and a platform security incident. The
integration runs (X-03, X-04), the Dec 2 backup recording (X-05) and the live defense all depend
on that path working on the day, in the room. The sprint plan's rule is "never demo live on a
laptop you have not tested in the room".

## Acceptance criteria

- **A demo-day runbook** in `recon/README.md`, followed from a clean checkout:
  - the Mac GPU setup the capture README still lacks: torch and torchvision first, then
    `requirements-gpu.txt`, `HF_HUB_DISABLE_XET=1`, and the weights cache;
  - starting the service: `PROOFSHAPE_RECON_DEVICE=mps uvicorn recon.service:app --host 127.0.0.1`;
  - starting the phone path: `PROOFSHAPE_RECON_PROXY=http://127.0.0.1:8000 npm run dev:device`;
  - accepting the certificate on the phone;
  - shutting down.
- **One rehearsal in the defense room, or on its network,** with the iPhone: a 25–30-frame
  session, end to end to the model and report, timed per stage. It records which network works:
  the room's Wi-Fi, or the iPhone's personal hotspot with the Mac joined to it.
- **The S6 failure rehearsal, adapted to the Mac.** Stop the service in the middle of a session
  and bring it back. Record what the phone shows and whether the session recovers or has to
  restart.
- **The memory ceiling is in the runbook.** The demo runs on the 32 GB M2 Max. A fallback 18 GB Mac
  caps sessions at about 14 frames (measured on #146).

## Tests

A written check: the rehearsal record below, with timings and the network that worked. No code
