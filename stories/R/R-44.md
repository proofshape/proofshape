## Why this exists

R-30 corrects each photo's own board plane before fusion: its tilt and its height, three of a
pose's six degrees of freedom. It leaves the other three alone: where the photo sits in the
board plane (x, y) and its rotation about the board's normal. P-41's accuracy review measured
what that leaves, on `main` at `607450a`, on the ten printed parts with caliper readings (s-04,
s-05, `g05_00`, `g05_02`, `g02_01` to `g02_06`):

- **Each photo sits about 2 mm from where the board says it is.** A photo's own ChArUco corners,
  lifted through that photo's depth, land a median 2.23 mm from their known board positions
  after R-23's global alignment (95th percentile 4.35 mm, 281 photos). R-30 takes each photo's
  mean height offset at its corners from 0.84 mm to 0.12 mm (median magnitude), and leaves
  1.60 mm in the board plane (median over the same photos), which moves the centre of the board
  by a median 1.5 mm and up to 5.0 mm. A rigid fit of each photo to its own corners would leave
  0.65 mm.
- **The photos disagree with each other on the part.** One photo's top-face pixels agree to
  0.25–0.29 mm. Across photos the top face differs by 0.66–0.73 mm (standard deviation), and
  walls by 0.8–1.1 mm, with single photos −4.2 to +2.0 mm off.
- **A sideways shift doesn't move a flat top face. It moves every wall and edge.** That matches
  the two measured floors: 0.86 mm for heights (D-073) and 5.278 mm in-plane (D-078). On these
  ten parts, each against its own CAD, wall vertices read 0.1 to 2.1 mm inside the CAD (median
  per part), and a good part first Passes at 3.7 to 6 mm with ambiguity set aside. Those
  CAD-relative readings mix the print's own departure from nominal, reconstruction error and
  registration error. They don't say how much of the wall error is this one cause.

P-41 also re-fused the same saved depth with each photo rigidly re-anchored to its own corners
before R-30's plane step, in a scratch harness outside the repo, and measured the result with
the repo's own cleanup, provenance, registration, deviation and verdict code:

| Ten parts, each against its own CAD | `main` | Rigid re-anchoring, nothing else changed |
|---|---|---|
| Wall readings, 95% within (median of parts) | 3.37 mm | 1.38 mm |
| Worst reading on a part (median of parts) | 4.31 mm | 1.88 mm |
| Parts that Pass at 3.7 mm (one non-critical `face`, ambiguity set aside) | 3 | 7 |
| s-05's two 30 mm widths at mid-height (caliper 29.97 mm) | 26.7 and 25.8 mm | 28.7 and 28.6 mm |
| Six widths of s-05, `g02_02` and `g02_06` against calipers, mean absolute error | 2.3 mm | 1.0 mm |

Seven parts improve. **Three read worse:**

- **`g05_00`, 5.58 → 9.80 mm.** It comes out 180° off, with no ambiguity flag.
- **`g02_01`, 4.14 → 5.42 mm,** at fitness 0.29.
- **`g05_02`, 5.01 → 5.26 mm.** The worst reading is at its bridged 12 mm hole in both runs.

The first two are CAD alignment, not reconstruction. Started from `main`'s pose, the same two
re-anchored meshes read 5.22 and 2.51 mm, and eight of the ten parts Pass at 3.7 mm. The pose
search samples the whole model surface, and surface that isn't the part misleads it (P-41).
I-15 is the story for that. The third is small, and nothing in this correction addresses a
bridged hole.

These are screening results: one capture per part, a scratch harness, no repo code. This story
turns the correction into tested repo code and measures it again.

## Acceptance criteria

- **The correction, in `fuse_run`, before R-30's plane step.** For each photo, fit the rigid
  transform (rotation and translation, no scale) that takes that photo's lifted ChArUco corners
  onto their known board coordinates, and apply it to that photo's points and camera pose. A
  photo with fewer usable corners than a stated minimum, or one R-38 rejected, keeps today's
  behaviour, and the report says which and why. `tsdf_fusion.json` records, per photo, the
  corners used, the residual before and after, and the shift applied. Provenance and uncertainty
  use the same corrected poses, through R-30's `corrected_extrinsic.npz`.
- **Measured on real captures and recorded here**, before against after on the same saved depth:
  the ten calipered parts and the saved s-04 phone sessions. The record has the per-photo corner
  residual, per-vertex wall and top-face deviation against each part's own CAD, the box-shaped
  parts' widths against calipers, Observed and stray-surface counts, the CAD pose each run
  landed on, and fusion time. A part that gets worse is reported, not dropped. @dalwalyk or
  @mbj1994 re-runs at least s-04 and s-05 from a clean checkout.
- **Whether it is on in the served path is a team decision, behind a gate set before the
  measurement.** It goes on by default only if the record above meets all three of these on
  every one of the ten parts, and both other members then agree in a decision (number checked
  per D-028):
  - **Pose.** The CAD pose with the correction on is right, and it is the same pose as with the
    correction off wherever the off pose is right too. "Right" and "same" are as I-15 defines
    them: against a placement read from the photographs, and to within 2 mm of posed surface.
  - **Error.** Neither the part's worst reading nor its wall 95% figure is more than 0.3 mm
    higher with the correction on than with it off.
  - **Coverage.** The part loses no more than 5% of its Observed vertices within 5 mm of the
    part, and its share of Observed vertices farther than that rises by no more than 5 points.

  A part that misses one blocks the default, unless the record explains it and both members
  accept that explanation by name in the decision. Until then the correction merges off by
  default, and this story says so. Either way the contract is untouched, and D-073 and D-078
  are not changed here.

## Tests

Unit tests are written with the correction, all synthetic:

- frames given a known sideways shift and rotation each come back onto the board, and the
  recovered transform matches the planted one;
- the corrected points and the corrected camera pose stay consistent with each other (the same
  check R-30's derivation makes);
- a frame with too few corners is left alone and reported; so is a frame R-38 rejected;
- corners that already agree give no correction;
- through `fuse_run` on a synthetic box with per-frame sideways offsets, the fused walls sit
  closer to the box with the correction than without;
- provenance reads the corrected poses.

The real-capture runs are a written check: commands, commit, captures and numbers in this file.
The captures and run outputs stay outside git.

## Notes and decisions

- **Written up and claimed together on 2026-11-20**, from P-41's review (R-43's claim is the
  pattern). The evidence above is P-41's; its write-up and script are P-41's pull request.
- **Fold check** (`main` at `607450a`, and every origin branch for the story number):
  - R-30 is the story this extends. Its own notes scope it to the frame's board plane.
  - R-23 solves one global similarity from the lifted corners. R-38 drops whole photos whose
    corners are far out. Neither corrects a photo that is a millimetre or two off.
  - R-43 (@dalwalyk, claim in #424) sweeps the fusion grid with every other setting fixed. This
    story changes where each photo sits, not the grid. They interact: in P-41's run a 1 mm grid
    on its own left the walls where they were (3.55 → 3.31, 3.92 → 3.85, 4.32 → 4.29 mm on s-04,
    s-05 and `g02_05`). R-43's sweep is worth reading on both inputs.
  - I-14 and D-086 keep surface outside the CAD's outline out of the verdict. #292 is the board
    skin. Neither moves a photo.
- **The gate, against P-41's scratch run.** That run would miss it on four parts. `g05_00`
  misses on pose, and with it on error. `g02_01` misses on error, and its pose is in doubt at
  that fitness. `g02_04` and `g02_06` miss on coverage: their share of stray surface rose from
  38% to 48% and from 2% to 12%. `g05_02`'s 0.25 mm rise is inside the error margin, and is
  reported here so it isn't lost.
- **Where the three margins come from.** 0.3 mm is about twice what two members' machines differ
  by on the same capture: @dalwalyk's fresh run of s-04 and s-05 (#427) read the wall 95% figure
  0.13 and 0.12 mm away from the first run's. The 5% and 5 points are round numbers. All three
  are proposals for the claim's reviewers to agree or change now, before the measurement, so
  they aren't fitted to it.
- **Re-worded on 2026-11-21 after review on #426** (@dalwalyk): the third criterion now says how
  a pose is judged and what blocks the default, and all three parts that read worse are listed.
- **Rigid, not a similarity.** P-41 tried both. Rescaling each photo's depth as well gave the
  same wall gain, added stray board surface on `g05_00` (24% → 34% of Observed vertices), and
  needs a per-photo depth factor carried into provenance. A rigid correction changes camera
  poses only, which R-30's file already carries.
- **What P-41's run says this does not fix:**
  - the 12 mm through-hole on the G-05 plates, which is bridged (+5.1 to +5.6 mm on the control
    in every variant), so the 4 mm hole shift still reads like its control (#407);
  - how fragile the smallest part's alignment is: `g02_01` (the 20 mm square) reads 2.51 mm from
    `main`'s pose and 5.42 mm from the free search, at fitness 0.29 to 0.37;
  - stray board surface (#292's kind). Its share rose with re-anchoring on `g02_04` (38% → 48%)
    and `g02_06` (2% → 12%). The second criterion reports it.
- **It does not lower any threshold by itself.** D-073 and D-078 were measured on R-01's raw
  cloud through `cad_size_check`, which this story does not touch. In P-41's run the parts that
  Pass do so at exactly 3.7 mm, the existing gate (0.70 × 5.278), with worst readings of 1.1 to
  2.8 mm. Re-measuring the floors on the fused mesh is separate work, listed in P-41.
- **Freeze.** R-30's correction is shipped behaviour, and this completes it, so it isn't a new
  feature under M5. It still changes every fused mesh, so it has to merge by M6 (Dec 2): Dec 3–4
  is the code freeze, with no behaviour-changing commits. If the record or the reviews aren't in
  by then, the third criterion's off-by-default path is what merges.
- **Work in progress at the claim** (D-064): R-36 is waiting on the defense room. C-26's claim
  is open in #418. P-41 closes in its own pull request. I-15 is claimed in the same pull request
  as this story and is worked first, because this story's default waits on it.
- **Estimate basis:** R-30 took 6 h against 5, R-38 7 h against 4. The method and the before
  figures already exist, so 6 h covers the function and its tests (about 3 h), the wiring and
