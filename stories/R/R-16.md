## Why this exists

R-04's known-size acceptance criterion originally read "an object of known size measures
correctly within a stated tolerance." That criterion asks two different questions: whether the
alignment produces a comparable measurement at all, and whether that measurement is *good
enough* — which needs a real tolerance figure to judge it against. R-04 answered the first
question and recorded the raw comparison below; it could not answer the second, because no
tolerance figure exists anywhere in the project yet. That figure has to come from the
ground-truth calibration study (`G-01` printer characterisation through `G-05` defect ladder),
not from a single hand-picked comparison. A PR attempt to derive one from that single comparison
anyway (D-039, 3x the alignment RMS) was reverted on review — see D-032 and R-04's notes for why
a well-caveated provisional number still isn't acceptable here. This story exists so the judgment
happens once a real tolerance does, instead of never happening or happening dishonestly.

## What R-04 already recorded, that this story judges

This is the one comparison that exists as of R-04 closing (PR #39). Copied here so this story is
self-contained and doesn't require re-reading R-04's full notes to know what it's acting on:

- **Specimen:** `s-04`, a printed part with a known triangular face.
- **Reference measurement:** @TabeenRaoof, digital caliper, 0.01 mm resolution, 10 readings,
  mean **60.011 mm**, range 0.02 mm (recorded on PR #35). The measured feature is the face's
  near-vertical leg — apex to the adjacent base corner, opposite the hypotenuse, distinct from
  the short base edge.
- **Reconstruction-side measurement:** the same face located in the aligned, board-frame point
  cloud (`points-board-frame-mm.ply`, R-01's `points.ply` transformed by R-04's
  `translation_mm`/`rotation`/`scale`). Three corners were picked by visual inspection of a
  strict top-down orthographic projection (board x/y, mm): `A = (102.13, 80.73)`,
  `B = (42.42, 52.05)`, `C = (102.69, 52.14)`. Edge lengths: A-B = 66.24 mm (hypotenuse),
  A-C = 28.60 mm, B-C = **60.27 mm** (the leg matching the caliper feature, right angle at C
  confirmed by `sqrt(A-C^2 + B-C^2) = 66.71 mm`, within ~0.5 mm of A-B).
  - **Related alignment diagnostic, not a tolerance input on its own:** the same Lightning run's
    fitted camera-centre alignment RMS was 10.015 mm (calibrated-K, 29/30 frames;
    `metric_alignment.json`). D-039 multiplied this by 3x to get a 30 mm tolerance — that
    reasoning was rejected, but the number itself is still a legitimate alignment diagnostic
    that `G-04`'s noise-floor work may want as one input among several, not the only one.
- **Difference:** 60.27 mm vs. 60.011 mm = **0.26 mm (~0.4%)**.
- **Known limitations of this comparison**, already flagged in R-04: corner-picking was manual,
  not from a repeatable automated procedure; naive per-axis extrema, PCA-long-axis slabbing, and
  RANSAC plane/line fitting were all tried and failed on this part before the top-down
  projection worked; no measurement uncertainty has been characterised for the point-picking
  step itself; and it is one specimen, one feature, one reading — not a study.

## Acceptance criteria

- Obtains a real, measured tolerance from the ground-truth calibration study (`G-03` caliper
  measurement, `G-04` noise floor per tier) — not derived, re-estimated, or approximated by this
  story itself.
- Applies that tolerance to the s-04 comparison recorded above (0.26 mm difference on the B-C
  edge), and to any additional specimen comparisons `G-02`/`G-03` produce by the time this story
  is picked up — a single specimen was flagged as insufficient evidence on its own, so if more
  than one is available by then, this story should judge all of them, not only s-04.
- Reports the result per specimen as within-tolerance, out-of-tolerance, or explicitly
  insufficient-evidence (for example, if the calibration study's conditions don't cover this
  part's size, material, or feature type) — never a pass/fail dressed up from a number this
  story invented itself.
- Records the outcome back in `stories/R-04.md`'s notes (referencing this story and whatever
  decision number the real tolerance gets in `docs/decisions.md`), so R-04's known-size
  acceptance criterion has a final, traceable answer instead of a permanent forward reference.
- Unit tested against the stated tolerance, once one exists to test against.
