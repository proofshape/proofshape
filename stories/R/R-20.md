## Why this exists

VGGT scores every pixel's depth with a confidence. R-01 keeps all of them: `--min-confidence`
defaults to 0.0, because, in `recon/vggt_runner.py`'s own words, "no threshold has been measured
yet." R-07's fusion doesn't read the confidence at all. R-17 measured the result on all five golden
objects:
- 11.5–29.3% of the segmented points are stray points, not the part.
- About three-quarters of pixels sit at VGGT's confidence floor.
- As a diagnostic, dropping points below 1.5 cut stray points to 1.1–2.3% with the part's measured
  size unchanged.

R-18's fused meshes for s-03/s-04/s-05 failed with both fusion methods. Fusing those same
low-confidence pixels is the leading suspect, but nobody has tested it.

## Acceptance criteria

- R-01's default confidence threshold is chosen from measurement on the golden set, not guessed.
  For a range of thresholds, report the stray-point fraction and how much of the part survives;
  s-04 and s-05 can be checked against their CAD. Record the chosen value and its evidence in
  `docs/decisions.md`, and change the CLI default.
- R-07's TSDF fusion uses VGGT's per-pixel confidence, so a pixel left out of the cloud is left out
  of the fused surface too. Whether it masks or weights is the owner's call; record it.
- R-18's check (`fuse_run`, default and `--method poisson`) is rerun on all five golden objects. The
  per-object results are recorded beside R-18's, so the before and after can be compared.

## Tests

Synthetic depth maps where a low-confidence spurious region must not appear in the fused TSDF.
Check the same test fails with confidence ignored, so it is known to catch the problem. The chosen
threshold must reach `run.json` from the CLI. The golden-set rerun is a manual check, the same
exception R-17 and R-18 use: judging a real fused mesh is a human look.

## Notes and decisions

2026-10-09 (when written up, from R-17's findings):
- **Don't overshoot.** At confidence ≥ 5, s-05 kept 27,484 of 129,140 segmented points, and its
  walls became too sparse to measure. The useful range in R-17's diagnostic was well below that.
- **R-21 interacts with the mesh rerun.** `fuse_run` applies R-06's `segment_part`, so the part's
  base is cut off in the fused mesh too. Fusing an open-bottomed shell may itself hurt R-18's
  Poisson results. R-21 landing first would make this story's rerun more telling, but it isn't a
  hard dependency.
