## Why this exists

`recon.scale_agreement.check_scale_agreement` (R-05) is never called by `recon/service.py`. So a
real `getReconstruction` response never has `scale_agreement`, though the contract, the
`ScaleAgreement` model (`recon/service.py:155`) and the report page (`capture/src/report.ts:237`)
all expect it. X-03 (I3) needs a substituted part caught on scale, and that can't happen until the
check runs.

## Acceptance criteria

- **The check runs after registration.** It goes in the post-reconstruction measurement step
  (`recon/service.py`, after `registration.check_ambiguity`). It is given the board pitch
  (`recon.board_pose.BOARD_SQUARE_MM`) and the best candidate's `free_scale_residual` as the CAD
  registration residual. Its result is saved on the session record and returned as
  `scale_agreement` by `getReconstruction`.
- **When there's no residual, there's no result.** `free_scale_residual` is `None` when the
  Observed evidence is planar-only, or has too few points or ICP correspondences
  (`_free_scale_residual`, I-03). In that case `scale_agreement` is omitted, as
  `recon/README.md` already says for missing values. A board-pitch-only result is never invented
  (D-007).
- **A failure doesn't end the session.** If the check raises, the error is logged and
  `scale_agreement` is omitted, like the existing registration failure path. It doesn't fail the
  reconstruction.
- **The caliper source is out of scope.** The contract has no request field for a typed caliper
  reading. Adding one is a D-031 round and not this story's work. Say that in `recon/README.md`.

## Tests

Written with the change, as service-level tests on a synthetic session, the way
`tests/test_verdict_endpoint.py` exercises the post-reconstruction step. The check's input can
be forced by stubbing `free_scale_residual` on the selected candidate:
- a residual present gives a `scale_agreement` with the two sources and the right `flagged`
  value;
- `None` omits the field;
- a raising check omits it and the session still completes.

### Real-data limitation (2026-11-26, post-merge)

Seven of ten correct parts would display a disagreement flag at the current threshold.
The residual varies in both directions; its spread does not establish a uniform print-scale
error. The 94 focused tests reported by Dalwalyk establish the wiring, not reliable
real-part scale diagnosis. Neither a flag nor its absence establishes substitution or
dimensional accuracy. X-03 must not claim scale-based detection from these data;
a separately demonstrated shape-based detection is allowed by its existing criterion.
See [manual §7](../../docs/manual.md#7--scale-integrity) and [issue #456](https://github.com/proofshape/proofshape/issues/456)
for residual reliability and a team-agreed interim serving policy before public deployment.
The merged behavior and D-007/D-039 threshold are unchanged in this documentation closeout;
the reliability work is separate and not claimed complete.
