## Why this exists

Sprint plan §5: "I4 — during S6. Whole system on both phones; record the backup demo." §7's gate
table: "Gate M6 · I4: Thanksgiving week is bug-fixing only. The recorded backup demo is produced
here, not in December." This is the full-system integration point, run once the app is
feature-complete going into S6 (M5's freeze). It needs "capture → reconstruct → inspect → report"
working end to end, which means the verdict chain (X-03's own gate) must already be proven, not
just C-18/C-23's device testing — caught on review, the original draft depended on C-18 alone,
which would have let the checker flip this to Ready the day C-18 merges, with no verdict in
existence yet.

## Acceptance criteria

- The full capture → reconstruct → inspect → report loop runs on one iPhone and one Android
  phone, on the actual phones C-18 and C-23 tested — split into two stories on review of C-18
  (2026-11-12), so this criterion now names both rather than one.
- A recorded video of one full, successful session exists, produced during S6, satisfying the
  working agreement "keep a screen recording of the current working path."
- A written log records what broke and was fixed in this hardening pass, since Thanksgiving week
  is explicitly bug-fixing only per M6.
