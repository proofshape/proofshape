## Why this exists

Distinct from X-04 (running and recording the I4 integration day itself): sprint plan §2's
working agreement states "never demo live on a laptop you have not tested in the room. From R2
onward, keep a screen recording of the current working path. If the network or the pod fails,
play the recording and keep talking." M6·I4 names the recorded backup demo as its deliverable,
and M8's gate says "The recording is the plan of record if the network fails." This story is
specifically about keeping that single canonical recording current and ready, for the review
boundaries after S6 and the final defense — not the one-time act of producing it during I4.

## Acceptance criteria

- A single, canonical, dated recorded-demo file exists, covering the full working path as of S6,
  and is the one referenced everywhere a backup demo is needed (not a scattered set of ad hoc
  recordings).
- It is referenced from review/demo preparation for the remaining review boundaries and S8's
  defense ("live demo with the recording ready to cut to" per the gate table).
- A written record states where it is stored and when it was last refreshed, since the recording
  can go stale if the app changes after S6 and nobody re-records it.

## Tests

Written-check exception: the deliverable is a recording and a written record of its currency,
not unit-testable code.

## Notes and decisions

- If the app changes materially after S6, this story (or a lightweight follow-up) should
  re-record rather than let a stale recording stand as "the plan of record."
