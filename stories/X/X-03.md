## Why this exists

Sprint plan §5: "I3 — Fri Oct 30. Verdict proven end to end." §7's gate table: "Gate M4 · I3:
planted 4 mm hole shift → Fail; unobserved region → Rescan; substituted part caught on scale or
shape." C-14's own notes already point here directly: "The real deviations come from I-06 and the
real verdict from I-07. Both are still titles-only, and both are checked end to end at X-03 or
X-04." Every one of those three scenarios needs a real verdict, which needs I-06, I-07 and G-04's
σ_floor, none of which exist yet — and each scenario has its own real prerequisite, caught on
review, beyond just "the verdict chain exists":

- **The planted 4 mm hole shift** is `g05_02_hole_shift_4mm.stl`, explicitly labelled "(M4's
  part)" in G-05's own notes, photographed as part of the defect ladder by G-09 (which itself
  depends on G-05 and G-08). This scenario needs G-09, not just the verdict chain.
- **"Substituted part caught on scale"** needs the scale-agreement check actually running and
  correct. Today `recon.scale_agreement.check_scale_agreement` is never called anywhere in
  `recon/service.py` — `scale_agreement` is always absent from a real `getReconstruction`
  response — and issue #181 (still open) found the CAD ratio is the reciprocal of what D-039
  intends, so agreeing sources would be wrongly flagged and contradicting ones missed. Both need
  fixing before this scenario can honestly be demonstrated on scale.
- **A known risk to the "caught on shape" half:** on real golden data (s-04), I-05 already flags
  `orientation_ambiguous` from a near-duplicate pose only 1.6° and 4.2 mm from the best (recorded
  in R-33.md, from @TabeenRaoof's own rerun) — not a second real orientation, but I-05's
  RMSE-ratio rule flagging it anyway. Unless I-05's de-duplication is tightened first, a planted
  shift on a similarly-shaped part could come back `Rescan` instead of `Fail`, for the wrong
  reason.
