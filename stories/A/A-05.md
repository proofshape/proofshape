## Why this exists

Proposal §6.6: "A generative model then samples K plausible completions of the geometry it did
not [see], and different random seeds produce different surfaces. Where those samples disagree
is precisely where the system is guessing... The spread across samples is the disagreement map:
high spread means the surface is unknown, not merely unseen from one angle... The disagreement
drives capture guidance and nothing else. It tells the next-view scorer where one more
photograph buys the most information." The glossary gives the canonical term: "Where the
generative completion samples differ from each other. Drives capture guidance only; never enters
a verdict." A-04's own notes already name this story as the next consumer of its samples: "Those
samples feed the disagreement map (A-05), which drives capture guidance and nothing else."

**What "disagreement" concretely means is already measured, not open:** A-12's acceptance
criteria defined and measured exactly this metric pair on real data — "U, the median seed-to-seed
distance where the camera did not see (more than 2 mm from the partial); O, the same where it
did" — with real T4 figures (0.77-2.48 mm unseen vs. 0.20-0.25 mm seen on s-04/s-02) confirming
"different seeds do give different surfaces on our data, and the differences sit in the surface
the camera did not see, which is what A-05's disagreement map needs."

A-12 also recorded a direct caution for this story: a faster completion configuration (50
sparse-structure steps, nearly halving runtime) left the seed-to-seed disagreement on unseen
surface at 0.40 mm, "below the criterion's 0.79 mm, half of median U(D) (1.58 mm)" at the default
step count — A-12's own conclusion: "fewer steps don't keep the spread" this story needs, so any
future speed optimisation to completion must be checked against that before being adopted.

**Ready by the declared dependency graph (A-04 and A-12 are both Done), but a real caveat
remains, same pattern as C-21's O-005 note:** `stories/README.md`'s Generative-AI section states
plainly that "A-05 is the first story that needs real per-session samples," and that completion
doesn't run on a host that serves suppliers yet — two concrete, named obstacles (D-058's
~49-minute inline budget, and the runner needing a second Python/torch the image's container test
currently forbids). This story's own acceptance criteria below scope its first pass to prepared
fixtures accordingly, leaving real per-session use to the not-yet-written runner story.

## Acceptance criteria

- Computes a per-vertex or per-region disagreement score over the K `sample_<k>.ply` completion
  files, using A-12's already-validated U/O methodology (point-to-triangle distance between
  sample pairs, pooled across seeds) rather than inventing a new metric.
- Feeds the next-view scorer so the capture-guidance arrow is "driven by the disagreement map" —
  the sprint plan's own S5 capture bullet names this exact consumer.
- Never contributes to a verdict and never changes served/Observed geometry (D-005/D-006,
  lane-wide hard rule) — disagreement is a guidance signal layered on Inferred surface only.
- **Decided (so this is genuinely Ready, not an open fork — AGENTS.md: "an ambiguous story is not
  Ready"):** this story's first pass runs against A-12-style prepared fixtures only, the same way
  A-04's own written checks did on the M2 Max. Running it against real per-session supplier data
  waits for the not-yet-written completion-runner-on-a-supplier-host story; this story does not
  claim that capability and must not silently assume the runner exists.

### Built (2026-11-11)

D-066 has the rule and why. In short:
- **The map.** `recon/disagreement.py` builds it inline, straight after the completion stage. It
  writes `completion/disagreement/{disagreement.json, targets.npz}`, never raises and never warns.
- **Spread.** Per sample point, the median over the other samples of the point-to-triangle
  distance to their meshes. That is A-12's measure, per point.
- **Seen.** Within 2 mm of the partial.
- **Weight.** The spread above this session's own seen-surface median, times area. The board band
  is left out.
- **Targets.** The weighted points are pooled into 2 mm voxels.
- **The scorer.** `recon.next_view` takes the map as an optional input and adds
  `disagreement_visible_weight` and `disagreement_rank` to each candidate. `ranked` and `best` keep
  C-08's meaning, because the agent policy reads `best`'s count.
  - Only the served mesh blocks a ray, as C-08's rule requires.
  - A target faces the camera only when every sample mesh is consistently wound.
- **The hand-off.** `agent_tools.score_next_view` picks the map up beside the served GLB and adds
  `best_by_disagreement`.

**Choices made while planning (owner, 2026-11-11):**
- The weighting is the excess over the session's own noise level, not a fixed threshold.
- It runs inline after completion, not as a separate tool.

**Why the meshes and not the `sample_<k>.ply` clouds** for distances: on s-04's seen surface,
point-to-point between two samples' 20k-point clouds reads 0.48–0.50 mm (measured, three pairs).
That is twice the 0.24 mm point-to-triangle figure. Most of it is the sampling's own spacing, so it
would bury the noise level the weighting rests on.

**Checked on A-12's prepared folders** (M2 Max, CPU; the folders stay in the fixtures staging copy,
not in git):

| Per-pair O, this map / A-12 (mm) | 0.24 / 0.24, 0.24 / 0.24, 0.25 / 0.25 | 0.26 / 0.26, 0.24 / 0.24, 0.20 / 0.20 |

- All six LaS-Comp meshes are consistently wound, so culling was on.
- The folders have no fusion record, so the board cut used A-04's recorded clearance on a level
  plane: 3.0 mm for s-04 and 5.0 mm for s-02.
- The U/O differences from A-12 are a different point sampling: A-12 used 50k fresh points, this
  uses the 20k in `sample_<k>.ply`.

**Next views on s-04.** These used R-32's VGGT run of the same 30 frames as the served mesh. The
A-12 partial lies a median 0.17 mm from it, and the run took 2.7 s.
- **C-08's best:** straight down from 400 mm. It sees 19,646 of 25,485 Unobserved vertices, most
  of them board skin and walls (R-33). It ranks 24th by disagreement.
- **The map's best:** straight down from 250 mm. It sees 118 of the 316 total weight.
- Where the weight sits:
  - 58% is 3–10 mm above the board, where P-29 saw the completions copy the observed shell's base
    flare;
  - 17% is the raised patch above the 30 mm top that P-29 saw change with the seed.

**Known limits** (also in D-066):
- **A guess over seen surface still counts.** That s-04 top patch is one: a photo from above
  re-observes the top but can't stop the model guessing there.
- **The weight's absolute size has no meaning yet.** Nobody has decided what weight a view needs
  to be worth a photo.
- **The phone never sees any of this.** The arrow follows C-07's fixed orbit, and getting either
  ranking to the phone needs a contract field (D-031). No story owns that yet.
