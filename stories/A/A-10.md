## Why this exists

Generative shape completion is never cut (D-017), but nothing names the model beyond the
proposal's "Diffusion-based shape completion, K samples", with "coverage-only guidance" as
the fallback (§9). Its GPU needs and input and output formats are unknown, so A-04 can't be
estimated until one model has actually run on our data.

## Acceptance criteria

- 2–3 diffusion-based shape-completion candidates are tried on a real golden-set segmented
  reconstruction, not only on their own training classes. For each: whether it runs at all
  on the Lightning T4 (16 GB, shared with VGGT) or the M2 Max; its input and output format;
  time per sample; whether two seeds give different surfaces; whether its licence fits a
  public academic repository.
- The choice is recorded as a decision in `docs/decisions.md`. Or, if none fits, the negative
  result is reported and taken to the team, since completion is never cut (D-017) and the
  proposal's fallback is coverage-only guidance.

## Tests

Manual-check exception: this story's output is a measured comparison and a decision, not code.
Spike scripts are throwaway and stay out of the repository; the exact commands and results go in
the notes so the run can be repeated.

## Notes and decisions

- Timeboxed at 4 h. Diffusion completion repositories often need custom CUDA operations; if
  a candidate won't build inside the timebox, that is a result, recorded as such.
- Many candidates are trained on everyday object classes. Mechanical parts on a board are a
  different distribution, which is why the test is on a real golden-set part.
- Shape completion passes the generative test by construction: different seeds give
  different surfaces. The spike checks that it actually does on our data.
