## Why this exists

The proposal (§6.6): "A generative model then samples K plausible completions of the
geometry it did not [see], and different random seeds produce different surfaces. Where
those samples disagree is precisely where the system is guessing." Those samples feed the
disagreement map (A-05), which drives capture guidance and nothing else. Completed surface
"never touches the verdict" (D-005, D-006, issue #157).

## Acceptance criteria

Rewritten 2026-11-03 when the story was split (D-058); the original criteria are kept in the
notes below.

- **The stage.** It runs inside the reconstruction pipeline after R-10, unless
  `PROOFSHAPE_COMPLETION=0`, timed under a `"completion"` entry in `stage_timings_s` (free-form,
  no contract change). It isolates the observed part from the board skin, keeping the part's lower
  walls, samples a partial point cloud from it, picks and crops one capture photo, and writes a
  versioned request for the model runner, a separate process (D-057). With a runner configured in
  `PROOFSHAPE_COMPLETION_RUNNER`, it runs it under a time budget and a hard timeout and ingests up
  to K samples as `artifacts/completion/sample_<k>.ply` in board-frame millimetres, plus a
  `completion.json` (seeds, K, model pins, timings, inputs). Every threshold, K and the budget are
  declared parameters with stated defaults; K and the budget are provisional until A-12 measures
  them. A `prepare` / `ingest` CLI lets the request run on another machine.
- **The verdict mesh is untouched.** Every upstream artifact, `mesh_uncertainty.glb` and
  `sigma_mm.npz` included, is byte-identical with the stage on or off, and the GLB served to the
  client is unchanged. `recon/provenance` gains `LABEL_INFERRED` and a source colour for later
  use; no served file carries them yet.
- **Guidance can't break a reconstruction.** No completion outcome fails a reconstruction. With no
  runner configured, the stage records `unavailable` in `completion.json` and adds no warning
  (D-058). A configured runner that fails, times out or returns invalid output adds one fixed
  warning, and nothing the runner prints reaches a warning or `session.json`.

## Tests

Unit tests written alongside the code (D-029), against a stub runner script run as a real
subprocess:
- isolation on a synthetic mesh: skin out, lower walls kept, unobserved surface out;
- seeded sampling;
- photo choice and crop, including an EXIF rotation;
- strict parsing of the runner's result file and meshes;
- K sample files written, and the seed passed through (same seed gives the same bytes);
- every failure path ending in one warning or none, as above;
- upstream files byte-identical with the stage on or off.

CI has no GPU and LaS-Comp is CUDA-only, so the real model is A-12's. A written check here runs the
stage on s-01…s-05 on the Mac. With no runner it prepares the inputs only, and for each object it
records the isolation counts, the partial cloud's extent and the chosen photo. Golden data never
enters git.

  raises on any label other than Observed or Unobserved, and treats the whole mesh as an
  occluder, where guessed surface must not block a view. Capture's colour mapping, legend

- **From A-10 (D-057), 2026-11-02:** the model is LaS-Comp, pinned there. Facts that change
  this story:
  - **Input and output.** Input is a PLY point cloud plus one photo, normalised to a unit cube and
    mapped back afterwards, so coordinates stay in millimetres. It expects z up, while the board
    frame's z points into the board, so turn it 180° about x first. Output is a mesh in the input's
    coordinates; sample it to points for `sample_<k>.ply`.

    not in the service's Python ≥3.11 interpreter. The heavy CUDA dependencies (spconv, kaolin,
    pytorch3d, xformers) belong in that environment, not `requirements-gpu.txt`. Install LaS-Comp
    at build time: its repository has no LICENSE file.
  - **Setup.** The TRELLIS `pipeline.json` needs a `sparse_structure_encoder` entry (see A-10).
  - **Correction to the note above:** the served model isn't "the part alone" yet. On s-04 it still
    had a thin board skin, 3–5 mm above the board, across the whole area. A-10 isolated the part
    by height and clustering, so this stage needs the same step, or a fix upstream first.
  - `sigma.npz` in the criteria is `sigma_mm.npz` in the code (`recon/uncertainty.py`).
  - The contract wording for `inferred` regions now rides on A-11's D-031 request (D-056), not
    I-07's.

### Built (2026-11-03): what was chosen, and why

- **Two modules.** The stage itself is `recon/completion.py`. The runner boundary is
  `recon/completion_contract.py`: standard library and numpy only, in Python 3.10 syntax, so A-12's
  runner can import it from the repository root. `pip install -e .` refuses Python 3.10, so that is
  the only way in. Tests enforce both.
- **The runner returns meshes, the service samples them.** One seeded, CI-tested sampler serves the
  partial cloud and every sample, and the runner, which CI can't test, stays thin. The result file
  carries no paths: the service names every file from the sample index, so a runner can't point it
  at upstream files. The mesh reader is strict, because `tsdf_fusion.read_mesh_ply` would misread a
  foreign PLY carrying normals as garbage without any error.
- **Part isolation, not A-10's flat 6 mm cut.**
  - The core starts at fusion's own measured cut (`tsdf_fusion.json`'s `board_surface`) plus
    `skin_allowance_mm` 3.5. The cut varies across the golden set, and a fixed 6 mm core picks up
    board skin on s-02.
  - The core's largest spatial cluster is the part.
  - Lower Observed vertices come back only inside a 60° cone under the core. That restores the
    bottom of the walls the 6 mm cut lost, and drops the flat skin around them.
  - A core reaching the pattern's edge is refused as skin, not part.
  - Every number is a declared default with its reason in `CompletionParameters`.
- **Labels from the served mesh,** `mesh_uncertainty.glb`, after R-10's σ downgrade. A vertex
  downgraded for noisy depth isn't trusted surface to complete from either.
- **The photo comes from R-02's board poses, not VGGT's.** They are in full-resolution stored
  pixels, they exist for every backend, and they need no `preprocess_geometry` mapping. The crop
  is taken in stored pixels, as the rest of the pipeline works, and only the crop is turned
  upright by its EXIF orientation. A-10 never exercised that (s-04 is orientation 1); s-02's
  frames are mostly orientation 6.
- **The process.**
  - `subprocess.run` with a timeout, not a process-group kill, because it behaves the same on
    Windows, where one of us runs the tests.
  - The runner's output goes to a 64 KiB log tail only.
  - `GEMINI_API_KEY` and the endpoint token are stripped from the runner's environment.
  - One run at a time (D-057). A second concurrent session skips completion with a warning rather
    than queueing inside the pipeline.
- **`labels_to_colors` fixed on the way.** It started from `np.empty`, so any label other than the
  two known ones got whatever bytes were in memory, with no error. It now raises.
  `colors_to_labels` still refuses `INFERRED_COLOR` on purpose.
- **Mutation-tested by hand.** Six deliberate defects each failed at least one test:
  - secrets no longer stripped;
  - always warning;
  - no cone rule;
  - no frame conversion back;
  - the disable flag ignored;
  - no scale check.
- **Local test runs.** This checkout's `.venv` has its editable install pointing at another
  worktree, so `inspection` didn't resolve here. The suite was run with `PYTHONPATH=inspect`;
  CI's fresh install isn't affected.

### Written check: the stage on the golden set (2026-11-03, M2 Max)

**The run.** `PROOFSHAPE_RECON_DEVICE=mps python scripts/smoke_r14.py
fixtures/data/golden_capture/s-0N --data-dir <scratch>`, with `PYTHONPATH` set to this checkout,
no Gemini key, and no runner.

**What it showed.**
- All five reconstructions passed, with the stage inline.
- Each completion stage took about 0.2 s, ended `unavailable`, and added no warning.
- Nothing was committed: golden data never enters git.

**Against CAD.** Only s-04 (60 × 26 × 30 mm by its bounding box) and s-05 (30 × 30 × 60 mm) have
reference CAD.
- **Heights match.** The partial reaches 30.2 mm on s-04 and 61.3 mm on s-05. The bottom of the
  walls comes back down to fusion's cut, which A-10's 6 mm cut had lost.
- **Footprints match above the base.** Above about 11.5 mm they agree with CAD: s-04 is
  58.7–60.1 × 23 mm (1st–99th percentile; its triangular section narrows with height), and s-05 is
  about 31 × 31 mm.
- **The base carries a skirt; said plainly rather than tuned away.** Below the core height the
  footprint grows: about 72 × 27 mm on s-04 and 34 × 37 mm on s-05, at the 1st–99th percentile.
  - That is about 6 mm at each end of s-04 and 2–3 mm per side of s-05.
  - Most of it is the cone rule letting in up to 4 mm of board skin around the base
    (`skin_allowance_mm` × cot 60° + the 2 mm tolerance).
  - At s-04's ends, some skin also sits just above the core height.
  - Points below the core height, skirt and genuine lower wall together, are 15% of s-04's
    partial and 6% of s-05's.
  - Tightening `min_wall_angle_deg` or `wall_xy_tolerance_mm` would shrink it, at the cost of more
    of the lower wall. That's a call for after A-12 shows whether the model cares, and after
    R-26/R-27 change the skin.
- **s-01…s-03 have no CAD,** so their extents can't be checked against truth. s-03 is a large dome,
  and its core comes within 7 mm of the pattern's edge, 3 mm short of tripping the 4 mm edge guard.

**The photos.** Checked by eye: each `view.png` frames the whole part. s-02 is the EXIF-rotated
case (orientation 6), and its crop is consistent with s-01's view of the same padlock.
