## Why this exists

Proposal §12 item 4: "Robustness. Glossy, dark and thin parts; lighting variants; a hand in
frame; wrong part; photo of a screen; replayed session against a used code." G-02's own notes
already carve this exact scope out of the specimen batch on purpose: "Surface finish (glossy,
dark, thin-walled) is G-11's robustness set, because finish breaks reconstruction rather than
measuring its noise." A-01 already consumes part of this mechanism and names it directly: "Use
G-11's robustness set (screen photo, wrong part) for the manual check once it exists. No
accuracy, cost or latency is claimed from it." Today those two fixtures exist only as owner-local
files (`fixtures/data/vlm_checks/screen_photo.jpeg`, `wrong_part.jpeg`) used informally in A-01's
own manual check — this story is what formalizes and commits a reusable set, for A-01 and (once
it exists) A-02's veto testing both to share.

## Acceptance criteria

- Printed fixture set, on G-01's recorded printer and settings: at least one glossy-finish part,
  one dark-coloured part, one thin-walled part — chosen within G-02's convex-prism constraint
  where possible, or recorded as a deliberate exception where finish itself requires breaking it.
- A screen-photo fixture and a wrong-part fixture, captured as still images in the same
  `fixtures/` convention G-08 specifies (G-08 itself hasn't run yet; this story follows its own
  story's convention, not a precedent it's already set), formalizing (not duplicating) A-01's
  existing owner-local files, so A-01's manual check and any later A-02 veto testing have a
  shared, committed reference.
- Each fixture run through the normal reconstruction pipeline where applicable, with the failure
  mode recorded as a written result (does reconstruction degrade, does the VLM catch it) — not a
  pass/fail tolerance, since D-003 forbids an unmeasured tolerance claim.
- The written record cross-references A-01 directly, and (once it exists) A-02, so the
  robustness set's role as shared fixtures across stories is explicit rather than each owning a
  private copy.

## Tests

Written-check exception, same pattern as G-01/G-02/G-08: physical fixtures and captures are a
written record against these acceptance criteria, not a unit test. Any code written to classify
a failure mode gets its own tests alongside it.

### The printed parts (designed 2026-11-16, @TabeenRaoof, before printing)

**One shape, so only the finish or the wall changes.** All three parts are built on G-02's pentagon
specimen, `g02_04_pentagon_r22x25mm.stl` (a regular pentagon of circumradius 22 mm, 25 mm tall).
Its grey print, already captured for G-04 (G-08), is the control for every part here. A different
shape per part would mix a shape effect into each finish's result.

`g02_04` is the specimen to build on because of G-04's result (D-073). It's one of the five
specimens whose height G-04 trusts. G-04's pose search went visibly wrong on the square
(`g02_01`) and the triangle (`g02_03`), but not on the pentagon (R-37).

| Part | File | Filament | What it tests |
|---|---|---|---|
| Glossy | `g02_04_pentagon_r22x25mm.stl`, unchanged | the shiniest the lab has (a silk PLA if it has one; otherwise its shinier PLA Basic, which was gold of the original blue and gold) | specular highlights that move between frames |
| Dark | `g02_04_pentagon_r22x25mm.stl`, unchanged | black Bambu PLA Basic, ordered through the lab if it isn't in stock | low texture and contrast against the board |
| Thin-walled | `g11_01_thin_wall_pentagon_r22x25mm.stl`, new | grey Bambu PLA Basic, the control's own spool | walls 1.2 mm thick, about 1/20 of their 25 mm height |

**The thin-walled part** is written by `scripts/make_g11_thin_wall.py` and checked by
`tests/test_make_g11_thin_wall.py`. It is the pentagon hollowed into an open-top tray: the same
outer footprint, position and 25 mm height, with walls 1.2 mm thick perpendicular to each face, on
a 2.0 mm floor.
- **1.2 mm** is three 0.4 mm nozzle widths, so the walls print solid with G-01's settings.
- **The 2.0 mm floor** sits within segmentation's board-clearance cut
  (`DEFAULT_MIN_BOARD_CLEARANCE_MM`, 2.0 mm), so only the walls are left for reconstruction to
  keep.
- **It is a deliberate exception to G-02's convex-prism constraint.** The cavity floor is a third
  z level, so `prism_from_cad` refuses it, and a test checks that refusal. Its result is
  therefore recorded from the reconstruction itself, not from the size check: does each wall
  come back with both faces, as one merged slab, or with holes?

**Printing.** Use the A1 mini and G-01's slicer settings (0.20 mm Standard @BBL A1M, 2 wall loops,
15% grid infill). For each part, record:
- the filament and colour;
- the filament preset, if not Bambu PLA Basic (a silk PLA has its own preset, the one deliberate
  settings change);
- the `.3mf`.

Label each part with its name ("G11 glossy", "G11 dark", "G11 thin") on the base, not on a face
the camera sees. Measure the height and one wall's thickness with calipers, so each result has a
printed size to compare against.

### Through the reconstruction pipeline (2026-11-18, criterion 3)

**How it was run.** The four photo sets went through the reconstruction service in-process, on
`main` at `d205149`, on the M2 Max (`PROOFSHAPE_RECON_DEVICE=mps`, VGGT). Each run created an
order carrying the CAD, uploaded the Drive frames, finished, and waited for the result: the same
path as the phone, minus the network. The grey `g02_04` (G-08's 27 frames) ran alongside as the
control. Each part has one run. The outputs are outside git, in `proofshape-data/g11-runs/`.
- The order spec was empty, so `getVerdict` refused every session ("no feature in this spec was
  judged"). The verdict's own inputs, registration and deviation, still ran on the order's CAD.
- No `GEMINI_API_KEY` was set, so the VLM didn't run. Whether it catches these finishes is
  still open.
- Heights are `cad_size_check` on the raw point cloud, against the `g02_04` CAD, compared with
  the caliper readings above. The tray was measured against `g02_04` too: its outer shape is the
  same, and its own CAD is refused by `prism_from_cad` (three z levels), as designed. Edges aren't
  reported, because D-073 found that measure isn't trustworthy.

| | Control (grey) | Glossy | Dark | Thin tray |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Frames | 27 | 30 | 30 | 29 |
| Reconstruction, s | 36.6 | 49.0 | 42.6 | 39.0 |
| Finish to result, s | 48.7 | 59.3 | 53.6 | 48.1 |
| Status, warnings | complete, none | complete, none | complete, none | complete, none |
| Observed | 73% | 66% | 69% | 78% |
| Board scale (R-23) | 0.9976 | 0.9967 | 0.9966 | 0.9963 |
| Height, cloud, mm | 25.81 | 24.44 | 23.28 | 21.19 |

| Difference, mm | +0.85 | −0.54 | −1.65 | −3.73 |
| Wall point spread, mm (10–90%, per edge) | 4.0–5.1 | 4.5–5.8 | 5.2–7.4 | 5.9–6.8 |
| Served model's top (99th percentile), mm | 25.5 | 25.6 | 24.9 | 19.9 |
| Registration (I-05) | orientation ambiguous | orientation ambiguous | orientation ambiguous | **failed** |

- **The control reproduces G-04.** D-073 records board scale 0.9976 and a +0.86 mm height error
  for this specimen. This run gives 0.9976 and +0.85 mm, so the service path on current `main`
  measures the part as G-04 did.
- **Glossy: no visible degradation.** The height is 0.54 mm short, within σ_floor (0.86 mm,
  D-073) of the caliper. The wall spread is a little wider than the control's. The silver silk's
  highlights didn't break the board pose, the depth or the fusion.
- **Dark: degraded, not broken.** The cloud's height reads 1.65 mm short, about twice σ_floor,
  and its wall spread is the widest of the solid parts, up to 7.4 mm. The served model still has
  a flat top at 24.9 mm. So the black PLA's low texture shows up as noisier walls and a top face
  that sits lower in the cloud, not as a failure. From one run, the finish can't be separated
  from run-to-run scatter.
- **Thin tray: the 1.1 mm wall is below what fusion resolves.**
  - The open top stays open: the cloud has 319 points more than 4 mm inside the walls above
    24 mm, against 19,541 for the control's top face.
  - Each wall comes back as one slab, never with two faces. Mid-height points spread about 6 mm
    across a wall that is 1.1 mm thick, the same spread as the solid control's single face.
  - The served model is worse than the cloud. Fusion runs on 2.0 mm voxels with an 8 mm
    truncation, so a 1.1 mm wall is thinner than one voxel. The model's walls are rounded ridges
    about 5 mm thick that stop near 20 mm, about 5 mm under the printed top. Its floor shows at
    about 7 mm, against the CAD's 2.0 mm.
  - The height in the table (21.19 mm) is the size check's top-face density peak landing on a
    rim only 1.1 mm wide. It isn't a measurement of the tray. The cloud's wall points reach
    24.4 mm at the 95th percentile.
  - Registration failed against the tray's own CAD and against the solid `g02_04`: "No candidate
    could be refined", with 0 Observed correspondences within 3.14 mm for every one of its nine
    candidates. The cause wasn't found here. The service records it as
    `registration_or_deviation_failed`, so `getVerdict` can only Rescan this part.
- **Orientation ambiguous on every solid pentagon, the control included.** A regular pentagon
  looks the same after each 72° turn: the control's best and runner-up poses score the same
  (fitness 0.545, RMSE 1.33 mm against 1.33 mm). I-05 flags that correctly. It's a property of
  the shape chosen for this set, not of the finish. So no part here can reach Pass through
  I-07's rule, whatever its finish.
