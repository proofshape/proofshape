## Why this exists

The verdict has to be shown catching a defect of known size. M4's gate is "planted 4 mm
hole shift → Fail", and the proposal's planted-defect test uses "shifted holes and missing
or excess material at 2, 4 and 8 mm" (§12). Without printed defects of known size there is
nothing to plant.

## Acceptance criteria

- One base shape with a hole, its defect-free control, and variants at 2, 4 and 8 mm for a
  shifted hole, missing material and excess material, designed before printing, as ASCII STL
  in `fixtures/reference_cad/`. If time runs short, the shifted hole comes first: M4 needs it.
- Every part is printed on G-01's recorded printer and settings and labelled: the same Bambu Lab
  A1 mini model (ideally the same unit), filament and slicer profile as G-01's cubes and G-02's
  specimens. **G-05 can't be Done until G-01 has recorded those settings** (G-01's second
  criterion); G-01's cube readings aren't needed.
- The actual size of each printed defect is calipered into `fixtures/caliper_g-05.json`,
  since the print, not the CAD, is truth.

## Tests

Written check for the parts. The readings file is checked as the Ground truth section's *Where
readings go* says.

## Notes and decisions

- The base can't be a G-02 prism: a hole fails `prism_from_cad`'s convex-hull check, and a
  pocket or bump adds a third z level. That's fine here, because this story only makes the
  parts. Detecting the defects is I-06/I-07's job, and no existing code measures them.
- Photographing the ladder is G-09.

- **2026-11-09: no longer blocked on G-01 as a whole**, the same change as G-02's (#267).
  - The design half has nothing to wait for: the base, the control and the 2 / 4 / 8 mm
    variants as STL.
  - The printing needs only G-01's printer and settings, which G-01 records at its first lab
    print (Tuesday 2026-11-10). The parts can print on that visit, on the same unit.
  - So the dependency moved from the `Depends on` line into the second criterion, where it
    still decides when G-05 is Done.
