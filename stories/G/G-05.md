## Why this exists

The verdict has to be shown catching a defect of known size. M4's gate is "planted 4 mm
hole shift → Fail", and the proposal's planted-defect test uses "shifted holes and missing
or excess material at 2, 4 and 8 mm" (§12). Without printed defects of known size there is
nothing to plant.

**Cut to two parts on 2026-11-12 (D-069).** With three weeks to M6, only the two parts X-03
needs are printed: the control `g05_00` and M4's 4 mm hole shift `g05_02`. The full ladder
stays designed and committed, but the other eight parts are out of this story and unprinted.

## Acceptance criteria

- One base shape with a hole, its defect-free control, and variants at 2, 4 and 8 mm for a
  shifted hole, missing material and excess material, designed before printing, as ASCII STL
  in `fixtures/reference_cad/`. **Met on 2026-11-09 (#276).** Of the ten, only `g05_00` and
  `g05_02` are printed (D-069).

  **The design (written 2026-11-09, before printing):** one base and nine variants, each with
  exactly one defect. Millimetres, z up, base at z = 0; a rectangle is its x range × y range.
  `scripts/make_g05_defect_ladder.py` writes every file, and a test checks the committed files
  are exactly its output.

  | Element | Geometry | Why (notes below) |
  |---|---|---|
  | Base | 50 × 32 × 20 plate, a 16 × 11 notch cut from one corner ([34,50] × [21,32]) | The notch fixes the orientation without the hole |
  | Hole | 12 × 12 square, through, at [6,18] × [10,22] | Flat walls for the caliper; the same form as I-04's and I-05's synthetic plate |
  | Defect site | 10 × 10 at [35,45] × [5,15], on the top face | At least 5 from every edge, the notch, and the hole at its largest shift |

  | File | Part ID | Defect | Volume |

  These are planted-defect parts for M4 and the proposal's §12 test, not σ_floor specimens, and
  deliberately not prisms. **Once a file is printed it is frozen:** a change becomes a new file,
  never an edit to a printed one. The eight unprinted files aren't frozen, but they stay exactly
  as the generator writes them, so a later story can print them with no new design work.
- **`g05_00` and `g05_02` are printed** on G-01's recorded printer and settings and labelled: the

  print, not the CAD, is truth. The printed shift is the 4 mm part's hole centre minus the
  control's.

## Tests

`tests/test_make_g05_defect_ladder.py`, for the design:
- the ten committed files are exactly what the generator writes, and `--check` catches a change;
- each loads in mm with its base at z = 0 and its designed extent, is a closed, outward-wound
  solid of its designed volume, has unit facet normals that agree with its winding, and is pinned
  as not an R-22 prism;
- a height probe straight down at every 1 mm cell finds each part differs from the plain plate
  only by its own defect;
- G-02's board-corner check, with the bounding box taken from the vertices;
- each part registers to its true pose against the control with no orientation ambiguity, and
  the notch keeps the hole-missing case unambiguous where a plain plate isn't.

Then a written check for the two printed parts, and the readings file is checked as the Ground truth
section's *Where readings go* says.

## Notes and decisions

- The base can't be a G-02 prism: a hole fails `prism_from_cad`'s convex-hull check, and a
  pocket or bump adds a third z level. That's fine here, because this story only makes the
  parts. Detecting the defects is I-06/I-07's job, and no existing code measures them.
- Photographing the ladder is G-09.

- **2026-11-09: no longer blocked on G-01 as a whole**, the same change as G-02's (#267).
  - The design half has nothing to wait for: the base, the control and the 2 / 4 / 8 mm
    variants as STL.
  - The printing needs only G-01's printer and settings, which G-01 records at its first lab
    print (Tuesday 2026-11-10). The parts can print on that visit, on the same unit.
  - So the dependency moved from the `Depends on` line into the second criterion, where it
    still decides when G-05 is Done.

- **2026-11-09: the design, and why.** @TabeenRaoof chose the top-face pocket and bump, and the
  50 × 32 × 20 mm L-plate.
  - **The notch.** On a plate whose only asymmetry is its hole, a moved or missing hole lets the
    180° flip fit nearly as well. I-05 then flags the session ambiguous (D-061), and the verdict
    is Rescan, not Fail: D-008's trap. I-05's own synthetic 60 × 40 plate falls into it with its
    hole missing. The notch fixes the orientation on its own.
  - **20 mm tall.** R-21's layer search histograms everything within
    `DEFAULT_MAX_BOARD_LAYER_MM` = 10 mm of the board, the part included, so a large flat top
    lower than that is cut away (R-26, #180). D-060 says the search can't yet tell such a top from
    the misalignment artifact. The top is at 20 mm, and every pocket floor is at 12 mm or more.
    The plane fit's own band, `DEFAULT_BOARD_SEARCH_BAND_MM` = 15, does take the 8 mm pocket's
    floor in as a candidate, and its sigma clipping removes it. The bottom cut is max(2 mm, the
    layer's top), which reached 4–6 mm in R-17: below every defect.
  - **Defects on the top, not the walls.** R-23 measured heights at 0.990 and 1.000 of CAD, but
    edges from 0.840 to 1.002, with s-04's 26 mm edge about 4 mm short. Its long wall also leans
    about 3 mm (R-32). A 2 mm wall defect would sit inside that error.
  - **The hole moves toward the centre.** Moving it away was worse, and so was an 18 × 12 notch
    (measured below).
- **Measured before printing (synthetic, 2026-11-09).**
  - Setup:
    - I-01 scans of each part: σ 0.3 mm and a 20% Unobserved patch, both declared defaults, not
      measured noise. The board-contact face was also hidden, as in a real scan.
    - Registered against the control with I-03 and I-05 at the test settings (700 samples, 36
      yaws, 6 candidates, 3,000 RANSAC iterations), not production's (1,600 / 72 / 8 / 20,000).
  - None of the ten parts was flagged over seeds 0–7, and the best pose was always the true one,
    within 0.2° and 0.06 mm.
  - Lowest runner-up/best ratio per part:

    | Part | Lowest ratio |
    |---|---|
    | Control | 2.47 |
    | Shift 2 mm | 1.79 |
    | Shift 4 mm | 1.36 |
    | Shift 8 mm | **1.22** (seed 6, the narrowest margin against D-061's 1.20) |
    | Pockets | 2.06 or more |
    | Bumps | 1.77 or more |

  - With the hole missing, the notched plate isn't flagged (1.54 or more). The same plate without
    its notch is flagged on every seed (0.96–1.10).
  - Rejected:
    - the hole moving away from the centre: the 4 mm shift was flagged 2 of 24 times, with one
      wrong pick;
    - an 18 × 12 notch: the 8 mm shift was flagged 1 of 24 times, at 0.47.
  - With the contact face left visible, the 2 mm shift was flagged once in 24 (1.16). Its
    runner-up is a near-duplicate 3° and 1.2 mm from the best, which I-05's 2° merge keeps
    separate. That's an I-05 question, not a G-05 one.
- **Fusion from ideal depth (2026-11-09).**
  - Setup: 48 views at 30–75° elevation, board pixels masked as `segment_part` does, then R-07's
    `fuse_tsdf` at 2 mm voxels and 8 mm truncation.
  - The 12 mm hole stays open at 4, 10 and 17 mm up. On the 4 mm part its centre moves exactly
    4.0 mm. A 16 mm hole gained nothing.
  - Every top reads about 1 mm high. Relative to it, the pockets read 1.8 / 3.2 / 8.0 mm and the
    bumps 2.0 / 8.0 mm.
  - Real VGGT depth is untested until G-09. Judge a shift from the hole's centre, where a
    symmetric wall bias cancels.
- **No σ_floor or k here.** The 2 mm rungs may correctly come back Unverifiable (proposal
  §12.3). That would be a finding, not a fault in the parts.
- **WIP.** This is @TabeenRaoof's third active story, with G-01 and A-11 (#273). It was taken so
  the parts could print at G-01's first lab visit.
- **Print plan** (two parts since D-069).
  - Slice with G-01's settings (Bambu Studio's stock 0.20 mm Standard, Bambu PLA Basic), on the
    same A1 mini unit and in the same colour as G-01's cubes. A second A1 mini unit is allowed;
    record which.
  - Print `g05_00` and `g05_02` as one job, both in the same rotation, with no supports and
    without letting Arrange rotate them. Record which part axis ran along the printer's X.
  - Record the commit and each sliced file's `shasum -a 256` here, plus the Bambu Studio version.
    Keep the `.3mf` in Drive, not git.
  - Take the parts off one at a time in slicer order, writing each part ID in pen on its
    **bottom** face, never on the inspected top. Confirm each ID with one caliper reading of the
    left web: 6 mm on the control, 10 mm on the 4 mm shift.
- **Measurement plan (for the printing PR).** G-06's caliper, 10 placements per feature, read at
  mid-height. On each of the two parts:
  - the left web a, from the x = 0 face to the hole;
  - the right web b, from the hole to the x = 50 face, at y ≈ 13–16;
  - the hole width w, with the inside jaws.
  - The hole's centre is a + w/2, and its shift is the difference from the printed control's.
  - That's 6 features and 60 readings. They need two new `kind`s (for example `wall` and
    `hole_width`) in `tests/test_caliper_files.py`'s `KINDS` and `fixtures/README.md`'s tables,
    changed together in that PR. The pockets' `depth` and the bumps' `step` aren't needed now.
