## Why this exists

The verdict's Pass and Fail rules scale σ by a factor `k` (Pass needs "σ ≤ tolerance / k"
in the proposal's verdict table), and `k` "is a planning prior of 3; the M3 measurement
sets it". Nothing has yet checked that R-10's σ predicts real error at all.

## Acceptance criteria

- Per-vertex σ (R-10, on the R-07 to R-10 mesh) is plotted against per-vertex measured error
  on the calipered specimens, for the board reference.
- `k` is chosen from that curve and recorded in `docs/decisions.md` with the curve. σ_floor
  is G-04's, not this story's.
- If σ doesn't predict error, that is reported as the result rather than worked around.

## Tests

The per-vertex distance code is new and has tests written alongside it. The curve and the choice
of `k` are a written record.
