## Why this exists

Every accuracy figure the project has comes from the golden and G-08 photographs. Those are
full-resolution photos taken with the iPhone's camera app, each carrying EXIF. The expo
(Sat Dec 5) captures through the app instead:
- 480 × 640 frames with no EXIF, so the focal length is estimated from the board (R-36);
- the app's own blur gate (C-25), and its auto-capture (C-27);
- a hall nobody on the team has seen.

P-41's accuracy review (`docs/accuracy-review-2026-11-20.md`) measured the app path on three
saved s-04 sessions only, and lists "one capture per part, one phone" among its limits. R-36's
home pre-check read s-04's height and edges with `cad_size_check` on one session. That is
comparable, but it is one part, by hand, against nominal CAD.

So nobody knows whether a part read through the app reads the way its photographs do, or how
much that changes in another room and light. This story measures it, on `main`, before the code
freeze (Dec 4), so that any gap it finds can still go to a story.

## Acceptance criteria

- **Each of the ten calipered parts is captured once through the app on `main`,** with the iPhone
  C-18 tested. The parts are s-04, s-05, `g05_00`, `g05_02` and `g02_01` to `g02_06`.
  - Each session's order carries that part's own CAD.
  - Each session runs through `scripts/measure_part_accuracy.py run <session>/frames --cad <CAD>`
    on the same commit as a fresh run of the part's photographs.
  - The record has one row per part, photographs against app:
    - the wall and top-face figures and the worst reading;
    - the stray and unmeasured shares;
    - the smallest tolerance that Passes, and the ambiguity flag;
    - whether the CAD pose is the same, by I-15's `poses_are_same`;
    - the verdict the service returns for the order.
- **At least four parts are captured again in a second room, under different light,** and
  compared with the first set the same way. The four are the parts the expo demo will show, and
  at least `g05_00` and `g05_02`. The record describes the room and the light in words (window or
  ceiling, daylight or lamps, glare on the board). It's the nearest thing to the expo hall that
  can be rehearsed. R-36 owns the hall's network and logistics.
- **Every difference that changes a verdict, a pose or the ambiguity flag is named:**
  - between the photographs and the app, or between the two rooms;
  - with its cause where it can be found, or "unknown";
  - and routed to I-17 if it's that story's cause, to a new issue otherwise.

  This story sets no threshold and changes no constant (D-003).

## Tests

## Notes and decisions

- **Written by P-42 (2026-11-24)** after a ten-part re-run of P-41's script on `main` at
  `70b2773`. That run reproduced I-15's own criterion-4 record, using the photographs only.
- **Sessions stay out of git and out of any public archive.** They live with the other sessions
  outside the repository. The people who take them are team members, so G-14's consent question
  doesn't arise. Anyone else's phone or hands in a frame does raise it.
- **s-04 is a known limit.** The golden s-04 lies on its other triangular face (I-16, #441), so
  its pose is neither a pass nor a failure. If the app capture puts it on the face its CAD rests
  on, the record says so, because that is new evidence for I-16.
- **Expected effects to check, not assume:**
  - fewer pixels per millimetre at 480 × 640;
  - no EXIF focal length;
  - the blur gate's threshold of 200 (C-25).

  P-41 found phone sessions start closer on the board (1.1–1.3 mm after R-30). That was one part,
  so it isn't a prediction for the rest.
