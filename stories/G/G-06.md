## Why this exists

Three open questions turn on caliper readings nobody has taken:

- R-22 compared s-04 and s-05 with their CAD, "not caliper readings of the printed parts
  … one caliper reading per part would remove the doubt".
- R-23 (D-051, on #152) anchors metric scale on the printed board's nominal 20 mm squares,
  but nobody has measured that sheet, and a printer can scale a page silently (D-007).
- Only one caliper reading exists anywhere: s-04's 60 mm leg (PR #35).

## Acceptance criteria

- Every s-04 footprint edge and its height, all four s-05 walls and its height, and at least
  three squares of the F-06 board sheet plus one multi-square span are measured with the
  protocol in the Ground truth section, into `fixtures/caliper_g-06.json`. For s-05's square
  footprint, which way each wall faced on the board is recorded.
- `tests/test_caliper_files.py` checks every `fixtures/caliper_*.json` against the format in
  `fixtures/README.md`.
- The notes below record s-04's and s-05's measured-minus-CAD differences, and the board's
  measured pitch against 20 mm, as results.
