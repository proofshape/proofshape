### Method (written 2026-10-28, before any reading was taken)

The Ground truth section's protocol, made specific to these three objects. Readings go into
`fixtures/caliper_g-06.json`, one block per feature in the order below, and
`tests/test_caliper_files.py` checks them.

**Instrument.**
- The digital caliper used for PR #35, at 0.01 mm resolution.
- Its accuracy is the maker's stated figure, recorded in the file, or "no accuracy spec" (`null`)
  if it states none. Resolution is not accuracy.
- Zeroed with the jaws closed before each object.

**One reading.**
- Open the jaws, seat the caliper on the feature, and close gently to contact.
- Record the display exactly as shown, then take the caliper off before the next one.
- Ten such placements per feature. No reading is rounded, retaken or dropped.
- The file gives the mean, min, max and sample standard deviation to four decimals.

**s-04** (triangular prism, CAD height 30 mm).
- The three footprint edges, read corner to corner at about mid-height (15 mm). That is inside
  `measure_prism`'s 0.3–0.7 h band and away from the first-layer flare.
- In CAD millimetres, the edges are:
  - (0, 0)–(60, 0): 60 mm;
  - (60, 0)–(59.575, 25.9965): 26 mm;
  - (0, 0)–(59.575, 25.9965): 65 mm.
- On the part, the 60 mm and 26 mm sides meet at the near-square corner (about 89° in the CAD),
  and the 65 mm side is opposite it.
- Then the height, top face to base, with the part off the board.

**s-05** (rectangular prism, 30 × 30 footprint, CAD height 60 mm).
- Its four walls are taped 1 to 4, anticlockwise seen from above. That matches the CAD's own
  order:
  - wall 1, (0, 0)→(30, 0);
  - wall 2, (30, 0)→(30, 30);
  - wall 3, (30, 30)→(0, 30);
  - wall 4, (0, 30)→(0, 0).
- Each wall's length is read across the parallel faces at its two ends, at about mid-height
  (30 mm), with the caliper close to that wall. Walls 1 and 3 share a pair of end faces, as do 2
  and 4, so any difference between them is a real taper, not a slip.
- The height is read first, top face to base. The tape tabs then go on the top face, one beside
  each wall, so no jaw ever closes on tape.
- **Which way each wall faced.** The four walls look alike in photos, so this is recoverable
  only from a mark on the block (a chip, or worn paint) that can also be found in the
  golden-capture photos.
  - If there is one, record each wall's direction with s-05 standing as it did in the golden
    capture. The reference is the printed caption line under the pattern ("www.calib.io | 6x8 |
    …"): "faced the caption edge", "the far edge", "the left edge" or "the right edge", left and
    right as the caption reads.
  - If there isn't, each wall records "not recoverable from the golden-capture photos: the walls
    look alike". G-04 then compares s-05's walls as a set, as R-22 already does.
  - No new photographs are taken for this. A capture with known orientation would be a new
    fixture set, outside this story.

**The board** (the F-06 sheet used for the golden capture, flat on a hard surface, with its
printed caption line nearest you and reading normally).
- **Corners.** The board is 8 squares across and 6 down. A *corner* is a point where two black
  squares touch at their tips, so each grid line between two rows has 7 of them.
- **Counting.** Rows are counted from the far edge (opposite the caption), and corners from the
  left.
- **Reading.** The caliper lies flat on the paper, with the jaw tips lined up on two corners by
  eye.
- **Three single squares**, spread across the sheet so uneven page scaling would show:
  - top-left: from the 1st to the 2nd corner on the line between rows 1 and 2;
  - centre: from the 4th to the 5th corner on the line between rows 3 and 4;
  - bottom-right: from the 6th to the 7th corner on the line between rows 5 and 6.
- **One six-square span:** from the 1st to the 7th corner on the line between rows 3 and 4.
- Nominal sizes are D-034's 20 mm squares: 20 mm per square, 120 mm for the span.
- Lining jaw tips up on print is a coarser placement than closing jaws on a solid edge. Its
  effect shows in the spread of the ten readings, which is reported as measured.

**What this does not do.** No tolerance and no pass or fail (D-003), and no comparison with
reconstructions, which is G-04's job. The results, added after measuring, report measured minus
CAD for s-04 and s-05, and the board's measured pitch against 20 mm, as measured, whichever way
they come out.

### Deviations from the method (recorded 2026-10-28, after measuring)

- **Resolution.** The caliper used displays one decimal, so its resolution is 0.1 mm, not the
  0.01 mm the method assumed from PR #35 (whose readings had two decimals). Its stated accuracy
  is ±0.02 mm. That is finer than its display step, so here the 0.1 mm step limits each reading,
  not the stated accuracy.
- **s-05 as two widths.** s-05 was read as two widths: one with the jaws on sides 1 and 3, one
  with them on sides 2 and 4. The method asked for four sets of ten, one held near each wall.
  - The two widths give the lengths of all four walls. The first is the length of walls 2 and 4,
    the second the length of walls 1 and 3.
  - What isn't measured is whether a width differs near one wall from the other (taper).
  - The file records each width once, under one CAD edge of the pair, and says so in its
    `where`.
- **Two typing fixes, removed at the owner's word.** A stray "65" before the ten readings of
  s-04's longest side, and an eleventh value at the end of the sides-2-and-4 list.
- **s-05's walls on the board.** Side 1 faced the far edge (opposite the caption), side 2 the
  left, side 3 the caption edge, side 4 the right. That is s-05 as it stood in the golden capture,
  per the owner.
- **Board spans across the whole pattern.** The board's spans were read across the whole pattern,
  edge to edge, in both directions: 8 squares wide, 160 mm; 6 squares high, 120 mm. The method
  asked for six squares corner to corner along line 3. Two directions show whether the page
  scaled differently across and down, which a single span can't. Edge-to-edge readings include
  any ink spread at the outer edges. Each span's first reading, sent before the rest, is one of
  its ten.
- **Three squares, edge to edge in both directions (2026-10-29).** Three black squares, circled
  in the owner's photo, were read edge to edge across each square, ten placements left to right
  and ten far edge to caption. That makes six items, where the method asked for three
  corner-to-corner readings along lines 1, 3 and 5.
  - The squares are row 1 column 2 (top-left), row 3 column 4 (centre) and row 5 column 6
    (bottom-right), with rows from the far edge and columns from the left.
  - Reading both directions is what shows the page's two different scalings.

### Results (measured 2026-10-28 and 2026-10-29)

Measured with the method and deviations above, each a mean of ten placements, from
`fixtures/caliper_g-06.json`. These are raw comparisons, with no tolerance (D-003).

| Feature (CAD edge or kind) | CAD (mm) | Measured mean (mm) | Range (mm) | Measured − CAD (mm) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| s-04 (0, 0)–(60, 0) | 60.0 | 60.13 | 60.1–60.2 | +0.13 (+0.2%) |
| s-04 (60, 0)–(59.575, 25.9965) | 26.0 | 26.36 | 26.3–26.4 | +0.36 (+1.4%) |
| s-04 (0, 0)–(59.575, 25.9965) | 65.0 | 65.41 | 65.3–65.5 | +0.41 (+0.6%) |
| s-04 height | 30.0 | 30.23 | 30.2–30.3 | +0.23 (+0.8%) |
| s-05 width, sides 1–3 (walls 2 and 4) | 30.0 | 29.97 | 29.9–30.0 | −0.03 (−0.1%) |
| s-05 width, sides 2–4 (walls 1 and 3) | 30.0 | 29.98 | 29.8–30.1 | −0.02 (−0.1%) |
| s-05 height | 60.0 | 60.23 | 60.2–60.3 | +0.23 (+0.4%) |

**What the s-04 and s-05 numbers show.**
- s-04 is slightly larger than its CAD on every feature: +0.13 to +0.41 mm, the most on its
  short side (+1.4%).
- s-05's widths match its CAD to within 0.03 mm, and its height is 0.23 mm over.
- That answers R-22's question of whether the parts themselves differ from the CAD. They do,
  but by under 0.5 mm. That is far less than the 15–19% shortfall R-22 found on s-05's
  reconstructed walls, which therefore comes from the reconstruction, not the part. G-04
  repeats the comparison against reconstructions using these readings as the truth.
- s-04's 60 mm side reads 0.12 mm above PR #35's mean for the same edge (60.011 mm, read at
  0.01 mm). That is a little over one display step here.

**The board's pitch,** against D-034's 20 mm squares. "Across" is left to right as the caption
reads; "down" is far edge to caption edge.

| Feature | Nominal (mm) | Measured mean (mm) | Range (mm) | Measured − nominal (mm) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Whole pattern across, 8 squares | 160.0 | 160.16 | 160.1–160.2 | +0.16 (+0.10%) |
| Whole pattern down, 6 squares | 120.0 | 120.55 | 120.5–120.6 | +0.55 (+0.46%) |
| Top-left square (row 1, col 2), across | 20.0 | 20.03 | 20.0–20.1 | +0.03 (+0.15%) |
| Top-left square, down | 20.0 | 20.08 | 20.0–20.1 | +0.08 (+0.40%) |
| Centre square (row 3, col 4), across | 20.0 | 20.01 | 20.0–20.1 | +0.01 (+0.05%) |
| Centre square, down | 20.0 | 20.07 | 20.0–20.1 | +0.07 (+0.35%) |
| Bottom-right square (row 5, col 6), across | 20.0 | 20.04 | 20.0–20.1 | +0.04 (+0.20%) |
| Bottom-right square, down | 20.0 | 20.07 | 20.0–20.1 | +0.07 (+0.35%) |

**What the board numbers show.**
- **The sheet is slightly larger than nominal, and not equally in both directions.** It is
  about 0.1% larger across (160.16 mm over 8 squares) and about 0.45% larger down (120.55 mm
  over 6). The three squares agree: +0.05% to +0.20% across, +0.35% to +0.40% down.
- **Effect on reconstructions.** Scaling a reconstruction to the nominal 20 mm makes it read
  that much small: about 0.1% across the board and 0.45% down it. One scale factor can't describe
  both, and R-23's similarity fit uses one. On a 30 mm part, the difference between the two
  directions is about 0.1 mm. G-04 is to use these measured pitches, not 20 mm, as its second
  criterion already says.
- **Not ink.** The squares and spans were read edge to edge, so they include any ink spread at
  the edges. Ink spread would add about the same amount in both directions, so it doesn't explain
  the difference between them.
- **Left versus right.** On the down span, the owner saw 120.5 mostly near the right side of the
  board and 120.6 mostly near the left. At one display step, that hints the page is slightly
  taller on the left; it doesn't measure it.
- **The survey.** All 24 black squares were read once per side, a few twice, and every survey
  reading showed 20.0 mm. The ten-placement readings on three of those squares show 20.1 on a
  share of placements, mostly down the page. A difference this small sits at the display's
  limit (20.0 shows for anything from 19.95 to 20.05 mm), so one reading per square couldn't
  show it.
