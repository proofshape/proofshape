## Why this exists

σ_floor has to be measured on parts whose true size is known, and the proposal asks for
5–8 printed parts (§12). The only two parts with CAD today, s-04 and s-05, can't separate
the method's noise from one part's quirks.

## Acceptance criteria

- 5–8 specimen CAD files, ASCII STL, are in `fixtures/reference_cad/`, spanning a range of
  hand-sized footprints and heights (D-003) that is written below before printing.

  **The size range (written 2026-11-08, corrected 2026-11-09 — review on #269, @TabeenRaoof):**
  six specimens, chosen for shape and size variety the golden set doesn't have (s-04 is one
  scalene triangle, s-05 one square-ish box).

  | File | Shape | Footprint | Bounding box (x × y) | Height |
  |---|---|---|---|---|
  | `g02_01_square_20x20x15mm.stl` | square | 20 × 20 mm | 20.0 × 20.0 mm | 15 mm |
  | `g02_02_rect_50x20x25mm.stl` | rectangle | 50 × 20 mm | 50.0 × 20.0 mm | 25 mm |
  | `g02_03_right_triangle_40x30x20mm.stl` | right triangle | legs 40 × 30 mm | 40.0 × 30.0 mm | 20 mm |
  | `g02_04_pentagon_r22x25mm.stl` | regular pentagon | circumradius 22 mm | 41.8 × 39.8 mm | 25 mm |
  | `g02_05_hexagon_r28x35mm.stl` | regular hexagon | circumradius 28 mm | 48.5 × 56.0 mm | 35 mm |
  | `g02_06_rect_70x45x40mm.stl` | rectangle | 70 × 45 mm | 70.0 × 45.0 mm | 40 mm |

  Three polygon families (square/rectangle, triangle, pentagon/hexagon) not in the golden set;
  bounding-box diagonal spans about 28–84 mm and height spans 15–40 mm.

  **The original justification here was wrong and is corrected, not just the numbers.** It
  claimed every footprint stayed at or under `s04_triangular_prism_60-26-65x30mm.stl`'s bounding
  box, reading 60-26-65 as that file's extent when they are its **triangle's side lengths**;
  s-04's actual bounding box is 60.0 × 26.0 × 30.0 mm (s-05's is 30 × 30 × 60). Loaded and
  checked directly: `g02_05` (48.5 × 56.0 mm) and `g02_06` (70.0 × 45.0 mm) are both larger
  footprints, on at least one axis, than anything the golden set has actually measured with
  R-23. That's fine to include — hand-sized still covers them — but the real argument for why
  R-23's anchor should still work on them is corner visibility, not a false "no larger than
  s-04" claim: the board has `(BOARD_SQUARES_X-1) * (BOARD_SQUARES_Y-1)` = 7 × 5 = 35 interior
  ChArUco corners (`recon/board_pose.py`), spaced `BOARD_SQUARE_MM` = 20 mm apart. A centred
  70 × 45 mm part (`g02_06`, the largest) hides the 3 × 3 = 9 corners directly under it from a
  straight-overhead frame, leaving about 26 of 35 visible per frame — comfortably above
  `MIN_CORNERS_FOR_POSE` = 6 (`recon/board_pose.py`) for that frame's own pose, and board corners
  accumulate across many frames toward `MIN_BOARD_CORNER_OBSERVATIONS` = 30
  (`recon/metric_alignment.py`), which R-23 already needs far fewer than one frame's worth of to
  clear. A non-overhead frame, or the part placed off-centre, hides fewer still.
- Every specimen is a convex polygon extruded along z, the only shape `prism_from_cad` in
  `recon/cad_size_check.py` measures. Each fits inside the board's 160 × 120 mm footprint
  (`DEFAULT_FOOTPRINT_MM` in `recon/part_segmentation.py`) with enough board left visible
  for R-23's board anchor to find its corners.
- Each specimen is printed on G-01's recorded printer and settings and labelled with its ID: the
  same Bambu Lab A1 mini model (ideally the same unit), filament and slicer profile as G-01's
  cubes, preferably on the same visit. **G-02 can't be Done until G-01 has recorded those
  settings** (G-01's second criterion); G-01's cube readings aren't needed.

## Tests

`tests/test_specimen_cad.py`, written with the STLs: every specimen file loads through
`prism_from_cad` and fits the board footprint. The prints themselves are a written check.

## Notes and decisions

- Departs from proposal §12, item 2, on purpose: "different shapes and finishes" becomes shapes
  only. Surface finish (glossy, dark, thin-walled) is G-11's robustness set, because finish
  breaks reconstruction rather than measuring its noise.
- R-23's pull request (#152) currently needs at least 30 board-corner observations; check
  the merged value before choosing the largest footprint.

- **2026-11-08: no longer blocked on G-01 as a whole.** The team wanted G-02 moving before G-01
  closes.
  - The design half has nothing to wait for: the size plan, the STLs, and the
    `prism_from_cad` / footprint test.
  - Only the printing needs G-01, and only its printer and settings, which G-01 records at its
    first lab print (Tuesday 2026-11-10). Its cube readings aren't needed.
  - So the dependency moved from the `Depends on` line, where the checker would keep the whole
    story Blocked, into the third criterion, where it still decides when G-02 is Done.
