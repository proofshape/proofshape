## Why this exists

The measured print is truth, not the CAD. G-04 compares each reconstruction against these
readings, so they have to measure the same edges `measure_prism` measures, the same way.

## Acceptance criteria

- Every footprint edge and the height of every G-02 specimen are measured with the caliper
  protocol in the Ground truth section of `stories/README.md`, into
  `fixtures/caliper_g-03.json`.
- Each feature's measured-minus-CAD difference is recorded below as a result, not judged
  against any tolerance.

## Tests

The readings file is checked as the Ground truth section's *Where readings go* says.

## Notes and decisions

### Measured 2026-11-14

**Who and how.** @TabeenRaoof took the readings with the G-06 caliper (0.1 mm steps, stated accuracy
±0.02 mm): 10 placements per feature, 26 features, 260 readings in `fixtures/caliper_g-03.json`.
They were typed into a caliper sheet that drew each specimen from above with lettered corners, and
copied into the file by script, not retyped. The file is checked by `tests/test_caliper_files.py`.

**Method,** the Ground truth section's protocol as G-06 applied it to s-04 and s-05:
- the square and the two rectangles across their parallel faces, two widths each;
- the triangle, pentagon and hexagon corner to corner, side by side;
- every height base to top, with the jaws flat;
- sides at mid-height, away from the first-layer flare.

Each feature's CAD vertex pair is in the file. The three parts whose sides look alike were marked
on the top face first: the square's sides 1–4 (side 1 is the CAD y = 0 wall, counting
counter-clockwise from above), and the pentagon's corners A–E and the hexagon's A–F,
counter-clockwise from above. `g02_01` and `g02_02` were measured on their A1 mini reprints, not
the first prints from the full-size A1. The square hasn't been photographed yet, so G-08 records
which way each numbered side faced on the board.

**What the readings show, without judging them:**
- Every specimen except the triangle is within ±0.2 mm of CAD on every feature.
- The triangle's three sides read 0.54 to 1.01 mm short. That is several times every other
  difference here, and the opposite sign to G-06's triangle s-04, which read 0.13 to 0.41 mm long
  measured the same way, corner to corner. Re-check a few readings on `g02_03` before G-04 relies
  on it.

**The measurer's notes, as written:**
- `g02_02`: "the bottom has some defects towards the sides and a little pushed in but slightly
  bulged on the other side because of this"
- `g02_05`: "corner between 2 and 3 at the bottom not sides is a little defected (pushed in) when
  measuring the height from that corner it's around 34.7- 24.8 mm instead of 34.9-35. the defect is
  slightly towards 2 and less on side 3". The hexagon's corners are lettered A–F, so which corners
  "2 and 3" are still needs saying. The range is probably 34.7–34.8 mm.
- `g02_06`: "when looking at the shape from the front, the bottom left corner is pushed in from the
  bottom not from the sides". Which corner (A–D) still needs saying.
