## Why this exists

X-03's first criterion is "the planted 4 mm hole shift (`g05_02`) produces `Fail`". The re-shot
`g05_02` (#421) registers upright, and isn't ambiguous under I-13. But on this measured pair and
#407's two specs, it gets the same verdict as the control. A 4 mm hole shift moves a wall by at
most 4 mm, and the verdict rule judges in-plane deviations against the measured in-plane floor of
5.278 mm (D-078). That explains this pair's result. It doesn't establish a general limit for
every 4 mm in-plane defect.

P-41's review (2026-11-20, `docs/accuracy-review-2026-11-20.md`) then tried the pair under every
fusion and alignment variant it had, including R-44's re-anchoring. The hole shift read like its
control in all of them. The control's own 12 mm hole is bridged over in the reconstruction, at
+5.1 to +5.6 mm, so the shifted hole has no clean signal to stand out from.

A bump on the top face is judged against the height floor instead, 0.86 mm (D-073). #407's
what-if planted G-05's 8 mm bump into the control's real session, and suggested a tolerance
window where the control Passes and the bump Fails. That makes it the one planted defect left
with a chance of showing as `Fail` in X-03 this term. Whether the print does is what this story
measures. `g05_09_bump_8mm.stl` is designed and on `main`, but D-069 cut it from
printing.

**This is #407's option 1, and this story's claim is the team's decision on it.** @TabeenRaoof
proposed taking it on 2026-11-21. The pull request that files this story also amends D-069 and
re-words X-03's first criterion, and it merges only if both other members approve. If either
declines, none of it lands, and the hole shift stays X-03's measured negative (option 3).

## Acceptance criteria

- **The scope change is recorded before anything is printed.** D-069 gets a dated amendment:
  `g05_09` (the 8 mm bump) is printed. `g05_08` (the 4 mm bump) may be printed in the same lab
  session. If it is, the next three criteria apply to it too. If it isn't, this story still
  closes. X-03's first criterion is re-worded to the bump, and X-03 depends on this story. The
  4 mm hole shift stays in X-03 as a scenario whose measured outcome is recorded, not dropped
  (X-03: "not a reason to pick a different part quietly"). Both other members approve.
- **Printed and calipered under G-05's plan.** Same printer, settings and filament as `g05_00`
  and `g05_02`, unrotated, with the part ID on the bottom face. The sliced file's sha256 is
  recorded, and the `.3mf` kept in Drive. The bump's height above the top face is read 10 times
  (G-06's caliper) into `fixtures/caliper_g-05.json`. That needs a new `step` kind, added to
  `tests/test_caliper_files.py`'s `KINDS` and `fixtures/README.md`'s tables in the same change.
- **Photographed under G-09's setup:** 25–30 stills, added to
  `fixtures/specimen_capture_manifest.json` with its hash lock regenerated. The download script
  fetches them with no change. The part sits **right side up**, checked against the control's
  frame IMG_4983 before the full set is taken (#407's correction, #421).
- **One check on `main`, recorded in this story,** of the real session against the control's CAD.
  It names the commit, and says whether R-44 and I-15 were on it. It records:
  - that the pose is upright and I-13's coverage ratio;
  - the bump site's signed deviation next to the control's at the same site, and whether the
    site has Observed support;
  - the verdict at #407's tolerance sweep for a `face` feature, and for any Fail, whether it
    comes from the bump site (X-03's first criterion) or from elsewhere on the mesh (D-080).

  No pass or fail line is claimed before the print is measured. #407's figures come from a
  what-if that planted the bump into the control's real session. They motivate printing the part,
  and establish neither a physical result nor a bound on what the print will read.

## Notes and decisions

- **Written up by P-37** (2026-11-20), from #407's options and its synthetic what-if (2026-11-20
  comment), and posted on #407 as a draft so it couldn't be claimed before the team chose.
- **Waiting since 2026-11-21 on a lab slot** (D-064). Nothing in this story can start before
  the print, and the print needs the lab. It becomes active when the slot is booked.
- **Filed and claimed on 2026-11-21** by @TabeenRaoof, who printed, calipered and photographed
  the other two G-05 parts and has the lab access. The fold check was re-run on `main` at
  `4c670ad`, and the number against every origin branch:
  - G-05 and G-09 are Done, for `g05_00` and `g05_02` only;
  - G-11's parts are robustness parts, not planted defects;
  - no story prints any other G-05 ladder part;
  - G-13 (P-41's follow-up, in #427) re-measures the verdict's floors. It doesn't add a part.
- **Why the 8 mm bump first.** In #407's what-if, the control **Passes** a `face` at 6–7.5 mm
  tolerance, and the planted 8 mm bump **Fails** at every tolerance swept. That's the clearest
  contrast X-03 can show, though at a loose tolerance. The 4 mm bump (`g05_08`) only shows Rescan
  against Fail, in a 2–3 mm non-critical window with about 1.5 mm of margin. It keeps M4's 4 mm
  amplitude, so it's worth adding if there's lab time.
- **What P-41 adds to that.** The lower edge of the window is the control's own worst reading,
  5.1 to 5.6 mm, at its bridged hole. With the CAD alignment fitted on the upper half of the
  model, the control first Passes at 5.5 to 6 mm. Sharper walls (R-44) and re-measured floors
  (G-13) don't move that edge on this part, because the hole sets it. A control with no hole
  would give a wider window, but it needs a new CAD, and this story doesn't propose one.
- **The control's pose has to hold.** In P-41's scratch run, R-44's correction with nothing else
  changed turned the control 180° with no ambiguity flag, which I-15 exists to stop. The check
  in the last criterion runs on `main` as it is that day, and says so.
- **Known risk.** A real reconstruction rounds the bump's edges, at 2 mm voxels and 8 mm
  truncation, so it may read lower than the what-if. v1's verdict is mesh-wide (D-080). The
  control already Fails tight specs through its hole bridging and wall bias (I-14), so a bump
  helps only at a tolerance above the control's own reading. If the real print doesn't separate,
  that is the finding to record.
- **Re-worded 2026-11-21, at @dalwalyk's review of #432.** X-03's first criterion now requires
  the Fail to come from the bump site, with the control within tolerance there, because v1's
  verdict is mesh-wide and another surface could cause it. The last criterion records that
  attribution. The what-if is described as motivation, not as a result or a bound.
- **Timing.** X-04 and X-05 wait on X-03, and their gate is M6 on Dec 2. The print needs a lab
  slot, and the week of Nov 23 is Thanksgiving week.
- **Freeze.** This adds a test part, not a feature, so it isn't new scope under M5's freeze. The
  part's design is already on `main` (G-05). D-069 itself named printing more "unless time is
  left after M6" as the way back in. Taking it before M6 is the reason for the amendment.
- **Estimate basis:**
  - `g05_02`'s slicer time was 25 min 24 s (G-05). The bump adds 8 mm of height over a 10 × 10 mm
    site.
  - G-09's photos took 2 h actual for two parts, so about 1 h for one.
  - The calipers, the `step` kind and the check take about 1 h.

  That's 3 h for `g05_09` alone, and about 1.5 h more for `g05_08`.
