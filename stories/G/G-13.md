## Why this exists

The verdict thresholds one quantity: how far each Observed vertex of the fused mesh sits from the
registered CAD. Three measured numbers decide what it can say about that quantity, and none of
the three was measured on it.

- **The two floors.** 0.86 mm for heights (D-073) and 5.278 mm in-plane (D-078) both come from
  `cad_size_check` reading part dimensions off R-01's raw point cloud: five heights and eleven
  edge lengths, on the six G-02 specimens. Neither is a per-vertex distance on the fused mesh.
- **The outline margin.** D-086 measures a vertex only inside the CAD's outline, with no margin.
  It was set on `main`'s meshes, where no positive margin separated flare from wall scatter.

P-41's review (`docs/accuracy-review-2026-11-20.md`) measured what follows from that, on ten
calipered parts each against its own CAD:

- **The gate, not the data, decides when a good part Passes.** Pass needs tolerance ÷ 0.70 to
  reach the in-plane floor, so nothing Passes under 3.69 mm. In the scratch run with R-44's
  correction, the parts that Pass have worst readings of 1.1 to 2.8 mm, and they Pass at exactly
  3.7 mm. A critical `hole` or `edge` under 3.69 mm is Unverifiable at intake for the same reason
  (I-10).
- **Only the inner half of each wall is measured.** Under the 0 mm margin, 22 to 72% of the
  Observed surface is unmeasured, every part's wall median is negative, and a wall that is
  oversize is mostly not seen. On the re-anchored meshes, in the scratch run, a 2 mm margin gave
  two-sided wall readings (medians −0.5 to +0.3 mm) and roughly doubled the measured surface on
  several parts, with worst readings at or under 2.7 mm on seven of the eight prisms.

R-44 changes how sharply walls are reconstructed, and I-15 changes the pose on some parts. Both
change the quantity these numbers describe. So this story waits for them, and then measures the
three numbers on the pipeline as it is actually served.

## Acceptance criteria

- **Measured on the served pipeline, on the verdict's own quantity.** After R-44 and I-15 land,
  the ten calipered parts go through `scripts/measure_part_accuracy.py`. The record gives, per
  part and pooled, for each of the verdict's two pools as the verdict itself splits them: the
  per-vertex deviation, the per-vertex sigma, and the share of readings inside
  `k × max(sigma, floor)` for each candidate floor. A second member re-runs at least two parts.
- **The outline margin is settled first, on that record,** because the floors depend on which
  vertices are measured. The record compares 0 mm with the candidate margins: the measured share,
  wall readings on both sides, and how much surface more than 5 mm from the part each margin lets
  in. The outcome is a dated amendment to D-086, or a recorded reason to keep 0 mm.
- **The floors are proposed as amendments to D-073 and D-078, by a statistic stated before the
  numbers are read.** D-077's own definition is the starting candidate: the smallest floor at
  which `k × max(sigma, floor)` covers 95% of a good part's readings in that pool, with `k` left
  at 0.70. The constants in `inspect/inspection/verdict.py` and `intake_check.py` change only if
  both other members approve the decision. If they don't, the measurement merges as a record and
  the constants stay as they are. The contract is untouched either way.
- **What the change does to verdicts is on record before it merges.** For each of the ten good
  parts, and for `g05_02` against the control's CAD, the tolerance sweep before and after. A good
  part that Fails at a tolerance where it didn't before blocks the change. I-08's hard-rule tests
  pass unchanged, I-10's intake arithmetic is restated with the new thresholds, and
  `docs/manual.md` is updated wherever it quotes one.

## Tests

The tests that pin the constants (`tests/test_verdict.py`, the intake-check tests, I-08's
hard-rule tests) are updated in the same pull request as the constants, and nowhere else. Any
logic added to the measurement script, such as the coverage figure for a candidate floor, gets
unit tests as it is written. The real-capture runs are a written check: commands, commit,
captures and numbers in this file. Captures and run outputs stay outside git.

## Notes and decisions

- **Written up by P-41** on 2026-11-21, a day after the review, when @TabeenRaoof asked for it
  to be filed and not left as a proposal. Fold check on `main` at `4c670ad`, and every origin
  branch for the number:
  - G-04 measured D-073 and R-37 measured D-078, both through `cad_size_check` on the raw
    cloud. G-10 would have measured floors for references that were then cut (D-083).
  - G-07 measured `k` (D-077) against `max(sigma, floor)` on top-face vertices of the fused
    mesh. It is the closest precedent, and this story keeps its `k`.
  - I-14 set the margin (D-086) on `main`'s meshes and recorded the bias it leaves.
  - R-43 measures edge loss across fusion grids against calipers. It doesn't measure the
    per-vertex quantity, and the fusion grid doesn't feed D-078, which reads the raw cloud.
  - #405 is the instability of the edge measurement behind D-078. This story doesn't use that
    measurement.
- **Why it waits.** Measuring now would describe a pipeline that is about to change. If R-44
  merges off by default, the floors are measured on the default that is served, and R-44's
  configuration is recorded beside it for whenever it is switched on.
- **It promises no number.** The figures above are a scratch run on one capture per part. The
  floors may come out near today's, and that would be a result too.
- **What the record can and can't support.** Vertices on one part are not independent readings,
  and there are ten parts with one capture each. The record says what that supports. If the
  evidence is too thin to move a floor, it says so, and the floor stays.
- **Not in this story:** how a vertex is assigned to a pool. The verdict splits by height, which
  puts 35 to 530 top-face vertices per part in the in-plane pool. The record reports what a
  split by CAD face would give, and changes nothing. Symmetric CADs, the every-vertex rule and
  the 3.0 mm sigma cap are also out of scope.
- **If `k` doesn't hold.** `k` stays D-077's 0.70. If the record shows 0.70 doesn't cover what
  D-077 said it covers, the story says so and stops there. That would be its own decision.
- **Freeze.** New thresholds change what the service returns, so they merge by M6 (Dec 2) or
  not until after the Dec 3–4 freeze. The measurement record can merge at any time.
- **Not claimed.** A Blocked story has no owner. @TabeenRaoof has said they intend to take it
  when it unblocks.
- **Estimate basis:** the measurement is scripted (P-41), so the runs are about an hour. The
  margin comparison, the floors, two decision amendments, the constants with their tests, and
  the before-and-after sweep are about four more.
