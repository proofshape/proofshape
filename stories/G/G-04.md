## Why this exists

The critical path runs through this story. M3 reads "σ_floor is a measured number. Every
demo tolerance is set from it" (sprint plan S3). The verdict logic can't set tolerances
without it (I-07, I-09), and R-16 needs a measured figure to judge R-04's known-size
comparison against.

## Acceptance criteria

- The method is written below before anything is computed: per-feature error is
  reconstruction minus caliper (G-03, G-06), not minus CAD; scale error is separated from
  shape error by the similarity fit; each edge's bias is reported apart from the spread
  (R-22's Finding 2); mean and 95th-percentile error for the board reference (proposal
  §12); how σ_floor follows from them; and which figure R-16 is to judge against.
- The chain runs on every G-08 capture: R-01 and R-02, then R-04 with R-23's board anchor,
  then `recon/cad_size_check.py` extended to take caliper truth and G-06's measured board
  pitch. The confidence filter used is named (D-045).
- The result is recorded as a decision in `docs/decisions.md`, with its conditions: landscape
  phone stills, the board reference, R-01's raw point cloud rather than the R-07/R-08 mesh.
  It says it doesn't yet hold for app capture or for the sheet and card references (G-10):
  σ_floor is defined per reference (glossary), and this is the board reference's.
- If σ_floor comes out above 3 mm, that is reported as the result and the proposal's M3
  fallback applies ("demo tolerances move up and the Unverifiable path becomes the
  story"), rather than the method being tuned until the number looks better.
