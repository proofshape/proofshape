## Why this exists

Every specimen and the defect ladder come off this printer, and from G-03 on the printed
part, not its CAD, is the truth everything is measured against. FDM drifts by a few tenths
of a millimetre (proposal §12), so how far this printer drifts, per axis, is the noise floor
of the ground truth itself. The plan wanted printer access settled at M0 (sprint plan D5);
nothing records that it was.

## Acceptance criteria

- SFBU lab access is confirmed and recorded below: who books it, how far ahead, the
  turnaround per print, and a named fallback (a print service) if the lab falls through.
- The printer model, nozzle, material and colour, and slicer settings (layer height, walls,
  infill) are recorded, so G-02 and G-05 print on exactly these settings.
- The cube protocol is written below before anything is printed: cube size, at least three
  prints, the axes measured, and 10 caliper readings per axis per print.
- Per-axis deviation from nominal, and its spread across prints, are reported as measured,
  in `fixtures/caliper_g-01.json`. No pass/fail judgement and no tolerance.

## Tests

Written check for the record. The readings file is checked as the Ground truth section's *Where
readings go* says.

**Cube protocol (ready to execute once the above is settled):**
- Print **three calibration cubes**, same model/material/settings each time. Recommend a cube
  edge length in the same size range as the G-02 specimens (tens of millimetres) — large enough
  that caliper resolution is a small fraction of the edge, small enough to print quickly and sit
  easily on the board. The owner confirms the exact nominal size against whatever model file is
  used; this note doesn't fix a number, since "no promised numbers" applies to a planning note
  the same as to a result.
- For each of the 3 cubes, measure all **3 axes** (X, Y, Z edge length), **10 independent caliper
  placements per axis**, following the Ground truth section's method in `stories/README.md`
  ("Calipers, PR #35's protocol"): read away from the first-layer flare, state the caliper's
  resolution, and say "no accuracy spec" if the instrument states none. That is 3 cubes × 3 axes
  × 10 readings = 90 raw readings total.
- Report mean, min, max and sample standard deviation per axis per cube. No tolerance, no
  pass/fail judgement — per this story's own acceptance criteria, that judgement is G-04's job,
  not this one's.
- Record every reading in `fixtures/caliper_g-01.json`, in the exact schema `fixtures/README.md`
  already defines: `version: 1`, `story: "G-01"`, `measured_by`, `date`, `instrument` (type,
  resolution_mm, accuracy_spec), and one `features` entry per cube per axis — `object` naming the
  cube (e.g. `"cube-1"`), `kind: "cube_axis"`, `cad_vertices: null`, `nominal_mm` from the cube
  model, `nominal_source: "cube"`, `square_count: null`, `where` describing the placement,
  `orientation_on_board: null`, and the 10 `readings_mm` with their `mean_mm`/`min_mm`/`max_mm`/
  `std_mm`. That is 9 `features` entries (3 cubes × 3 axes).
- `tests/test_caliper_files.py` (landing with G-06) will check this file against that schema once
  it exists; until then, check it by hand against `fixtures/README.md`.
