## Why this exists

Every specimen and the defect ladder come off this printer, and from G-03 on the printed
part, not its CAD, is the truth everything is measured against. FDM drifts by a few tenths
of a millimetre (proposal §12), so how far this printer drifts, per axis, is the noise floor
of the ground truth itself. The plan wanted printer access settled at M0 (sprint plan D5);
nothing records that it was.

## Acceptance criteria

- SFBU lab access is confirmed and recorded below: who books it, how far ahead, the
  turnaround per print, and a named fallback (a print service) if the lab falls through.
- The printer model, nozzle, material and colour, and slicer settings (layer height, walls,
  infill) are recorded, so G-02 and G-05 print on exactly these settings.
- The cube protocol is written below before anything is printed: cube size, at least three
  prints, the axes measured, and 10 caliper readings per axis per print.
- Per-axis deviation from nominal, and its spread across prints, are reported as measured,
  in `fixtures/caliper_g-01.json`. No pass/fail judgement and no tolerance.

### How to print and measure

**Slicing.** Use Bambu Studio's stock 0.20 mm Standard process preset for the A1 mini (0.4 mm
nozzle), and the Bambu PLA Basic filament preset, unchanged. Save the sliced project (`.3mf`). It holds every setting, and
the exact values are read from it into this file: layer height, wall loops, infill density and
pattern. That is how G-02 and G-05 will print on exactly these settings. Record the filament colour
used.

**Printing.** Three separate print jobs, one cube each, so that run-to-run variation shows. Place
each cube centred on the bed, in the orientation it loads in. Before taking each cube off the bed,
mark it with a pen: its number (1, 2, 3) on top, and "F" on the face toward the printer's front.
Then:
- X is across the left and right faces;
- Y is across the front and back faces;
- Z is from top to bottom.

**Measuring.** Use the G-06 caliper: a digital caliper reading in 0.1 mm steps, with a stated
accuracy of ±0.02 mm. Measure once the cube has cooled.
- For each cube and axis, take 10 independent placements: reopen the jaws and move along the face
  between readings.
- Read X and Y at mid-height, about 15 mm up, away from the first-layer flare.
- Read Z across the top and bottom faces, away from the edges.

That is 3 cubes × 3 axes × 10 = 90 readings. They go in `fixtures/caliper_g-01.json` with
`nominal_mm` 30.0, `nominal_source` `"cube"` and `kind` `"cube_axis"`.

Reading the table:
- Every deviation in it is smaller than one 0.1 mm caliper step. Each mean is an average of
  readings on that step, not a finer measurement.
- A true spread well under one step can read as zero. Cube 3's Z reads 30.0 all ten times.
- What the readings do show: Y read short on all three prints, by close to the same amount, and X
  moved more between prints than Y or Z did.
