## Why this exists

G-04 needs reconstructions of the calipered specimens, so it needs photographs of them,
stored the way the golden set is: outside git, checksummed, and downloadable by anyone.

## Acceptance criteria

- 25–30 landscape stills of each G-02 specimen on the G-06-calipered board sheet, under the
  golden set's lighting A, following F-06's capture pattern.
- Stored on the same Google Drive as the golden set, with
  `fixtures/specimen_capture_manifest.json` holding each sample's frame count and
  `content_sha256`, computed the way F-06 computes it.
- `fixtures/download_golden_capture.sh` downloads either set: its hard-coded
  `for sample in s-01 s-02 s-03 s-04 s-05` loop reads the samples from the manifest instead.
- The manifest is locked by a checksum git actually tracks. `fixtures/*.sha256` is ignored
  today (only the golden set's was force-added), so either the lock lives inside the JSON or
  `.gitignore` allows it.

## Tests

`tests/test_download_golden_capture.py` is extended for the manifest-driven loop, and
`tests/test_fixture_ignore_rules.py` covers any `.gitignore` change. The photographs are a
written check.

## Notes and decisions

- Landscape stills, for two reasons. The golden set is landscape (5712 × 4284), so the
  specimens are shot the way every reconstruction so far was. And @TabeenRaoof's review
  of #152 (2026-10-17, blocking item 1) reproduced a 4–5% scale error on portrait frames
  in R-23's corner mapping; that is an open review finding, not yet fixed or recorded in
  R-23, and landscape stills stay clear of it either way. App capture belongs to S6's
  accuracy runs, not here.
- The photographs are never committed (`AGENTS.md`).
