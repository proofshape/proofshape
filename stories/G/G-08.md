## Why this exists

G-04 needs reconstructions of the calipered specimens, so it needs photographs of them,
stored the way the golden set is: outside git, checksummed, and downloadable by anyone.

## Acceptance criteria

- 25–30 landscape stills of each G-02 specimen on the G-06-calipered board sheet, under the
  golden set's lighting A, following F-06's capture pattern.
- Stored on the same Google Drive as the golden set, with
  `fixtures/specimen_capture_manifest.json` holding each sample's frame count and
  `content_sha256`, computed the way F-06 computes it.
- `fixtures/download_golden_capture.sh` downloads either set: its hard-coded
  `for sample in s-01 s-02 s-03 s-04 s-05` loop reads the samples from the manifest instead.
- The manifest is locked by a checksum git actually tracks. `fixtures/*.sha256` is ignored
  today (only the golden set's was force-added), so either the lock lives inside the JSON or
  `.gitignore` allows it.

## Tests

`tests/test_download_golden_capture.py` is extended for the manifest-driven loop, and
`tests/test_fixture_ignore_rules.py` covers any `.gitignore` change. The photographs are a
written check.

## Notes and decisions

- Landscape stills, for two reasons. The golden set is landscape (5712 × 4284), so the
  specimens are shot the way every reconstruction so far was. And @TabeenRaoof's review
  of #152 (2026-10-17, blocking item 1) reproduced a 4–5% scale error on portrait frames
  in R-23's corner mapping; that is an open review finding, not yet fixed or recorded in
  R-23, and landscape stills stay clear of it either way. App capture belongs to S6's
  accuracy runs, not here.
- The photographs are never committed (`AGENTS.md`).

### Done (2026-11-15)

**The photographs.** @TabeenRaoof shot all six specimens on 2026-11-15:
- **Setup:** the same physical board sheet G-06 calipered (owner-confirmed; it has the same fold
  creases as in the golden photos), on the golden set's table under lighting condition A.
- **Camera:** iPhone 16 main camera, 5712 × 4284 landscape. That is the camera and resolution
  the golden set used.
- **Storage:** one Drive folder per specimen (`g02_01` … `g02_06`), on the same Drive account as
  the golden set.

| | g02_01 | g02_02 | g02_03 | g02_04 | g02_05 | g02_06 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Part | square prism | rect. prism | right triangle | pentagon | hexagon | large rect. |
| Frames | 29 | 30 | 25 | 27 | 29 | 29 |
| Board posed by R-02 | all | all | all | all | all | all |
| Median ChArUco corners | 28 | 22 | 25 | 23 | 17 | 13 |

**Checked before locking.**
- R-02's own `estimate_board_poses` posed the board in every frame. Reprojection RMS was
  2.0–2.4 px median, against golden s-04's 2.48 px under the same function, where 1 of 30 frames
  failed.
- No frame is softer than the golden set's softest (Laplacian variance at 1428 px wide).
- `g02_03`'s first frame was taken on the ultra-wide lens (2.22 mm, 4032 × 3024). A second lens
  would break the single-camera calibration a reconstruction relies on, so it was deleted from
  Drive before the hashes were computed, leaving 25 frames.

**The manifest.**
- `fixtures/specimen_capture_manifest.json` follows the golden manifest's format. Each
  `content_sha256` and `aggregate_bytes` was computed with the downloader's own
  `sample_content_hash`, the function that reproduces golden s-04's recorded hash exactly.
- `reference_cad` maps each sample to its G-02 STL.
- `specimen_capture_manifest.sha256` locks the manifest. `.gitignore` now tracks top-level
  `fixtures/*.sha256`, so this lock needs no force-add, and checksums inside `fixtures/data/` stay
  ignored.

**The downloader** was already manifest-driven: F-08 replaced the hard-coded `s-01 … s-05` loop,
with a test. Downloading the specimen set is the same script pointed at the other manifest
(`fixtures/README.md`).

**End to end against the real Drive folders, 2026-11-15, on the M2 Max:**
- **Passed:** `g02_01`, `g02_02` and `g02_03` downloaded through `fixtures/download_golden_capture.sh`,
  passed the frame-count and `content_sha256` checks, and were swapped in. That proves the
  manifest format, the hashing and `g02_03`'s post-deletion hash.
- **Refused by gdown:** after about 100 files from these folders that day, gdown failed every
  further file with "Failed to retrieve file url", cookies on or off. Drive itself still served
  those same files on its plain download URL. A retry an hour later failed the same way, and the
  script correctly refused to swap in the incomplete `g02_04`.
- **The other three:** `g02_04`, `g02_05` and `g02_06` are hashed from byte-exact copies (each
  file's size checked against Drive's), with the same `sample_content_hash`.
- **Cross-check against the originals:** the owner's own photo folders, as they came off the phone,
  hash to exactly the manifest's six `content_sha256` values with the same function (`g02_03`
  without the dropped ultra-wide frame). So the originals, the Drive copies and the manifest
  agree byte for byte for every sample.
- **Still to do:** the second-member run of the downloader, on a fresh machine and day, completes
  the end-to-end proof for these three.
