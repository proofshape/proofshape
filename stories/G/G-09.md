### `g05_02` re-photographed right side up (2026-11-20, @TabeenRaoof)

**The 2026-11-18 `g05_02` set had the plate top face down.** It was found while reviewing
#416 (I-14):
- **The photos are a mirror image.** The control's frames have the notch at the front-left and
  the hole on the right. The old `g05_02` frames had the notch at the front-right and the hole
  on the left, with the board the same way round (IMG_4983 vs IMG_5013).
- **It wasn't a rotation or a misprint.** A rotation can't produce a mirror image, and G-05's
  G-code check rules out a mirrored print. With a through-notch and a through-hole, a plate
  that is flipped over looks like the CAD's mirror.
- **So no registration pose could be right.** `global_register` only seeds upright poses: all
  nine refined candidates had R[2,2] = −1, and the "best" one, at yaw −179.4°, was wrong.
- **What that invalidated:** #407's top-face "signal" for this part (p95 6.69 vs 2.00 mm) and
  its `g05_02` verdicts, I-13's `g05_02` row and I-14's criterion-3 baseline. All four are
  corrected where they were recorded. The control's set was right side up and is unaffected.
