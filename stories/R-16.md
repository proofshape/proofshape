## Why this exists

R-04's known-size acceptance criterion originally read "an object of known size measures
correctly within a stated tolerance." That criterion asks two different questions: whether the
alignment produces a comparable measurement at all (R-04 answered this — see its notes for the
recorded 60.27 mm vs. 60.011 mm caliper comparison on the s-04 specimen), and whether that
measurement is *good enough*, which requires a real tolerance figure to judge it against. No such
figure exists yet: it has to come from the ground-truth calibration study (`G-01` printer
characterisation through `G-05` defect ladder), not from a single hand-picked comparison. A PR
attempt to derive one from that single comparison anyway (D-039) was reverted — see R-04's notes
and D-032 for why. This story exists so that judgment happens once the real number does, instead
of never happening or happening dishonestly.

## Acceptance criteria

- Applies the tolerance the ground-truth calibration study establishes (`G-03` caliper
  measurement, `G-04` noise floor per tier) to R-04's recorded known-size comparison.
- Reports the result as within-tolerance, out-of-tolerance, or explicitly
  insufficient-evidence if the study's conditions don't cover this comparison — never a
  pass/fail dressed up from a number this story invented itself.
- Unit tested against the stated tolerance, once one exists to test against.
