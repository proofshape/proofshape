## Why this exists

On 2026-11-20 @TabeenRaoof asked what in the project is marked Done but still doesn't work as
planned, with the reconstruction and the verdict first, because the scans look noisy. An earlier
audit of the same question was written against `bbf24f1`, 138 commits behind `main`.

The review ran `main` at `607450a` on the ten printed parts that have caliper readings, each
against its own CAD, and then looked for the cause. It found that most of the wall error comes
from photos disagreeing sideways, which the board's corners can correct, and that stray board
surface can turn the CAD alignment without a flag. Those findings change what the team should
build before Dec 2, so they have to be on record where both other members can read them, check
them, and re-run them, before a story is built on them.

## Acceptance criteria

- **The findings are written up** in `docs/accuracy-review-2026-11-20.md`: the method, what each
  part reads on `main`, the causes with their numbers, what was tried and what didn't help, and
  the limits. Every figure comes from a recorded run and names its commit, captures and command.
  Each is stated as what it is: one capture per part.
- **The measurement can be re-run.** `scripts/measure_part_accuracy.py`, with unit tests,
  reproduces the per-part table and the per-photo corner residuals from the captures in the
  committed manifests. The offline fusion variants came from a scratch harness. They are
  labelled as that, and R-44 has to reproduce them in repo code.
- **The work it calls for is written up, not started.** R-44 is filed and claimed with this
  story. The CAD alignment's sensitivity to stray surface gets its own story, since R-44's
  default depends on it. Anything that needs a choice from the team first (the floors, the
  outline margin) is listed as a proposal, not filed as a Ready story.
- **Both other members are asked to review, and to re-run the script if they can.** The record
  says who re-ran what, and what they got.
