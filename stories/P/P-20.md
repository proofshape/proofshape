## Why this exists

The midterm presentation is a graded deliverable.

## Acceptance criteria

- The deck is finished and presented.
- Every figure in it comes from a recorded result (the progress log, a story's notes), with no
  unmeasured accuracy or latency number (`AGENTS.md`, "No promised numbers").
- It is committed under `submissions/` at a size git can keep, or linked if it can't be
  shrunk. The local draft is 9 MB; the 18 Sept deck was 1.5 MB.
- **Content covers everything from the start of term up to Thursday 15 October 2026 (Pacific)
  and nothing after.** A fixed boundary, not a moving one — otherwise every re-review would need
  its own fresh set of numbers, and content merged tomorrow would make today's accurate sentence
  wrong by the time someone reads it. Anything that landed after that boundary (C-08's merge,
  R-23 being claimed, the P/G/I lanes, C-13's successful device session) is next-steps material
  for the final deck, not this one. Same rule, same boundary, as P-21.

## Tests

Written-check exception (D-052): this story's output is a document, and a document can't be
unit-tested. The check is a reviewer reading it against the acceptance criteria above.
