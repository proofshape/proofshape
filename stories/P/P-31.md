## Why this exists

`stories/README.md`'s "Phase 3 and beyond · titles only" section has carried 17 one-line titles
across four lanes since P-24/P-25/P-26 each deliberately deferred the second half of their own
lane: P-25 kept I-06..I-10 because "they wait on G-04's σ_floor and the D-032 tolerance schema";
P-24 kept G-10 and G-11; P-26 kept A-02, A-03, A-05, A-07 and A-09. Cross-cutting (X-01..X-05,
the sprint plan's four integration points and the backup demo) was never written up at all.

Several of the original blocking reasons have genuinely cleared since then: D-062 now fully
specifies I-06's GLB attribute format; A-06 and A-08 are both Done, clearing A-07's stated
structural prerequisite; and every dependency I1 (X-01) ever needed — the thin capture thread,
the endpoint, deploy, the GPU workspace — is Done. This is not writing ahead blind; it is
catching up now that real facts exist for some of these, while the rest (I-07 onward, most of
G/A/X) get written honestly as Blocked, grounded in what's already decided rather than guessed
ahead of it.

  in — no invented tolerance, σ, cost or `k` (D-003), and every open design question (I-10's
  per-feature-vs-whole-order fork, A-02's `unchecked`-blocks-Pass fork, A-09's Tier-1-vs-Tier-3
  conflict) is stated plainly rather than silently resolved.
- Four of the 17 (I-06, A-05, A-07, X-01) come out `Ready` by the declared dependency graph,
  because every story they depend on is already Done; the other 13 are `Blocked`, honestly, on
  work that hasn't happened yet. A-05's `Ready` carries a real caveat, same pattern as C-21's
  O-005 note: it cannot run against real per-session data until a completion-runner story (not
  yet written, not one of these 17) exists — stated plainly in its own acceptance criteria rather
  than hidden behind a `Blocked` state the dependency graph wouldn't actually support.

  now has a story file; stale "still titles-only" cross-references in C-14/C-15/the capture-app
