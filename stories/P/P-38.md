    declared in the final report and the defense");

    (#407, #421);

  - the narrative being template-only (D-082);
  - shape completion being `unavailable` on both served hosts (D-058, D-072);
  - the capture agent, served by A-15. If A-15 misses the freeze, the report says the agent was
    built and not served, as a shortfall against D-017, not as a cut;

    result;
