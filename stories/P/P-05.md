## Why this exists

A rule that lives only in prose is kept by memory, and in the first week the backlog's
states had already drifted from the story files. This puts the definition of done in front
of every pull request, and makes unblocking a dependent story a script, not a role.

## Acceptance criteria

- `.github/PULL_REQUEST_TEMPLATE.md` carries the definition-of-done checklist (D-025, #3).
- `scripts/check_story_states.py` reports story files that disagree with the index and, with
  `--fix`, flips a story from Blocked to Ready only when every dependency is Done (D-027, #5).
- `AGENTS.md` says to set a story's state at merge time (#2).

## Tests

The checker is code, so it has tests: `tests/test_check_story_states.py`, added in #5 after
@dalwalyk's review asked for them. That made them tests after the script, not alongside it,
which is the gap D-029 (P-07) was written to close. The template and the `AGENTS.md` rule are
documents: written check, reviewed in #3 and #2 by @mbj1994.
