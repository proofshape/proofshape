## Why this exists

After A-12's Colab run, the completed shapes looked well outside a 2–3% error: the padlock's
shackle came out bent. So the questions were whether more photos, or fixing the issues already
filed, would improve the reconstruction's accuracy. Answering them meant measuring. The measuring
found a hang that blocks every reconstruction on the Mac. None of this belongs to a single story.

## Acceptance criteria

- **The accuracy question is answered with measurements**, recorded where the team reads them:
  the progress log, 2026-11-07. That covers A-12's shapes against the observed shell and the CAD,
  and golden s-04 rebuilt from 30 and from 15 photos, checked with `recon/cad_size_check.py`. The
  caveats are stated, and no tolerance is claimed (D-003).
- **Every open issue @TabeenRaoof filed is mapped to the story or decision that owns it.** Issues
  that were already fixed are closed, each with links to the code that fixed it.
- **Work found with no owner is written up or handed over.** That means filing the Mac hang as
  #261 and writing it up as R-31, and raising #231's walls with R-30's claimant on #258.

## Scope

What was done on 2026-11-07:

- **Accuracy.**
  - The A-12 completions were compared with the observed shell and the CAD. The padlock's
    shackle is chrome and was barely observed, so LaS-Comp guessed it, differently for each seed.
  - s-04's observed walls come out 3–9% narrow at mid-height, with extra surface flaring out at
    the base.
  - No project document or submission states a 2–3% figure.
- **Photo count.** s-04 was rebuilt from 30 photos and from two 15-photo subsets, then compared
  with the CAD. Scale and height came out right in every run. More photos bought coverage
  (observed fraction 0.169 against 0.124 and 0.082), but not shape accuracy. The figures are in
  the progress log.
- **The Mac hang.** Open3D and torch each load their own `libomp`, and since `4237642` the
  pipeline loads Open3D before VGGT. Filed as #261, with a reproduction table, the stack trace and
  the verified workaround `OMP_NUM_THREADS=1`. Written up as R-31.
- **Issues against stories.**
  - P-27's 24 issues and ten stories were reconciled.
  - #183 and #184 were fixed by R-27 and #108 by C-09, but were still open. All three are now
    closed, each with links to the fixing code.
  - #231 was measured on current `main`: walls are 70% of the served model's faces, and 56% of its
    Observed faces are board skin. That went to @dalwalyk on #258.
