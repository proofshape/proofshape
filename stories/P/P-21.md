## Why this exists

The midterm report is a graded deliverable, written by the team.

## Acceptance criteria

- The report is finished and submitted.
- Every figure in it comes from a recorded result, with no unmeasured accuracy or latency
  number (`AGENTS.md`, "No promised numbers").
- It is committed under `submissions/` at a size git can keep, or linked if it can't be
  shrunk. The local draft was 8.7 MB with print-resolution figures; recompressed to the
  resolution a printed page actually needs, it is 1.6 MB, in line with the review decks
  (P-18, P-19) already in the repository.
- **Content covers everything from the start of term up to Thursday 15 October 2026
  (Pacific) and nothing after.** A fixed boundary, not a moving one — otherwise every
  re-review would need its own fresh set of numbers, and content merged tomorrow would
  make today's accurate sentence wrong by the time someone reads it. Anything that landed
  after that boundary (C-08's merge, R-23 being claimed, the P/G/I lanes, C-13's successful
  device session) is next-steps material for the final report, not this one.
