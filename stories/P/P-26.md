## Why this exists

The course requires about a third of the project to be generative AI, and those components are
never cut (AGENTS.md, D-017). None of it is built: VLM supervision was due in S2 (its M2 gate, "a
screen photo is rejected by the VLM", was missed) and shape completion in S3. The generative-AI
lane is nine titles on one line of `stories/README.md`. Its first two stories can start now; the
rest wait on the verdict or on shape completion.

## Acceptance criteria

- The six stories listed under *Scope* below have story files and rows in a new "Generative AI"
  section of `stories/README.md`, and `scripts/check_story_states.py` passes on them.
- Every story cites what the proposal already decides (§6.5, §6.6, §8.3), the hard rules (D-005,
  D-006, issue #157), and the generative test ("run it twice, can it give two different
  answers?"), and promises no cost, latency, accuracy or K.
- The titles-only list keeps A-02, A-03, A-05, A-07 and A-09.

## Scope

The six stories, by topic. They are the content of the first criterion, not separate criteria.

- **A-01** VLM frame supervision: sample frames, call a pinned Gemini Flash-Lite model (as the
  proposal names), record structured results server-side.
- **A-10** Spike: choose the shape-completion model. No model is named beyond the proposal's
  "diffusion-based shape completion, K samples".
- **A-04** Shape completion stage: K samples stored, Inferred label defined, the verdict mesh left
  untouched.
- **A-11** Show the VLM hint to the supplier: a contract field and a capture message, carrying the
  D-031 request.
- **A-06** Agent tool definitions: the five tools of proposal §6.7 as typed, deterministic server
  functions. Added 2026-10-23 at @TabeenRaoof's request, because the capture agent is the largest
  generative-AI item and its tools mostly exist already.
- **A-08** Deterministic capture policy: the agent's fallback, which the proposal says is built
  first, so every session completes. Added with A-06.
