## Why this exists

The rest of the capture app has been five titles on one line of `stories/README.md` since
September: heatmap rendering, the report page, buyer setup, the board PDF with the order code,
and device testing. The sprint plan put the first two in S4 and the next two in S5, and S5 ends
with M5's feature freeze on Fri Nov 13. Two pieces of work behind them have no story at all:
- **Hosting the app.** D-048 deferred it "to its own future story".
- **The order store** the buyer page saves to. R-14: "buyer/order storage is not in the written
  backlog yet".

The Nov 13 boundary call "agree[s] next sprint's stories", so they need writing before it.

## Acceptance criteria

- The seven stories listed under *Scope* below have story files and rows in a new section of
  `stories/README.md`, and `scripts/check_story_states.py` passes on them.
- Every story says what it waits for. Every feature story says it is S4 or S5 work filed four days
  before M5, and where it sits on the cut list (sprint plan §10). None states an unmeasured
  number or tolerance (D-003), and the heatmap leaves everything that isn't Observed grey (D-005).
- D-062 records how the heatmap's data reaches the viewer. The titles-only list loses its capture
  line, and the manual's §3.4, §4 and §8.3 name the story that builds each part.

## Scope

The seven stories, by topic. They are the content of the first criterion, not separate criteria.

- **C-14** Heatmap rendering in the viewer, built against a canned sample in the mock, in D-062's
  format.
- **C-15** Report page: the verdict, its reasons in plain language, the regions, the heatmap, and
  printing to PDF. Built against the contract's verdict examples.
- **R-34** Order store: an order with its CAD and spec, and sessions checking the order code
  against it. A D-031 contract change.
- **C-16** Buyer setup page: the CAD, the tolerance table, the placement note and the capture
  link.
- **C-17** Board PDF with the order code: the printed code, a QR code and an orientation arrow,
  with the board itself unchanged.
- **C-18** Device testing: the capture path on one iPhone and one Android phone, keyboard access,
  and the error messages.
- **C-21** Hosting the capture app: a build in CI, GitHub Pages from `main`, and O-005 answered
  before a public page points at the paid service.
