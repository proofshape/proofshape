## Why this exists

The sprint plan's S4 "Inspection" sprint starts 2026-10-19 and ends on Oct 30 with two gates,
I3 ("verdict proven end to end") and M4 ("planted 4 mm hole shift → Fail; unobserved region →
Rescan; substituted part caught on scale or shape"). Both need registration. The inspection
lane is ten titles in `stories/README.md`'s "Phase 3 and beyond" and nothing more. The first
five don't wait on ground truth: the sprint plan built the synthetic mesh generator to free the
inspection lane from sprint one (§4 item 3).

## Acceptance criteria

- The five stories listed under *Scope* below have story files and rows in a new "Inspection"
  section of `stories/README.md`, and `scripts/check_story_states.py` passes on them.
- Every story cites the settled method (proposal §7.1, D-008), keeps every threshold a declared
  parameter, and carries no tolerance, `k` or σ_floor (D-003, issue #157).
- The titles-only list keeps I-06..I-10 (they wait on G-04's σ_floor and the D-032 tolerance
  schema) and adds I-11 OBJ loading.

## Scope

The five stories, by topic. They are the content of the first criterion, not separate criteria:
each is its own story with its own acceptance criteria.

- **I-01** Synthetic mesh generator, and the `inspection` package laid out as D-053 records.
- **I-02** CAD loading: ASCII and binary STL, and GLB, coordinates as stored, no new dependency.
- **I-03** Global registration: FPFH + RANSAC candidate poses, scale fixed to 1, and the
  free-scale residual of proposal §7.1, in the sign convention R-05's code defines for its
  `cad_registration_residual` input (`recon/scale_agreement.py`, D-039).
- **I-04** Robust point-to-plane ICP with a Tukey loss.
- **I-05** Orientation ambiguity: refine every candidate, flag a runner-up within 10% (D-008).
