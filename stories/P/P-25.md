## Why this exists

The sprint plan's S4 "Inspection" sprint starts 2026-10-19, and its M4 gate (Oct 30, "verdict
proven end to end") needs registration. The inspection lane is ten titles in `stories/README.md`'s
"Phase 3 and beyond" and nothing more. The first five don't wait on ground truth: the sprint plan
built the synthetic mesh generator to free the inspection lane from sprint one (§4 item 3).

## Acceptance criteria

- These five stories have story files and rows in a new "Inspection" section of
  `stories/README.md`, and `scripts/check_story_states.py` passes on them:
  - **I-01** Synthetic mesh generator, and the `inspection` package (the import name D-030 left
    to I-01: directory `inspect/`, importable package `inspection` at `inspect/inspection/`).
  - **I-02** CAD loading: ASCII and binary STL, and GLB, coordinates as stored, no new dependency.
  - **I-03** Global registration: FPFH + RANSAC candidate poses, scale fixed to 1, free-scale
    residual in R-05's convention.
  - **I-04** Robust point-to-plane ICP with a Tukey loss.
  - **I-05** Orientation ambiguity: refine every candidate, flag a runner-up within 10% (D-008).
- Every story cites the settled method (proposal §7.1, D-008), keeps every threshold a declared
  parameter, and carries no tolerance, `k` or σ_floor (D-003, issue #157).
- The titles-only list keeps I-06..I-10 (they wait on G-04's σ_floor and the D-032 tolerance
  schema) and adds I-11 OBJ loading.
