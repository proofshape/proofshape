## Why this exists

σ_floor is the next step on the sprint plan's critical path ("provenance and σ → σ_floor
measured → verdict logic → accuracy runs → freeze", `docs/sprint-plan.md` §3), and provenance
and σ are done (R-09, R-10). The ground-truth lane is still five titles in `stories/README.md`'s
"Phase 3 and beyond", and M3 ("σ_floor is a measured number", 2026-10-16) passed with nothing
recorded. R-16 is Blocked on G-03 and G-04. R-22 and R-23 compare against CAD sizes nobody has
checked against the actual prints.

## Acceptance criteria

- These nine stories have story files and rows in a new "Ground truth" section of
  `stories/README.md`, and `scripts/check_story_states.py` passes on them:
  - **G-01** Book SFBU lab printer time and characterise the printer with a calibration cube.
  - **G-02** Specimen batch: 5–8 prisms the size check (`recon/cad_size_check.py`) can measure.
  - **G-03** Caliper every specimen.
  - **G-04** Error and σ_floor for the board reference, measured against caliper truth.
  - **G-05** Defect ladder at 2 / 4 / 8 mm: shifted hole, missing and excess material.
  - **G-06** Caliper the golden-set parts (s-04, s-05) and the printed board sheet.
  - **G-07** Reliability curve: does per-vertex σ predict measured error; it sets `k`. First
    drafted as G-06 on the unmerged `chore/proposal-revisions-2026-09-05` branch.
  - **G-08** Photograph the specimen batch as a fixture set, the way F-06 stored the golden set.
  - **G-09** Photograph the defect ladder into the same fixture set.

  G-01..G-05 keep the topics of the titles-only list. G-06..G-09 are new, from the design
  below.
- Every G story writes down its method before measuring and cites the rules it measures under:
  no metrology claims and no unmeasured tolerance (D-003), and the measured print is truth, not
  the CAD (`docs/archive/team-proposal-v2.md`).
- `fixtures/README.md` defines the caliper-reading file the G stories write.
- The titles-only list keeps the ground-truth work not written out yet: G-10 σ_floor for the sheet
  and card references, and G-11 the robustness set.
