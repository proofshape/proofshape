## Why this exists

A claim lived only in the story file, so the index said *that* someone had a story, not
*who*; and an assistant told to claim a story had no rule about whose name to write.

## Acceptance criteria

- `stories/README.md` has an Owner column, and a claim puts the same handle in the story file
  and the index, in one commit (D-037).
- `scripts/check_story_states.py` reports mismatched, missing and malformed owners, and any
  AI assistant named as an owner.
- The rule is written into `AGENTS.md`, `AGENTS_START_HERE.md`, `README.md`, the story
  template and the PR template.

## Tests

The owner checks are code, with tests in `tests/test_check_story_states.py` added in the same
pull request. The written rules are a written check, reviewed in #32 by @dalwalyk.
