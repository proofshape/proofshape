## Why this exists

The expo is on Saturday, Dec 5, from 10:00 AM to 3:00 PM at SFBU in Fremont, the day after M7's
code freeze. It needs a printed poster. P-39's notes flagged that the expo might need material
of its own, such as a poster or a handout. They also left open whether that work went in P-39
or in a story of its own, and nothing recorded it. The team was then given an SFBU capstone
poster to follow for style and layout, so the poster gets its own story here and stays off the
final presentation's hours.

## Acceptance criteria

- **A poster committed under `submissions/`, with the commands that built it.** The poster
  follows the SFBU template the team was given:
  - colours and layout: a beige header with the logo and mascot, a gold band, a navy body, and
    four columns of alternating tan and grey panels;
  - sections: Introduction, Methodology, Analysis & Results, Generative AI, Summary, References
    and Acknowledgement;
  - one 3D figure built from real pipeline output, not an illustration.
- **Every item under "Information to collect" below is answered** and recorded in this file, and
  the poster is rebuilt with it.
- **Every figure on the poster matches the final report (P-38) or a recorded result** at the
  freeze (`AGENTS.md`, "No promised numbers").
  - The results table, the generative-AI section and the stated limit are updated to what's on
    `main` then.
  - Each cut or limit is stated the same way as in the report and the deck (P-39).
- **Rendered and checked before printing,** page by page at full size. The final look is in
  PowerPoint, because LibreOffice's layout is close but not identical.
- **The file that went to print is the committed one.** The completion record gives the print
  size and where it was printed.

## Tests

Written-check exception (D-052): the output is a poster, which can't be unit-tested. The check is
a reviewer reading the rendered poster against the acceptance criteria above. Its generator,
`scripts/build_expo_poster.js`, is smoke-tested with the other deck builders in
`tests/test_generator_scripts.py`.
