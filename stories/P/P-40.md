## Why this exists

The expo is most likely on Fri Dec 5, the day after M7's code freeze (O-006, unconfirmed). It
needs a printed poster. P-39's notes flagged that the expo might need material of its own, such
as a poster or a handout. They also left open whether that work went in P-39 or in a story of
its own, and nothing recorded it. The team was then given an SFBU capstone poster to follow for
style and layout, so the poster gets its own story here and stays off the final presentation's
hours.

## Acceptance criteria

- **A poster committed under `submissions/`, with the commands that built it.** The poster
  follows the SFBU template the team was given:
  - colours and layout: a beige header with the logo and mascot, a gold band, a navy body, and
    four columns of alternating tan and grey panels;
  - sections: Introduction, Methodology, Analysis & Results, Generative AI, Summary, References
    and Acknowledgement;
  - one 3D figure built from real pipeline output, not an illustration.
- **Every item under "Information to collect" below is answered** and recorded in this file, and
  the poster is rebuilt with it.
- **Every figure on the poster matches the final report (P-38) or a recorded result** at the
  freeze (`AGENTS.md`, "No promised numbers").
  - The results table, the generative-AI section and the stated limit are updated to what's on
    `main` then.
  - Each cut or limit is stated the same way as in the report and the deck (P-39).
- **Rendered and checked before printing,** page by page at full size. The final look is in
  PowerPoint, because LibreOffice's layout is close but not identical.
- **The file that went to print is the committed one.** The completion record gives the print
  size and where it was printed.

## Tests

Written-check exception (D-052): the output is a poster, which can't be unit-tested. The check is
a reviewer reading the rendered poster against the acceptance criteria above. Its generator,
`scripts/build_expo_poster.js`, is smoke-tested with the other deck builders in
`tests/test_generator_scripts.py`.

## Notes and decisions

- **The draft so far** (2026-11-20, in this story's PR) has every section filled in:
  - **Layout:** 48 × 27 in, in the template's colours and four-column layout.
  - **The 3D figure** comes from golden part s-04's real session, `sess_f82401bb6852`:
    - 29 of the 30 iPhone photos, drawn as camera positions solved from the printed board;
    - a magnified close-up of the reconstructed part, coloured by its signed distance from the
      CAD;
    - the capture app's own heatmap colours: blue for missing material, red for extra, slate for
      never seen.
  - **Results:** each number on the poster has its source noted in the generator's `RESULTS`
    table.
- **To rebuild the figures:**

  ```
  OMP_NUM_THREADS=1 PYTHONPATH=. .venv/bin/python scripts/render_expo_figures.py \
      <proofshape-data>/i13-review/s-04/sessions/sess_f82401bb6852 submissions/assets/expo
  ```

  The session is local data, not in the repository. The figures it writes are committed, so the
  poster rebuilds without it.
- **To rebuild the poster:** `cd scripts && npm install && node build_expo_poster.js`. This writes
  `submissions/expo-poster.pptx`. For the PDF, render it with LibreOffice (`soffice --headless
  --convert-to pdf`).
- **Deck builds:** write colours as hex in the generator, as P-39 notes. Open3D's offscreen
  renderer doesn't run on the Mac ("EGL Headless is not supported"), so the 3D figures use
  matplotlib.
- **Estimate basis:** 1 h, as the team set it, for collecting the information above, applying it
  and sending the poster to print. The draft itself was built before this story was written, in
  the same session. Its time goes in the completion record as actual hours, not the estimate.
