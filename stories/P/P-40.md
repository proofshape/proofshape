## Why this exists

The expo is on Saturday, Dec 5, from 10:00 AM to 3:00 PM at SFBU in Fremont, the day after M7's
code freeze. It needs a printed poster. P-39's notes flagged that the expo might need material
of its own, such as a poster or a handout. They also left open whether that work went in P-39
or in a story of its own, and nothing recorded it. The team was then given an SFBU capstone
poster to follow for style and layout, so the poster gets its own story here and stays off the
final presentation's hours.

## Acceptance criteria

- **A poster committed under `submissions/`, with the commands that built it.** The poster
  follows the SFBU template the team was given:
  - colours and layout: a beige header with the logo and mascot, a gold band, a navy body, and
    four columns of alternating tan and grey panels;
  - sections: Introduction, Methodology, Analysis & Results, Generative AI, Summary, References
    and Acknowledgement;
  - one 3D figure built from real pipeline output, not an illustration.
- **Every item under "Information to collect" below is answered** and recorded in this file, and
  the poster is rebuilt with it.
- **Every figure on the poster matches the final report (P-38) or a recorded result** when the
  poster goes to print (`AGENTS.md`, "No promised numbers"). The print file is due 2026-11-23,
  before M7's freeze (Dec 3–4), so print time is the poster's own freeze. Anything that lands
  after it isn't on the poster.
  - The results, the generative-AI section and the stated limit are updated to what's on `main`
    then.
  - Each cut or limit is stated the same way as in the report and the deck (P-39).
- **Rendered and checked before printing,** page by page at full size. The final look is in
  PowerPoint, because LibreOffice's layout is close but not identical.
- **The file that went to print is the committed one,** rebuilt with the members' student IDs
  added and nothing else different. The IDs come from an untracked file (`--ids`) and never go
  into git, because the repository is public (D-013). The completion record gives the print size
  and where it was printed.

## Tests

Written-check exception (D-052): the output is a poster, which can't be unit-tested. The check is
a reviewer reading the rendered poster against the acceptance criteria above. The code around it
is tested:
- `tests/test_generator_scripts.py` builds the poster with the other deck builders, checks that
  only an `--ids` build carries student IDs (with made-up IDs), and that an IDs file missing a
  member is refused;
- `tests/test_render_expo_figures.py` checks what the figure renderer counts as Observed and
  measured, that a mesh without provenance colours or deviation data is refused, and that the
  coverage figure is the service's own area share.

## Notes and decisions

- **The first draft** and its two generators are in PR #425. They were written before this
  claim, which is why both reviews there (@dalwalyk and @mbj1994, 2026-11-20) asked for the claim
  to land on `main` on its own first (D-037). Those reviews list what the revision has to change.
  The revision continues in #425 once this claim merges.
- **The revision (2026-11-22)** follows both reviews. It keeps the template's header, colours,
  grid and centre figure, and changes what's inside them:
  - **one job per section:** the problem and the question; how it works; four verdicts worded
    from I-07's table, I-09's intake check and A-02's veto; four methodology cards;
  - **results as four cards,** each scoped to what was measured. The printer, wall-artifact and
    backbone-timing rows are dropped: the timings came from different hardware, and P-41 shows
    wall error isn't solved;
  - **a status badge on each AI feature** as of 2026-11-22;
  - **the hero re-rendered** from the same session: the board fills the frame, and the cameras are
    lighter. It has three labels, and the legend uses the viewer's own words ("Grey = Unobserved
    or not measured. Colours do not mean pass or fail."). ±4 mm is labelled the colour range, not
    a tolerance;
  - **coverage is the service's area share,** "76% of its surface area observed", from the
    session's `uncertainty.json`. The 84% in the first draft's figure facts was a vertex count.
- **To rebuild the figures,** from the session's local data (not in the repository; the figures
  it writes are committed, so the poster rebuilds without it):

  ```
  OMP_NUM_THREADS=1 PYTHONPATH=. .venv/bin/python scripts/render_expo_figures.py \
      <proofshape-data>/i13-review/s-04/sessions/sess_f82401bb6852 submissions/assets/expo
  ```
- **To rebuild the poster:** `cd scripts && npm install && node build_expo_poster.js` writes
  `submissions/expo-poster.pptx`. For the PDF, render it with LibreOffice (`soffice --headless
  --convert-to pdf`).
- **The print copy:** `node build_expo_poster.js <out>.pptx --ids
  <proofshape-data>/expo/student-ids.json`, with both paths outside git. The IDs file maps each
  member's name, as written on the poster, to their ID.
- **Work in progress at the claim (D-064): over the cap, on purpose.** I-15 and R-44 are active.
  G-12 is waiting on a lab slot (since 2026-11-21), and R-36 on #371's review (since 2026-11-17).
