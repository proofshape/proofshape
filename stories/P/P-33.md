## Why this exists

#305 split the Android device check out of C-18 into C-23, which is Ready but has no phone lined
up. The team has decided to park it, with a view to coming back to it before the expo if time
allows. That departs from two written commitments:
- **The proposal (§12):** "run on one iOS and one Android phone at every milestone. Tier 1 must
  pass on both."
- **The sprint plan:** S6's "test on one iPhone and one Android", and I4's "whole system on both
  phones".

The sprint plan also says cuts and carries are recorded at the boundary rather than re-decided
each time (working agreement 4, §10). Unrecorded, two things go wrong:
- **X-04 (I4) can never start.** It depends on C-23 and requires an Android phone, and the
  recorded backup demo (X-05) depends on X-04.
- **The manual misleads.** It still calls Android "Planned".

The expo date isn't recorded anywhere either, so "before the expo" has nothing to check against.

## Acceptance criteria

- **D-065 records the parking:**
  - what is parked (C-23) and what is unchanged (C-18, iPhone);
  - the revisit point: before the expo, if time allows, checked at the Dec 2 boundary at the
    latest, with any fix landing before M7's code freeze on Dec 4;
  - the trigger: an Android phone available, plus the hours to run it;
  - the cost, stated plainly: the proposal's §12 rule is unmet while it stands.
- **O-006 records the expo date** as the team's working assumption, "most likely Fri
  2026-12-05", unconfirmed, to be updated when it's known.
- **The stories and documents match it:**
  - X-04 no longer depends on C-23 and requires only an iPhone, adding Android if C-23 has run;
  - C-23 carries a dated parked note and stays `Ready` and unclaimed;
  - manual §2 says Android is untested and parked, so suppliers use an iPhone in Safari;
  - the sprint plan's S6 line and the capture section's C-23 note point at D-065.

  `check_story_states.py` passes.
