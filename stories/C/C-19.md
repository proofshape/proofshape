## Why this exists

When a model has no provenance colours, C-12's viewer skips the legend, renders one flat colour
and just says "3D model loaded". Nothing tells the user that observed and unobserved surface can't
be told apart in this model, which is the one thing C-12 exists to show (D-005) (#192).

The mock service takes that path on every normal session, because its default model has no
colours. It has also drifted from `recon/service.py` in other ways (#193):

- it finishes a session after one frame, where the service needs three;
- it serves the model for any session, in any state;
- it answers an unknown session with a 500 instead of the contract's `session_not_found`.

## Acceptance criteria

- A model with no provenance colours loads with a visible warning that observed and unobserved
  surface aren't shown.
- The legend's appearance and the colour remap are tested against a real GLB, not only through
  the constants drift test.
- The mock's frame minimum, model route and unknown-session responses match the service.

## Tests

- the no-provenance warning appears;
- the legend appears when provenance is present (today's tests only assert its absence);
- a real GLB goes through `GLTFLoader` into the remap: the mock's provenance fixture, or a file
  written by `recon.mesh_cleanup.write_glb`;
- `api.test.ts` expects `too_few_frames` below three frames, a 404 for a model that isn't ready,
  and `session_not_found` for an unknown session.
