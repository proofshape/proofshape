## Why this exists

Three small defects in how the capture app reads the phone:

- **Heading wrap (#189).** The duplicate check subtracts headings directly, so 359.5° and 0.5°
  come out 359° apart and a duplicate frame is accepted.
- **Roll ignored (#190).** Orbit coverage takes elevation from beta alone and heading from alpha
  alone, ignoring roll. A 10° roll moves the true heading by 14–27°, against a 15° tolerance. In
  landscape the straight-down slot is marked covered while the camera is level.
- **Gate order (#191).** Blur is checked before exposure. An all-black or all-white frame is
  reported as blurry, and the user is told to hold steadier for what is a lighting problem.

## Acceptance criteria

- Heading differences in the duplicate check wrap at 360°, using the existing
  `angularDistanceDeg`.
- Elevation and heading come from the full device rotation, with the camera looking along
  R(α, β, γ) · (0, 0, −1).
- Exposure is checked before blur.

## Tests

- a 359°/1° pair in the duplicate check;
- gyro readings with non-zero roll in portrait, and held upright in landscape, give the expected
  elevation and heading;
- an all-black frame reports `frame_rejected_exposure`.

These are unit tests on synthetic readings. One part can't be automated, because it depends on the
real sensor's conventions: on a real phone, the arrow and coverage behave with some roll and in
landscape. Record that device check with the device model and iOS and Safari versions; C-07's
check didn't record them precisely.
