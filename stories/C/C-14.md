## Why this exists

The heatmap is how a Fail shows where the part is out of spec: "signed point-to-mesh distance from
each Observed vertex to the CAD, rendered as vertex colors in the viewer" (proposal §7.2). Today
the viewer can only colour by provenance (C-12). The hard rule also governs this screen: "how the
heatmap is coloured" is named in it (AGENTS.md, D-005), so surface nobody saw must never look
measured.

## Acceptance criteria

- **A heatmap mode, only when there is data.**
  - When the loaded GLB carries D-062's `_DEVIATION_MM` with at least one measured Observed
    vertex, the viewer offers "Colour by: what was seen / deviation from CAD".
  - Without it, there is no control, and C-12 and C-19's behaviour is unchanged.
  - Switching back restores the provenance colours exactly. C-12's remap writes the colour buffer
    in place, so keep a copy.
- **Only measured, Observed surface is coloured.**
  - A vertex gets the deviation scale only if COLOR_0 says Observed **and**
    `_DEVIATION_MEASURED` is 1 (D-062's two gates). Every other vertex is drawn the Unobserved
    grey (glossary: Unobserved "stay[s] grey rather than neutral").
  - The scale is diverging: positive is extra material, negative is missing. Its zero colour is not
    the Unobserved grey.
  - Its two ends differ in lightness as well as hue (C-12's colour-blind rule).
- **The legend is in mm, and never claims pass or fail.**
  - Given the order's tolerance, the scale saturates at ±tolerance and the legend says so.
  - Without one, it saturates at ±the largest measured |deviation|, labelled "scale: largest
    measured deviation, not a tolerance".
  - No colour is labelled pass, fail, or in or out of tolerance; that is the verdict's job (D-003).
- **A canned sample in the mock** (sprint plan §5, D4). The stub serves a GLB in D-062's format,
  and `?viewer-demo=c14` mounts the viewer on it, as `?viewer-demo=c12` does today.
  - A committed script generates it, using `recon/mesh_cleanup.write_glb` extended to write extra
    vertex attributes. I-06 will call the same writer, so D-062's format is one piece of code on
    both sides.

## Tests

- Unit tests, on pure functions:
  - deviation to colour at zero, at both ends and beyond them;
  - the two gates: an Unobserved vertex with a value, and an Observed vertex with
    `_DEVIATION_MEASURED` 0, both stay grey;
  - the choice of range;
  - the legend text.
- A real `GLTFLoader.parse` of the committed fixture, as C-19 did for the provenance fixture.
- Toggling twice restores the original colour bytes.
- Python:
  - `write_glb`'s new attributes are named with a leading underscore, have one value per vertex,
    and carry no NaN;
  - the committed fixture is byte-identical to the script's output.
- Whether the scale reads clearly on a phone isn't testable here. It's one line in C-18 or X-04.
