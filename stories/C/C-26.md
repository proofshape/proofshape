## Why this exists

The capture page has no stylesheet. C-07's guidance arrow is a text glyph (`&#8593;`) at body
font size. It sits in `#shell-gyro-overlay`, below the video and the status lines
(`capture/src/shell.ts`, `APP_SHELL_HTML`), so on an iPhone it is tiny and often scrolled off the
bottom of the screen. "Capture frame" is a default-sized button. Nothing on screen says whether
the phone is at the next target until a frame has been taken. The sprint plan's S6 capture work
includes "UI bugs"; this is one, found by @TabeenRaoof while preparing a class demo.

## Acceptance criteria

- **The arrow is visible and in view.** It is drawn over the camera preview, at least 72 px
  across, in one calm colour (not black, not animated beyond its rotation). Its direction comes
  from `computeArrowGuidance`, unchanged. The preview is never cropped, so the photo is what the
  preview shows.
- **The preview's frame says where the phone is:**
  - **green** when the live reading is on the next target. This is `computeArrowGuidance`'s own
    `onTarget`, within the 15° coverage tolerance, so green means "this photo counts toward
    coverage";
  - **amber** within twice that tolerance;
  - **red** beyond it;
  - neutral before the first frame (there is no target yet);
  - green once the orbit is complete.
- **The shutter is a large round button** (at least 72 px) at the bottom centre of the preview.
  It keeps its id, its accessible name "Capture frame", its disabled rules, and a visible
  keyboard focus ring.
- **The end of the orbit can't be missed.** Once every slot is covered, a green banner in the
  middle of the preview says "Orbit complete" and points to Finish, with white text at 4.5:1 or
  better. The small status chip steps aside while it shows. Finish session is a large,
  full-width button (at least 56 px tall) that keeps its id, its disabled rules and a focus ring.
  Added 2026-11-20 at @TabeenRaoof's request after the first phone run.

## Tests

In `capture/src/`, written with the code:
- `orbit.test.ts`: the level function at its 15° and 30° boundaries, and across the ±180°
  azimuth wrap.
- `overlay.test.ts`: the reported level follows dispatched orientation events, through
  `complete`. The completion banner stays hidden until the last slot is covered, including
  when the orbit completes with no live reading.
- `shell.test.ts`: the viewfinder's `data-guidance` follows the overlay. The shutter keeps its id
  and accessible name. Finish session carries the large button style and keeps its id and name.
- `pages-build.test.ts`: the built page carries the stylesheet.

A unit test can't see size, colour or scrolling on a phone. That part is a written device check on
the iPhone, in portrait and in landscape, recorded in the notes. Landscape is where the old arrow
went off screen, so it has to be checked there (@mbj1994's review of #419).

## Implementation notes

### 2026-11-19: implementation (@TabeenRaoof, with Claude)

- **What changed:**
  - `capture/src/capture.css` is new, linked from `index.html` the way `buyer.html` links
    `buyer.css`.
  - `shell.ts` wraps the preview, `#shell-gyro-overlay` and the shutter in `#shell-viewfinder`.
    Every existing id is kept. The viewfinder scrolls into view when the camera starts.
  - `overlay.ts` draws the arrow as an SVG, with the same `rotate()` transform and hidden-on-target
    rule. It gains an `onGuidance` option reporting `idle`, `far`, `near`, `on-target` or
    `complete`.
  - `orbit.ts` gains `guidanceLevel`, with `NEAR_TOLERANCE_MULTIPLIER = 2`.
- **Colours:**

  | Use | Colour |
  |---|---|
  | Arrow | teal-blue `#1d6f8a` with a white edge |
  | Frame, far | red `#d64545` |
  | Frame, near | amber `#e0a526` |
  | Frame, on target | green `#2e9d5b` |
  | Frame, idle | grey `#9aa3ab` |

- **The preview uses `object-fit: contain` and `max-height: 72svh`,** so it is never cropped and
  the whole viewfinder fits on an iPhone screen.
- **Tests:** guidance level boundaries (15°, 30°, the wrap), overlay levels, the viewfinder's
  `data-guidance` through a real session against the contract stub, the shutter's name, and the
  built page's stylesheet. The completion banner stays hidden until the last slot is covered,
  and Finish session carries the large button style. On this branch the capture suite has 348
  passing tests across 25 files. tsc, eslint and prettier are clean.
- **Mutations, each applied and reverted, each failing at least one test:**
  - near `<=` changed to `<`;
  - the overlay reporting `near` in place of the computed level;
  - the overlay reporting `near` in place of `on-target`;
  - the shell ignoring the level;
  - the completion banner never shown (2026-11-20; both banner tests fail).
- **Device check:** 2026-11-20, @TabeenRaoof's first iPhone run (with C-27): the arrow, frame
  colours and shutter were as wanted. The banner and the larger Finish button were added after
  it and haven't been on a phone yet. Still to do: portrait and landscape on the final head,
  with @dalwalyk's run asked for on #420.
